The Research Collaboration & Task Management system offers a comprehensive set of features within the **CARDS** module. It enables effective team-based research coordination, including task routing based on contributor skills and workloads, real-time presence tracking without centralized servers, structured collaboration on research tasks and notes, AI-assisted brainstorming, and community research hub insights.

## Task Inbox & Routing

This system automates distribution of research tasks by analyzing coverage gaps and aligns them with contributor capabilities for efficient division of labor.

### Implementation Details

- **Routing Logic**: The routing pipeline is triggered by the function `routePersistedTopicTasks` in `state/routedTaskQueues.ts`. It uses the topic’s `TopicCoverageReport` to identify under-covered arguments and assigns them to eligible contributors with the fewest ongoing tasks, factoring skill levels (`SkillLevel`) and max concurrency limits. This routing is done in `buildAndPersistRoutingResult`[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/routedTaskQueues.ts138-143](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/routedTaskQueues.ts#L138-L143) and [docs/features/task-inbox.md71-115](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/task-inbox.md?plain=1#L71-L115)
- **Persistence**: Routed task queues are stored in `localStorage` under the key `routedTaskQueues` with functions like `listRoutedTaskQueues()` and mutation helpers in the same module [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/routedTaskQueues.ts20-110](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/routedTaskQueues.ts#L20-L110)
- **Task Verification Flow**: Marking a task as done moves it into a `PendingTaskVerification` state to require validation by a different contributor. Verification is handled by `verifyAndRecordResearchTask` in `state/researchProgress.ts`, gated by `assertVerifierAllowed` which prevents self-verification [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/TaskInboxPanel.tsx90-130](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/TaskInboxPanel.tsx#L90-L130)[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/pendingTaskVerifications.ts1-40](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/pendingTaskVerifications.ts#L1-L40)[docs/features/task-inbox.md60-110](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/task-inbox.md?plain=1#L60-L110)
- **UI & Identity Integration**: The `TaskInboxPanel` React component renders tasks grouped by topic, providing controls for marking done, reassigning, filtering tasks by contributor, and setting priority flags. It accepts an optional prop `signedInContributorId` derived from the better-auth session to prefill the "My tasks" filter and lock verification controls [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/TaskInboxPanel.tsx1-134](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/TaskInboxPanel.tsx#L1-L134)[apps/debate-ai.com/components/research/TaskInboxWithIdentity.tsx1-15](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/apps/debate-ai.com/components/research/TaskInboxWithIdentity.tsx#L1-L15)

## Prep Room & Topic Presence

The Prep Room offers a collaborative space for a specific debate topic, combining checklist management, routed tasks, evidence search, and live presence signaling without dedicated real-time infrastructure.

### Topic Presence System

- **Heartbeat-Based Presence**: Presence is implemented as timestamped `PresenceHeartbeat` records per (topic, contributor) pair, stored in `localStorage` under `topicPresenceHeartbeats`[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/topicPresence.ts1-20](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/topicPresence.ts#L1-L20)
- **Heartbeat API**: The function `recordPersistedPresenceHeartbeat(topic, contributorId, timestamp)` adds or updates heartbeats, while `listPersistedActiveContributors(topic, now)` queries contributors whose last heartbeat timestamps fall within a five-minute freshness window (default). This avoids the complexity of WebSocket connections. The roster updates every 30 seconds client-side [docs/features/team-collaboration-mode.md58-100](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/team-collaboration-mode.md?plain=1#L58-L100)[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/PrepRoomPanel.tsx120-143](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/PrepRoomPanel.tsx#L120-L143)

### Prep Room State & UI

- The `PrepRoomPanel` React component renders a topic switcher (all topics with checklists or submitted evidence), a keyword search filtered to the selected topic, routed research tasks for that topic, and an activity timeline of evidence and draft submissions [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/PrepRoomPanel.tsx20-160](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/PrepRoomPanel.tsx#L20-L160)
- Presence integration allows contributors to signal "I'm active here" with their contributor ID, which updates their heartbeat and refreshes the active roster [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/PrepRoomPanel.tsx140-150](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/PrepRoomPanel.tsx#L140-L150)
- Identity wiring is handled by `PrepRoomWithIdentity` which pre-fills the "Your ID" input from a signed-in session's identity [apps/debate-ai.com/components/research/PrepRoomWithIdentity.tsx1-15](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/apps/debate-ai.com/components/research/PrepRoomWithIdentity.tsx#L1-L15)

## Sprint Notes & Topic Sprints

Topic sprints unify multiple research signals — quests, task routing, progress tracking, and collaborative notes — scoped per topic.

### Sprint Notes Model

- `SprintNote` objects record live prep notes linked to a topic, with fields: `id`, `topic`, `authorId`, `text`, `status` (one of `"open"`, `"covered"`, `"needs-follow-up"`), optional `assignedToId`, creation and update timestamps [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/lib/team-collaboration-mode.ts49-70](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/lib/team-collaboration-mode.ts#L49-L70)
- The lifecycle of a note's status cycles through the three states by `nextSprintNoteStatus`, with pure functions `updateSprintNoteStatus` and `assignSprintNote` returning updated copies [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/lib/team-collaboration-mode.ts75-117](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/lib/team-collaboration-mode.ts#L75-L117)
- Notes can be grouped and sorted by topic or assigned teammate. The `buildSprintNotesPanelView` helper aggregates notes for UI panels grouped by topic [docs/features/team-collaboration-mode.md31-56](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/team-collaboration-mode.md?plain=1#L31-L56)

### Topic Sprint Composition

- The `buildTopicSprint` function composes a complete sprint view from six inputs:

1. **Quest Templates** (`QuestTemplate[]` from `state/dailyQuests.ts`)
2. **Quest Contributions** (`QuestContribution[]` from `state/contributions.ts`)
3. **Coverage Report** (`TopicCoverageReport` from `state/trackedArguments.ts`)
4. **Contributor Availability** (`ContributorAvailability[]` from `state/contributorAvailability.ts`)
5. **Assignments** (active and completed tasks for the topic)
6. **Notes** (topic scoped `SprintNote[]`)

These inputs are usually read from persisted stores to build a unified `TopicSprint` object [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/lib/team-collaboration-mode.ts160-180](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/lib/team-collaboration-mode.ts#L160-L180)[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/topicSprints.ts1-40](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/topicSprints.ts#L1-L40)
- The `TopicSprintPanel` React component renders the sprint UI comprising:

- A quest board showing open quests and progress
- Task routing controls and assignment lists
- A progress board for contributor completion status
- The shared wall of sprint notes with add/edit/assign actions
- Whiteboard notes providing a freeform brainstorming surface
- Whiteboard notes are persisted separately in `sprintWhiteboardNotes` in localStorage, with position updates allowed for drag/drop reordering [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/sprintWhiteboard.ts15-65](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/state/sprintWhiteboard.ts#L15-L65)
- Sprints support scheduling sprint sessions via `useSprintSessionsSync` hook, which synchronizes session lists and calendar events with backend APIs `/api/sprint-sessions`[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/TopicSprintPanel.tsx130-200](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/TopicSprintPanel.tsx#L130-L200)[packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/hooks/useSprintSessionsSync.ts1-40](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/hooks/useSprintSessionsSync.ts#L1-L40)

## Brainstorming & AI Assistance

The brainstorming board facilitates idea generation and team input enhancement via AI for under-covered arguments.

### Feature Overview

- **Boards & Ideas**: Brainstorm boards group ideas by argument block and category (e.g., frontline answers, impact framing). Ideas have metadata including contributor ID, upvotes, and AI generation flag [docs/features/brainstorm-board.md4-30](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/brainstorm-board.md?plain=1#L4-L30)
- **AI Idea Generation**: "Generate AI ideas" triggers calls to `requestTeamBrainstormAiIdeas`, which builds prompts with `buildTeamBrainstormAiUserPrompt`, POSTs to `/api/reason-ai`, and parses responses [docs/features/brainstorm-board.md75-85](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/brainstorm-board.md?plain=1#L75-L85)
- **Upvote & Merge**: Users can upvote ideas (`upvotePersistedBrainstormIdea`) and merge near-duplicates using `mergePersistedBrainstormIdeas`, consolidating votes and removing duplicates [docs/features/brainstorm-board.md89-95](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/docs/features/brainstorm-board.md?plain=1#L89-L95)
- **Integration**: The `BrainstormBoardPanel` displays boards and ideas from `localStorage` (`brainstormIdeas`), reflects topic selection from the coverage dashboard, and supports near-real-time updates [packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/BrainstormBoardPanel.tsx1-100](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/blob/34937310/packages/debate-team-collaboration/src/panels/BrainstormBoardPanel.tsx#L1-L100)

## Topic Coverage & Community Research Hub

These components provide team and community-wide views on research progress and contributor activity.

### Topic Coverage Dashboard

# Summary

The Research Collaboration & Task Management sub-system orchestrates distributed research efforts expertly by combining:

- **Task routing and verification** to ensure appropriate contributors receive and complete research assignments.
- **Prep Room and presence tracking** to foster synchronous collaboration without complex infrastructure.
- **Topic sprints and notes management** to unify ongoing quests, research assignments, and team note-taking.
- **Brainstorming boards with AI assistance** to fill coverage gaps with creative team and AI-generated ideas.
- **Community-wide research hub** providing activity views and contributor rankings to energize and incentivize participation.

This system achieves real-time collaboration and task management using persistent localStorage along with explicit session heartbeats and cross-tab synchronization strategies, implemented primarily in the `debate-team-collaboration` package.
