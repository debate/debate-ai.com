18. ### **Role Models, Achievement, and What Actually Changes Minds**

    1. Why does modern society assume we need the first of each demographic in each category? That reinforces that young women and minorities should search for a role-model with a matching appearance to inspire their path. Instead, we can teach students to draw inspiration from all historical leaders by their character in crisis. Global innovators of chess are a good example of why people would model based on strategy, not to pick those of similar ethnicity. Most of society which tries to avoid racial bias already judges minority leaders by character. But how do affirmative action and hiring for diversity help racists overcome seeing that group as less capable? They can entrench rooting for one's own team to have winners in mainstream competition as modern media politicizes minority leadership. Education groups students by race to compete for academic placement into a social position, but resentment builds with lawsuits over college acceptance and the social ladder. Those who would not be admitted otherwise often achieve what seems less impressive to peers in elite classrooms relative to one's hometown, reinforcing prejudice as less capable (Leslie, 2014). Even if all racism ends, prejudices can always form in groups based on interests even in those who think they are objective. We should examine how the intersections of identities create subconscious biases, instead of singling out one demographic as particularly incompatible with the debate format of timed speeches. The fact that debate can also be used to give speeches like an activist author writes means it is the very exchange of dialogue those authors would use to overcome systemic flaws. Activists come to find ways to take offense to call anyone in society racist, and assume that is enough to dismiss the concerns that person raised. Silencing our words by ignoring solutions research is like “spirit murder” insulting researchers who feel their time wasted with no common purpose. In 2026 and other years, the national final round involved such activism, and even those outside of debate can understand it via infographic videos for major rounds online (Debate AI, 2026). Antiracist activists have won at elite tournaments in the last decade showing that judges find them meaningful. It is for participants to decide for themselves what to pursue, but we must first learn to persuade on issues with speeches and research.

Reading and thinking of “white people” or a “white system” *gives up an entire neural cycle strengthening subconscious bonds via proteins shaping* *synaptic elasticity* to subconscious ontologies noticing *traits* *instead:* like “young,” “sports fans,” or “debate nerds.” Arora (2022) reports how group studies show that the subconscious labels some behaviour and academic interests to perpetuate a cultural stigma of “acting white” which spreads like toxic radiation to harm achievement. Like avoiding the n-word, activists could also try to avoid labeling people the r-word, as that applies to anyone, who could be that way subconsciously. Totalizing activism disempowers students to see themselves as less capable of elite success, given that society's subconscious racism will preclude engagement. Misadvertising debate as within an evil oppressive system is the worst way to boost minority interest. In the 1960s, the media popularized Norfolk Prison's 144–8 record beating elite schools at their own game (Haverty, 2016). Now under today's norms, those inmates would never have learned intricate persuasion on specifics and instead would demand the judge let them free as a rejection of the racist prison system excluding full participation. The media would have mocked calling that a prison riot, not a debate. Think of this as like two endings to the film, *The Great Debaters* (2007). Getting minorities interested to join by saying they can win just by raising awareness to reject racism to boost minority activist win rates over policy teams does not persuade policy teams to not see them as less capable. It only masks the hypocrisy of antiracist activists boycotting their own chance of a round to learn to persuade opposition on Africa aid or criminal reform or changing a culture's values. 

   3. Anti-racist authors would want their communities to have their followers be minority leaders to focus on their issues after learning persuasion via detailed research from debate, and to engage other groups in their concerns to help societal groups overcome overlooking the group's value and relevant perspective. Minority leaders should lead their own communities, teaching how to evaluate news media on key issues. We collectively learn how to compare on one topic to set a model for other topics. No one could outline a controversial view or do a historical debate, if it were enough to take offense to anything to claim the impact on one's personal spirit. We are all on the same side, like a law firm or an anti-racist Marxist activist association that has some lawyers “red-team” as opposition to help affirmatives learn to persuade society. Instead they want an easy win and to spread toxic hate misapplying authors to this context so that judges can feel like they look ethically superior, calling everyone else a racist to cajole minorities to reject learning issues. Like a frog slowly boiled without realizing it is in hot water, debate has incentivized diversity of thought by encouraging a chaos of ways to contrive scholars into relevance as if opposing common sense support for one's society. We are not pretending to roleplay just to satisfy a fetish for “fiating” fantasies; we are “keepin’ it real with you” when we say that we are already influencing our peers with quotes to support social changes. We can project departing to influence friends, online news groups, and society in all fields. One cannot argue that debate is bad without self-contradiction. Besides, if society were to reject all Western constructs and discourse, then the human mind would revert to the phallocentric tribalism we had in ancient Africa. Reverting to primal phallocentric prejudices means we’d no longer judge which leaders to support in an emergency via impact size – but something else entirely (*BBC Future*, 2020). As an alternative to divisive confrontation, we can exchange and discuss this paper and find common humor and common truths to envision a better way. Richard Pryor (2005), one of the “original gangsters” of Black comedy, explains how his social observations developed the intersubjective self-awareness that overcame division: “Black people got to look at themselves honestly, the same as white people did,” since they could laugh in the same room. Rain Pryor (2022), the prior Pryor’s daughter, furthers: “My father was able to use comedy as a vehicle for others to see their own defects and vulnerabilities, and laugh together instead of being divided.” Pryor became transformative partly because he made audiences confront racial differences together—not by pretending social hierarchies did not exist, but by making the contradictions, fear, pain, and absurdity recognizable enough that Black and white audience members could laugh while being together. Let’s end the contrived ethical supremacist toxicity and instead collaborate toward social coalescence on crowdsourced issue outlines to make “fiat” real.
