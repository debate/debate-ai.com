## Native Wrapper [#native-wrapper]

==A desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) that opens debate-ai.com in its own
window==. It works exactly like the website, except sign-in: press **Continue
in your browser**, sign in there, and you're handed back to the app.

- ==It starts with your computer and waits in the background==, so it opens
  instantly. Click its tray icon or press `Ctrl+`` (`Cmd+`` on a Mac) to show
  it; closing the window only hides it. Untick **Run on Startup** in the icon's
  menu to stop that.
- ==It updates itself in the background== and only installs while hidden.
- The camera and microphone work after the OS asks for permission once.
- The Android and iOS apps aren't ready yet.

## Video Library [#video-library]

==Debate rounds, lectures and top picks==, at **/videos**, with a page for each
format such as **/videos/pf**. Videos load as you scroll.

- **Addresses** start with the season, format and tournament
  (`/videos/21-22/cpd/ndt/finals/…`). Trim one to filter: `/videos/24-25/ld`
  shows that season's LD rounds.
- **Search** floats over the results (the magnifier top right), with filters,
  sort, and a grid or rows toggle. A compact search bar stays pinned as you
  scroll.
- **Live rounds** being broadcast show in their own row; see
  [Live Stream (Round Radio)](/docs/features/rounds#live-stream).
- **Favorites and hidden videos** follow your account (signed out, they stay
  in this browser).
- **WSD, Atlantic, Parliamentary, Congress and Speech** collections are cards
  above the search box on All Videos. **Atlantic** (the ACC Debate Tournament,
  `/videos/acc`) and **Parliamentary** (NPDA, NPTE, APDA, `/videos/parli`) also
  show on College Team Debate. Parliamentary and **Congress** (congressional
  debate, `/videos/congress`) are styles in the dropdowns next to WSD and
  Atlantic.
- Signed out, you can open ten videos before being asked to sign in.

### Lecture categories [#video-library-lecture-categories]

**Lectures** (`/lectures`) ==shows its categories as a bar of cards at the top
of the page==, each with its video count. Click one to switch to that
category.

The cards are grouped under four headings: **Getting started** (novice,
coaching, judging, research and speaking), **Strategy** (aff, neg,
counterplans, disads, T, critiques, impact calculus, round analysis),
**Topics & ideas** (topic lectures, philosophy, documentaries) and
**Formats** (Public Forum, WSD, NSDA Speech, demo debates). On All Videos the
Atlantic, Parliamentary, Congress and Speech collections sit in Formats too;
a collection with no videos yet is hidden.

==Every lecture from the National Speech & Debate Association's YouTube channel
is grouped under **NSDA Speech**== (`/lectures/nsda_speech`), whatever its
title says. NSDA *rounds* stay with their format (Policy, PF, LD).

### Stacked playlists [#video-library-stacked-playlists]

==Videos of the same debate share one card== with `<` / `>` arrows: a round and
its analysis, a round split into parts, or one upload per speech (played in
speaking order). Videos stack when they list the same teams, round and
tournament, or when one's YouTube description links the other. Turn it off
with **Stacked playlists** in the search panel.

==A round never gives away who won until you click Reveal decision==, which
rings the winner in gold.

### List layout [#video-library-list-layout]

==The rows view groups rounds by season, then tournament, newest first==, with
tournament logos and each season's topic (hover for the full resolution).
Click a row to open its watch page, or the popout icon to play it in the
corner while you keep browsing.

- **Team labels** show the school logo and debaters in a blue (Aff) or red
  (Neg) box. Ranked teams show last names and a **#N** badge. Hover any team
  for its stats card (rank, rating, win rates, badges and all-time records),
  or click it for the team's page.
- **View counts** change color and size by tier, up to a 👑 at 250k.
- A 🎖️ badge marks a top pick; hover it for the teams' all-time ranks.
- Each side's arguments get a category badge (K, CP, DA, T, Theory, FW) when
  the name shows it, and a **NEW** badge for new arguments.

### Suggesting a fix or adding a video [#video-library-suggesting-a-fix-to-a-video]

- ==Click the pencil on any watch page== to suggest corrections or request
  deletion. Moderators approve changes.
- The **+** button beside the search bar submits a YouTube link or channel for
  review. Upload your own round to YouTube first (unlisted works).

You need to be signed in for both.

### For admins [#video-library-fixing-a-video-admins]

The admin panel's **Video library** card edits any published video, with
**✨ Auto-fill with AI** to read details from the YouTube title and
description (the format, tournament, round, both teams and which side each
was on, written the way the library already names them) and **🏛️ Look up results** for college rounds. The **Incomplete
videos** tab lists videos missing details, and the **YouTube sync** tab
manages subscribed channels, transcripts, the Speech AI bulk runs, video
lengths and table exports.

## Topics & Statistics [#video-topic-statistics]

==Every season's debate resolutions, research areas and YouTube channel
stats== on one page, at **/practice/statistics**.

- **Debate Topics by Year:** pick a season to see its Policy, College, LD and
  PF resolutions and video stats. ==Click a resolution to sign up to debate it==
  ([Topic Sign-ups](/docs/features/team-collaboration#topic-sign-ups)); the
  search icon beside it searches its cards.
  The coming season holds a **topic poll**: rank up to five areas you'd like
  to debate (one ballot per season, sign-in needed).
- **Research Areas:** every resolution since 1927–28 sorted into 40 areas, with
  a trend line per area. The areas are unofficial categories.
- **Research Areas** resolutions also link to their sign-up.
- **700 Philosophical Resolutions:** ==value resolutions in 28 categories, with
  ids like Nuke.1 and Edu.69==, each with a Pro and a Con starting point.
  Search by any word or id, filter by category, and click one to sign up. The
  full list is also on [Value Topics](/docs/features/topics/value-topics).
- **YouTube Channel Statistics:** top channels and view charts.

## Video Watch Page [#video-watch-page]

==Every video has its own page==: the player, a synced transcript, the round's
details and related videos. Rounds are titled like `2026 NDT College Octas:
Kansas #14 OW (K) vs CSULB #7 MO (Policy)`, and hovering the season badge shows
that season's resolution. The back arrow at the start of the controls row,
before play/pause, returns to the video library.

### No spoilers [#video-watch-page-no-spoilers]

==The result stays hidden until you click Reveal decision==, which rings the
winner in gold and shows the ballots and judges. The YouTube description stays
hidden too, since it often gives the result away.

### By speech [#video-watch-page-by-speech]

==Each speech is a tab beside the player== (blue for Aff, red for Neg, grey for
cross-ex). ==Click one to play the video from that speech and start writing
its LLM summary==; the playing speech is outlined in red. Each tab opens on
**LLM summary**, then **Summary**, **Analysis**, **Live captions** and
**Transcript**, and a small live caption box sits above the tabs.

- A round whose speeches are timed (written up, marked or detected)
  ==starts at its first speech== instead of 0:00 when you open it from the
  beginning. A shared link with a time, or where you left off, still wins.
- **Skip breaks** (on by default) skips prep time between speeches.
- **Mark start** sets where a speech begins, or **Detect speeches** ==lets AI
  find every speech from the captions==.
- **LLM summary** outlines a speech the way you'd flow it. It is written
  once per speech and kept, so clicking back to a speech doesn't ask again.
- **Flow chart** (the graph icon next to Edit) ==draws the whole round as a
  mind map==. Each bubble shows its speech, with every Aff speech in its own
  shade of blue and every Neg speech in its own shade of red. **Speech
  columns** lines the speeches up left to right; click a speech's button to
  colour just its arguments and grey out the rest. **Free arrange** swirls the
  bubbles around the most-connected arguments, and you can drag them. You can
  edit the prompt, and it summarizes the last four speeches first if needed.
  Newer rounds often open with a chart already drawn, since one more round
  gets its chart every hour.
- **Alternatives** ==drafts other responses the speaker could have given and
  predicts the ballot after each== for the judge panel you pick. The top three
  have **AI Write** to draft the full speech in Debate Editor and record
  yourself giving it.
- ==Results are shared with every viewer==, so a round only needs analyzing
  once. Many rounds come pre-analyzed.

### Lectures [#video-watch-page-lectures]

A lecture or any other video that isn't a round ==opens on its **LLM
summary**==, an outline of the whole video's key points written from its
captions, with **Captions** in the next tab. The first person to open a
video writes it automatically, and ==it is saved and shared with every viewer
after that==. **Run again** rewrites it.

==Your watch page choices follow your account==: the copy button's format,
auto-scroll, **Skip breaks** and whether the live caption box is shown. Signed
out, they stay in this browser.

AI features need you to be signed in and use your own key if you've added one.
They work from auto-captions, so check them against the video.

### Team rankings [#video-watch-page-team-rankings]

When the teams are ranked, ==each gets a card with its rank, rating, win
rates, radar chart and season history==, plus a **Simulated matchup**
predicting the winner. Who actually won, and whether it was an upset, stays
hidden until you click **Reveal decision**. College cards show the school mascot (turn it off in
[Settings → Preferences](/settings/preferences)).

### Controls [#video-watch-page-controls]

The toolbar has play, slow-the-spread (75% speed), captions,
picture-in-picture, fullscreen, favorite and copy (URL, URL and title, or a
Chicago citation). **Minimize** hands the video to the floating player;
**Close** stops it. Only one video plays at a time across tabs.

**Pop out mini player** opens a small window with speech buttons. ==In the
desktop app it stays on top of every window==, so you can flow while it plays.

### Search and related videos [#video-watch-page-related-videos]

A search box under the details searches the whole library without stopping
the video; queue results to build a playlist. Related videos below show the
same tournament first, then the same format.

#### Up next in the popout player [#video-watch-page-up-next-in-the-popout-player]

A related video's popout icon ==lines it up in the floating player==, which
starts it when you leave the page. Click again to cancel.
