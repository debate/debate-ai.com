## Coaching Programs and Group Challenges [#coaching-programs]

==Create a coaching space for a squad roster== and open its live board:
topic sprint, group-challenge standings, each member's drills and practice
rounds, and a roster analytics table. Open it from the **Coaching Programs**
tab of [Coaching](#coaching).

## LLM Card Scoring [#llm-card-scoring]

==Scores a card on relevance, clarity, uniqueness, evidence quality and
usability==, ranks your cards, flags likely duplicates and can ask AI for a
written assessment. Paste several cards separated by `---` to score them in
bulk. Find it at **/research/cards/scoring**.

## AI Response-Outcome Charts [#response-outcome-charts]

==A vulnerability report for each saved round==: exposure per side, a chart of
your most exposed arguments with an Extend / Answer / Concede "what if"
picker, and an optional three-member AI counsel panel. Find it at
**/coaching/outcomes**.

## Scout-to-Strategy Workflow [#scout-to-strategy]

==Turns opponent scouting and judge profiles into a case choice and risk
level for an upcoming matchup==, with a case comparison table and an AI
evaluation. Open it from the **Strategy** tab of the
[Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds#prep-workspace).

## AI Outcome Responses [#ai-outcome-responses]

In a live round, open the ⋮ menu next to the speech timer and choose **AI
Outcome Responses**. ==It reads the round so far and suggests three ways to
give your current speech==. Each one shows:

- ==an estimated chance of winning==, with the best bet marked;
- a speech-ready outline you can copy;
- the opponent's likely answers and how the judge would likely vote;
- what could lose it, and card searches to fill evidence gaps.

You can change the judge paradigm or add a focus ("go for the DA") and
regenerate. ==The last run for each speech is saved with the round==: reopen
the dialog to see it again (with when it ran, the judge and your focus)
without waiting for a new one, and **Regenerate** replaces it. Signed in, it
follows your account to other devices. The win chances are AI estimates, not
predictions checked against real ballots.

## How Team Ratings Are Computed [#bradley-terry-debate-rankings]

The [Team Rankings](/docs/features/team-collaboration#team-rankings) rate
every team from ==every decided ballot of the season at once==, so beating
strong opponents counts for more than beating weak ones, and the order rounds
happened in doesn't matter.

- ==Aff and neg are balanced==, so a team isn't credited for drawing the
  favored side.
- Each judge's ballot counts on its own (a 3–0 beats a 2–1), and ==major
  tournaments count double==. Byes and forfeits are skipped.
- ==Teams with few rounds are pulled toward average==, so one hot weekend
  can't top the table.
- Ratings show on a ==0.0–10.9 scale==; the whole number is the team's tier,
  shaded gold at the top down to gray.

## Coach Materials [#coach-materials]

==Upload lecture transcripts, camp files, documents and recordings, then ask a
private coach AI questions answered only from them==. Pick a coaching style
(Standard, Socratic, Devil's Advocate or Fact-Checker); your choice is
remembered. A reviewer approves which materials can be used. Open it from the
**Coach Materials** tab of [Coaching](#coaching).

- ==Audio and video uploads aren't transcribed==.

## AI Coach Mode [#coaching-sessions]

==Extension, refutation, collapse and weighing prompts built from each round's
flow==, one card per session, with **Get AI feedback** for an open-ended
debrief. Pick a coaching style (Standard, Socratic, Devil's Advocate or
Fact-Checker) and it's remembered. Sessions sync to your account when signed
in. Open it from the **AI Coach Mode** tab of [Coaching](#coaching).

## Prediction Markets [#prediction-markets]

==Bet play-money points on who wins a round, who wins a tournament, and whose
team rating goes up==. Every account starts with **1,000 points**; points
==can't be bought, sold or cashed out==. Find it at **/practice/predictions**.

- **Site markets**: each month the site opens rating markets on each
  division's top five teams, and winner markets for the season's biggest
  tournaments.
- **Open your own**: a debate round, a tournament winner (up to 32 entries)
  or a team's rating move.
- ==Prices are the market's implied odds== and move as people bet; each
  winning share pays 1 point.
- Rounds and tournaments hosted on the site settle from their results; typed-in
  markets are settled by a moderator or by the creator if they hold no
  position. Voided markets refund every stake.

Good to know:

- You need a real (non-guest) account to bet, and can have ten markets open.
- ==You can't sell a position early, and a wallet never refills==.

## Coaching [#coaching]

==One page for a coach's tools==, one tab each:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Coach Workspace](/docs/features/tournaments-debaters#training-tools) | Every round and coaching panel, grouped by stage: flow, coaching, prep, scouting and practice. |
| [AI Coach Mode](#coaching-sessions) | Prompts generated from each round's flow. |
| [Coaching Programs](#coaching-programs) | Coaching spaces for a squad roster. |
| [Team Calendar](/docs/features/team-collaboration#team-calendar) | Tasks, deadlines and group-only virtual tournaments. |
| [Coach Materials](#coach-materials) | Grounding materials for the team coach AI. |
| Laptop-less Debating | A guide to debating off just a phone. |

Find it in the sidebar's **Practice Debates** section as Coach Workspace. The
open tab is part of the address, so you can bookmark or share it, and old
tool addresses open the matching tab.

**Load sample data** fills the Coach Workspace with demo scouting records and
a sample round built from real cards. ==The sample stays in this browser==,
and **Clear sample data** removes only the sample.
