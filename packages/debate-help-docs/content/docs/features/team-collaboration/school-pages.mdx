# Claiming a School Page

Coaches and school administrators can claim their school's page. Once a
moderator approves the claim, they manage the page: which teams and students
are part of the school, and the school's logo.

- **Route:** `/settings?category=my-school` (claim), `/@<school>` (manage; `/schools/<school>` redirects here), `/admin#schools` (review)
- **Package:** `debate-webview` (settings tab, school-page panel, admin panel), `apps/debate-ai.com` (API and tables)

## Claiming a school

1. Open **Settings → General → My school**.
2. Start typing your school's name and pick it from the list. If it isn't
   there, tick **My school isn't listed — create it** and type the name.
3. Choose whether you're the school's **Coach** or **School administrator**.
4. Confirm the claim one of two ways:
   - **Verify my school email** — enter the address your school gave you.
     We email a six-digit code; enter it under **Your claims**. Codes expire
     after 15 minutes and allow five tries; **Send a new code** issues another.
     Personal addresses (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud and the like) can't verify a
     school.
   - **I'll explain instead** — tell the moderators how you're connected to
     the school (at least 20 characters).
5. Submit. The claim then waits on a moderator, and you get a notification
   when it's decided, with any note the moderator left.

You can withdraw a claim any time before it's approved. You can't have two
open claims on the same school.

## Reviewing claims (moderators)

The **School claims** tab in `/admin` lists every claim waiting on review,
oldest first: the school, whether it's new, the claimant's name and account
email, the verified school address (if any), their explanation, and — when
the school already has owners — who they are. Approve or reject, optionally
with a note to the claimant. Claims still waiting on an email code aren't
listed until the code is entered.

Approving a claim on a school that isn't on any ranking list creates its
page, so `/@<name>` shows the school with its logo and roster before
any ranking lists it.

## Managing the school page

On the school's `/@<school>` page, owners (and staff) see controls under the school's
name:

- **Teams** and **Students** — add an entry by name (students may have an
  optional email, shown only to the page's managers) and remove one with ×.
  A school's roster holds up to 300 entries.
- **Logo** — **Set logo** uploads a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, scaled down to
  256 px; the trash button deletes it.

Everyone else sees the logo, who manages the page, and the roster. A school
nobody has claimed shows a **Claim this school's page** link instead.

## Known gaps

- An approved owner can only be removed by editing the database; there is no
  "remove owner" control yet.
- Roster entries are names, not links to ranked teams or user accounts.
