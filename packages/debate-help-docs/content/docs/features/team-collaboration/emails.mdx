# Emails

==The emails Debate AI sends, and when==. Every email carries the Debate AI
logo, ==one button to the page it is about==, and links to the site, the docs
and your settings.

| Email | When you get it | The button opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invited to collaborate on a file** | A contact shares a card or document with you from the Reason Editor's **Share with contacts** | Shared cards in [Contacts](/docs/features/team-collaboration/contacts) (`/contacts?tab=shared`) |
| **Invited to a debate** | Someone adds your email to a round in Create New Round and you don't have an account yet ([round invites](/docs/features/rounds/round-invites-and-notifications)) | The round (`/debate/…`) |
| **Welcome** | Written, not sent automatically yet | Debate AI, with links to research, practice rounds, Debate vs AI, videos and the docs |
| **Coach features** | Written, not sent automatically yet | The [Coach Workspace](/docs/features/ai-coaching/coaching), after a tour of the research-team tools (Task Inbox, Review Queue, Prep Room, Topic Coverage), coaching programs, the team calendar, coach materials, organizations and school pages |

==Registered users invited to a round get an in-app notification instead== of
the round email. ==Blocking someone in Contacts stops their shares==, and with
them the collaboration emails.

## Previews (admins)

The admin panel's **Emails** section (`/admin#emails`) ==lists every email
with sample data==, shows it as an inbox would (HTML) or as its plain-text
part, and has **Send test to me**, ==which sends the sample to your own
address==.
