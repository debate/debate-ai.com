# News & Forums

==One page for what's new in the community==, so a debater ==doesn't have to
separately check several pages==. It has two tabs: **News**, ==the feed of new
rounds, new files and forum activity==, and **Forums**, ==the discussion
threads==. It is the "News & Forums" row in the sidebar's
Practice Debates section (formerly Coaching).

Find it at **/news**. Old forum links still work and open the Forums tab or the
thread they pointed to.

## Forums

==Anyone signed in can open a thread== from the form at the top of the tab,
and ==files it under a category==: General, Policy, Lincoln-Douglas, Public
Forum, College, Evidence & research, Tournaments, Coaching & judging or
Off-topic. Threads opened before categories existed are in General.

- **Categories** — ==the pills above the list show one category's threads==;
  "All" shows every one. Each category has its own link, so it can be shared or bookmarked.
- **Sort** — ==Recent activity== (the default: the thread most recently
  replied to is on top), ==New== (newest threads first; replies don't move
  them) or ==Top== (most liked first). The choice is kept in the link too.
- **Likes** — ==every thread has a heart==, in the list and on the thread
  page, and so does every reply. Signed-out readers see the counts but need
  to sign in to like.

Opening a thread shows the opening post and its replies as a tree, ==the way
Reddit does==: a reply sits under the comment it answers, every level opens
on arrival, and ==any comment can be folded to one line== with the `[–]`
button beside its time or by clicking the line that runs down beside its
replies. A folded comment shows its author and how many replies it hides;
`[+]` opens it again. The back link returns to the thread's category.

## What the News tab shows

==Only new rounds, new files and forum activity, newest first==. ==A filter tab
appears for each category the feed has something in==:

- **Rounds** — ==the newest round videos on the site==.
- **Files** — ==the newest caselist files, plus one digest per day of new
  Argument Library cards== (the last three days). A digest lists a few card
  names, cleaned of Verbatim heading markers, then "and N more".
- **Forums** — ==each forum thread's latest activity==: "New thread" or "New
  reply in …". ==A new reply shows up as a new, unread item==.

Product updates, tool spotlights, Daily Best Card, Contributor Awards,
quest streaks, group challenges, revision incentives, prep notes and
coaching sessions ==are no longer posted here==; ==those features keep their own
pages==. ==Each item can be liked and is marked read on hover==.

**Following** ==shows updates from the teams and schools the reader follows==
from their profile pages: ==new rounds, new caselist research, tournament
results and monthly recaps== (see
[Team Rankings](/docs/features/team-collaboration/team-rankings#following-a-team-or-school)).
==The followed profiles are listed above the feed==, and their updates load just
after the rest of the page.

## Likes and read items follow your account

==Your likes and read items sync to your account==. Unliking an item on one
device ==clears the like on your other devices the next time they load the page==.
