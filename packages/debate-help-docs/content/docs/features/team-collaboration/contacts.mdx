# Contacts & Shared Cards

==The account-linked half of Debate Editor's real-time collaboration==. Co-editing
sessions ==used to reach a partner only over the clipboard==: start a session,
copy the share code or invite link, paste it somewhere. With Contacts, ==a
signed-in user has a contacts list==, ==can share the document they're editing
straight to a contact's account==, and the shared card shows up as **available**
==on that account on every device they sign in on==.

Find it at **/contacts** (tabs: Contacts and Shared cards). It's also in the
dock's Settings menu ("Contacts", with a count of pending requests), the Tools
page's Prep & Practice group, the editor sidebar's Documents row (the share icon
beside Upload), and `t contacts` in the Ctrl/Cmd-K search palette.

## Contacts list

==A typical friends list, saved to your account==:

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Add by name or email search, or by typing an email | A **pending** request. If that person already sent *you* one, it's accepted on the spot. |
| Accept / Decline (incoming) | Accept makes you contacts; decline removes the request. |
| Cancel (outgoing) / Remove (accepted) | Removes it; either side can remove an accepted contact. |
| Block | One-sided. Removes the contact *and stops every shared card* between the two of you, both directions. Blocked people can't send you requests or shares, and their attempts get the same generic refusal as any other, so they can't tell they're blocked. |
| Unblock | Lifts the block. Nothing removed by it is restored. |

Contacts show an **online / away** dot. ==A contact counts as online while they've
had the site open in the last two minutes==, so the dot can lag a little.

==Requests and accepts also send you a notification==.

## Organizations

An **organization** ==is a shared group, like a team or a school squad==. The
sidebar's account menu has an **Organizations** submenu: **Personal**, every
organization you belong to, **Members of …** for the active one, and **New
organization**. ==Creating one makes you its owner and switches to it==.

==The organization you pick changes what this page shows==:

| | Personal | An organization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contacts list | Your accepted contacts | The organization's other members (minus blocks either way); Remove is hidden, since members aren't contacts |
| Shared cards | Every live share | Only shares with its members |
| Who you can share with | Accepted contacts | Accepted contacts and its members |

==Members are added by an owner or admin from== **their own contacts** (Members of
… → Add), so ==nobody lands in a group without a contact who trusts them==; ==there
are no invitation emails==.

## Sharing a card

In Debate Editor, **Share with contacts** ==opens a picker of your contacts plus
an optional note==. It's the share icon in the sidebar's Documents row, beside
New file / New folder / Upload — ==sharing is something done to the open file==.
==It only shows when you're signed in and a document is open==, since ==sharing
starts a live co-editing session==. On Share:

1. If the document has no live session yet, ==the editor's own Start
   Collaboration Session step runs== (same confirm dialog), and collaboration is
   switched on if it was off, since sharing *is* the opt-in.
2. ==Each contact you picked gets a notification==, and ==the card appears in their
   Shared cards tab marked== **Available** (and **New** until first opened). ==They
   also get an "invited you to collaborate" email== with your note and a link
   (see [Emails](/docs/features/team-collaboration/emails)).

==Shares only ever reach== **accepted contacts** with no block in either
direction; ==anyone else you picked is reported back as skipped==. Sharing the
same document with the same contact again ==refreshes the share rather than
duplicating it==. ==The owner can== **Stop sharing** from the Shared cards tab;
==the recipient can== **Dismiss** their copy.

The Shared cards tab also has a paste box: a share code or full invite link
copied from the editor ==can be shared to contacts from there==, if you started
the session the old way.

### Privacy

==Co-editing is end-to-end encrypted==, and the server that passes edits between
you ==never sees the encryption key==. A share that follows a *person* across
devices does have to be kept with their account, so the site stores the share
code with it. ==The site already holds your editor documents, so this adds no new
kind of exposure==. See also [Card Sharing & Co-editing](/docs/features/team-collaboration#card-sharing-co-editing).

## Opening a shared card

**Open** on a received share opens Debate Editor, which then:

1. ==creates a== **fresh document** to hold the shared copy, so ==joining never
   overwrites the document you had open==;
2. joins the session ==the same way as pasting an invite link== (you confirm);
3. ==marks the share opened==.

==A share that arrives while the editor is open pops up with its own Open
button==. ==Co-editing needs the desktop layout==: on a phone ==the open is refused
with a message==.

## Your name on the cursor

Your cursor in a shared document shows your name. ==It's filled in from your
account the first time==, and ==never overwrites a name you typed== in the editor's
settings.

## Good to know

- ==Online status updates every half minute or so==, and a new share appears
  after the recipient reloads.
- ==Opening a shared card again always creates another fresh document==, though
  it rejoins the same session.
- ==A share goes to one person at a time==; there's no sharing to a whole
  organization at once yet.
- ==Organizations have no rename, leave, remove-member or role-change controls
  yet==, and no invitation by email.
- ==Your editor documents aren't separated by organization==.
