## Card Sharing & Co-editing [#card-sharing-co-editing]

==Send cards straight into a teammate's editor and edit a document together
live==, ==using the account you are signed in with== — the same one that syncs your
files and settings. ==There is no separate membership to link==.

Works in any Debate Editor surface: the REASON editor and speech documents.

### Turning it on

==There is nothing to turn on==. Sign in, and card sharing and co-editing are
ready on every device you use. ==Your name on cards you send and on your live
cursor comes from your account==. There are no pairing codes, recipient lists
or collaboration settings to fill in.

### Sending and receiving cards

==Cards teammates send you land in the== **Receive** pill next to the dropzone.
To share with a teammate, add them under **Contacts** and use **Share with
contacts**. ==Both of you need to be signed in==. ==Cards wait on the server for
up to a week if your editor is closed==.

### Editing a document together

Start a co-editing session from the Send pill's invite button, the **Start
Collaboration Session** command, or **Share with contacts** in the
editor. ==Everyone sees each other's cursors and edits as they type==.
==Starting a session needs you signed in==; ==someone you invite can join from the
invite link even without an account==.

==Everything is end-to-end encrypted in the editor before it leaves your
browser== — ==the server only ever passes along scrambled data it can't read==.

## Collaboration Prep Room [#collaboration-prep-room]

==A topic's shared prep space==: a switcher for every topic with either a
tracked-argument checklist or submitted evidence, ==a keyword search over
that topic's evidence and draft blocks==, and the topic's ==coverage-gap
research tasks routed to available contributors==.

Open it from the **Sprint** tab of the [Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration).

### What it shows

- A topic switcher and a one-line summary of the topic's prep.
- A keyword search over the topic's evidence and draft blocks.
- The research tasks routed to contributors.
- An "active now" roster of who is working on the topic.
- A timeline of the room's activity.

A **Shared task checklist** ==lets any teammate add a freeform todo item,
toggle it done, and remove it==.

### Good to know

- Everything in the room ==syncs to your account== when you're signed in, but
  ==it is yours alone, not one shared room==: each teammate ==keeps their own
  copy==.
- ==There are no reviewer permissions==.
- There is no shared file or attachment area yet.

## Contribution Leaderboard [#contribution-leaderboard]

==Ranks community contributors== — cards, summaries, analytics, and other
research contributions — ==by a blended helpfulness score==, and ==shows each
contributor's unlock tier, earned badges, and current daily-quest streak==.

Find it at **/coaching/leaderboard**.

### What it shows

| Column | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Rank | Position by helpfulness score |
| Contributor | Who made the contributions |
| Tier | novice/apprentice/veteran/expert |
| Contributions | How many contributions they've made |
| Total/Avg score | Blended helpfulness score |
| Streak | Current consecutive-day quest streak |
| Badges | Tier + streak-milestone badges |

A **Range** filter (All time/This week/This month) and a **Category**
filter ==re-derive the whole roster==. Each row has a "History" toggle showing
endorsement history.

### Good to know

- ==There are no contributor roles or permissions yet==.
- "My endorsement activity" ==only appears for a signed-in visitor==.

## Contributions Feed [#contributions-feed]

==Lets a contributor submit a contribution== (a card, summary, highlight,
annotation, original argument, or refutation), then ==lists every contribution ranked by blended helpfulness score==, with
Like/Save/Endorse actions per entry.

Open it from the **Rewards** tab of the [Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration).

### What it shows

A submission form followed by the ranked feed. Each entry shows its kind
badge, contributor, helpfulness score, likes/saves/endorsement counts, and
Like/Save/Endorse buttons. A "Flagged for review" moderator toggle ==narrows
the feed to popularity-driven outliers==.

A **Comments** toggle on each entry opens ==the same threaded discussion== used
on video watch pages and forum threads (signed-in accounts can comment and
like). ==Comments are collapsed until you open them==.

### Good to know

- ==A new contribution starts with a neutral quality score==.
- The Contributor ID is ==filled in for you, not locked==: you can still submit
  under another typed ID.

## Top Contributor Awards [#contributor-awards]

==Recognizes the top contributor in each contribution category== by total
helpfulness score within that category, and ==lets a day's standings be
frozen as an official "announced" result==.

Find it at **/research/cards/awards**.

### What it shows

One card per contribution category that has entries (Best Evidence Finder, Best Explainer, etc.), plus **Live standings**, **Announce
today's awards**, a **Hall of Fame**, **Announced history**, and **Peer
Nominations** with a "Second" action.

### Good to know

- ==Awards are not announced automatically==; someone has to press **Announce
  today's awards**.
- ==Anyone can nominate or second==; there are no permissions on it.
- ==Announced awards and nominations sync to your account== when you're signed
  in, but they are ==yours alone, not shared with the team==.

## Group Challenges [#group-challenges]

==Lets a coach create a squad-scoped friendly challenge== — either "reach N
matching contributions" or "reach N recorded wins" — with a roster and a
challenge window, ==showing every challenge's live standings==.

Find it at **/research/cards/group-challenges**.

### What it shows

Each challenge ==is its own card==: title, goal,
window, roster, and ==live standings (best first, 🏆 marking the MVP)==. A
"reach N wins" challenge gets a "Record a win" action.

### Good to know

"Record a win" ==takes a typed name, not a login== — signing in only fills it in
for you.

## Review Queue [#review-queue]

==Lets a user move a submitted card through peer review== — start a review,
submit it, leave and resolve comments, request changes, approve, and
publish — ==before it goes live in the Shared Evidence Library==.

Open it from the **Review** tab of the [Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration).

### What it shows

Every review with its status, lifecycle action buttons, a
comment thread (with severity and a "Resolve" action), ==an
unresolved-blocking warning, a review-aging badge==, and a **Reviewer
workload** table. Approve/reject/publish ==are gated on the acting
reviewer's own Progress Unlocks tier and blocked for self-review==.

### Good to know

==The reviewer is a typed name, not a verified account== — signing in only
fills it in for you.

## Task Inbox [#task-inbox]

==Shows every research task routed to a contributor, grouped by topic==, so a
contributor or coach can see what's assigned, ==mark tasks done, and have a
different contributor verify them before they count as complete==.

Open it from the **Routing** tab of the [Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration).

### What it shows

Per topic: task, coverage level, assignee, a "Mark done" action (moves to
**Awaiting verification**, ==where a different contributor must==
**Verify**), a "Reassign"/"Assign to…" control, a priority flag, a "My
tasks" filter, and a **Team capacity** view.

### Good to know

- "My tasks" ==takes a typed name== — signing in only fills it in for you.
- ==Anyone can reassign tasks or set priority==; there is no coach-only
  permission yet.

## Team Collaboration Mode [#team-collaboration-mode]

==Lets a teammate leave a live prep note against a shared topic sprint==, and
shows every note grouped by topic.

Find it at **/research/cards/collaboration**.

### What it shows

A form to add a note, notes grouped by topic with status-cycle and
assign/unassign actions, ==a live "active now" roster== per topic, and ==the full topic sprint==
(quests, task routing, progress board, notes) plus an **End-of-sprint
retrospective** download.

### Good to know

==Name fields take typed text== — signing in only fills them in for you.
