# Top Contributor Awards

==Recognizes the top contributor in each contribution category== by total
helpfulness score within that category, and ==lets a day's standings be
frozen as an official "announced" result==.

Find it at **/research/cards/awards**.

## What it shows

One card per contribution category that has entries (Best Evidence Finder, Best Explainer, etc.), plus **Live standings**, **Announce
today's awards**, a **Hall of Fame**, **Announced history**, and **Peer
Nominations** with a "Second" action.

## Good to know

- ==Awards are not announced automatically==; someone has to press **Announce
  today's awards**.
- ==Anyone can nominate or second==; there are no permissions on it.
- ==Announced awards and nominations sync to your account== when you're signed
  in, but they are ==yours alone, not shared with the team==.
