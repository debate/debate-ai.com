# Contributions Feed

==Lets a contributor submit a contribution== (a card, summary, highlight,
annotation, original argument, or refutation), then ==lists every contribution ranked by blended helpfulness score==, with
Like/Save/Endorse actions per entry.

Open it from the **Rewards** tab of the [Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration).

## What it shows

A submission form followed by the ranked feed. Each entry shows its kind
badge, contributor, helpfulness score, likes/saves/endorsement counts, and
Like/Save/Endorse buttons. A "Flagged for review" moderator toggle ==narrows
the feed to popularity-driven outliers==.

A **Comments** toggle on each entry opens ==the same threaded discussion== used
on video watch pages and forum threads (signed-in accounts can comment and
like). ==Comments are collapsed until you open them==.

## Good to know

- ==A new contribution starts with a neutral quality score==.
- The Contributor ID is ==filled in for you, not locked==: you can still submit
  under another typed ID.
