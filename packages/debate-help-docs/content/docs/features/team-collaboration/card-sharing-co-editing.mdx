# Card Sharing & Co-editing

==Send cards straight into a teammate's editor and edit a document together
live==, ==using the account you are signed in with== — the same one that syncs your
files and settings. ==There is no separate membership to link==.

- **Where:** any Debate Editor surface (`/reason-editor`, speech documents) →
  ⚙ Settings → **Collaboration**
- **Package:** `debate-editor`, served by the app's own relay at `/api/collab-relay`

## Turning it on

Switch on **Enable collaboration**. The **Account sync** row ==shows which
account this browser is syncing as==; if it says *Not signed in*, use its
**Sign in** link. ==That is the whole setup==.

## Sending and receiving cards

Copy **Your pairing code** and ==give it to a teammate==; add theirs under
**Recipients**. ==Drag a card onto the== **Send** pill to push it to them, and
==cards they send you land in== **Receive**. ==Both of you need to be signed in==.
==Cards wait on the server for up to a week if your editor is closed==, and
**Regenerate** ==cuts off anyone holding your old code==.

## Editing a document together

Start a co-editing session from the Send pill's invite button, the **Start
Collaboration Session** command, or **Share with contacts** on
`/reason-editor`. ==Everyone sees each other's cursors and edits as they type==.
==Starting a session needs you signed in==; ==someone you invite can join from the
invite link even without an account==.

==Everything is end-to-end encrypted in the editor before it leaves your
browser== — ==the server stores and forwards only ciphertext==.
