# 700 Philosophical Debate Resolutions

These topics frame questions as affirmative-or-negative resolutions about what we ought to believe, value, or regard as legitimate—not as proposals requiring a specific government policy. Each gives both sides a clear proposition to defend.

Topic IDs use Category.Number, such as Nuke.1, Dem.1, and Edu.1. Numbering restarts at 1 in each category. All 700 source entries are retained, including related or overlapping topics. Hidden source-number comments preserve traceability without retaining global numbers in topic headings.

Affirmative and negative positions are debate starting points, not endorsements or independently verified empirical findings. Institutional practices are evaluated for moral legitimacy rather than implementation details.

Source: “Debate Topic Dataset.” User-provided CSV text, n.d.

## Nuclear Ethics, Energy, and Security

### Nuke.1 — Go nuclear
Resolved: It is morally legitimate for society to go nuclear.

- Pro: The practice is justified insofar as it advances protection against catastrophic harm. In deciding whether society may go nuclear, this value should take priority over objections based solely on existing conventions.
- Con: The appeal to protection against catastrophic harm does not establish this practice’s legitimacy. A decision to go nuclear must also satisfy the independent demands of duties to future generations, rather than treating the preferred end as sufficient justification.
