## Flow-in-Speech Flow Annotations [#flow-annotations]

==Drop a timestamped note while watching a round, tie it to a flow argument,
and jump back to it later==. Find it at **/annotations**.

Notes are listed newest first with **Jump to**, can be filtered by speech,
speaker or tag, and show as a clock badge on their flow cell. Signed in, they
save to your account (private to you).

## Flow Cloud Save [#flow-cloud-save]

==Save a flow to your account and load it on any device==. Click the cloud
icon on a flow chip in Flow History; the **Saved to account** tab lists saved
flows and rounds with a search box, a count beside each heading ("2 of 5"
while searching) and a sort menu: newest saved first, oldest saved first, or
by name.

- ==Saving never silently overwrites a newer copy==. If another device saved
  first, the icon turns into a warning; click again to save anyway.
- ==Removing asks first==. The trash icon removes only the copy in your
  account, after you confirm; the copy in this browser stays, and removing a
  round leaves its saved flows. If a removal fails, the row says so and the
  item stays listed.

## Speech Transcript Summaries [#flow-summaries]

==One card per round with a per-argument summary, plus cross-ex questions and
extension ideas for anything unanswered==. Paste or dictate speech text and AI
builds the summary. Find it at **/summaries**.

## Round Workspace — Tools for this round menu [#flow-tools-menu]

The ==wrench button in the round sidebar== opens Argument Tree Outline, AI
Response-Outcome Charts, Practice Drills and AI Coach Mode for the selected
flow.

## Prep Notes [#prep-notes]

==Every strategy note across your flows, grouped Needs follow-up / Open /
Covered==, with priority, an assignee, replies and a **Jump to argument**
link. Open it from the **Notes** tab of the
[Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds#prep-workspace).

## Shared, AI-Generated Debate Flow [#shared-flow-sync]

==Log proposed edits to flow arguments and preview how they merge==, with
clashing edits flagged side by side for a person to resolve. Turn on **Live
sync** (off by default) to share edits with teammates as you work. Find it in
the Flow section of [Coaching](/docs/features/ai-coach#coaching).

## Speech Documents Cloud Save [#speech-documents-cloud-save]

Signed in, ==your [Speech Documents](#speech-document-target) send log and
speech-to-document links sync to your account== and follow you to other
devices.

## Speech Documents [#speech-document-target]

In Debate Editor, ==mark an open document as your speech doc== (File →
Speech), then ==press the backtick key to send the selected card or section
into it==.

The **/speech-documents** page keeps a log of everything you've sent, newest
first, with a fallacy-hint summary across your sends. A speech's header can
also link to one of your editor documents so its word counts come from that
file.
