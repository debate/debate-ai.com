## Flow-in-Speech Flow Annotations [#flow-annotations]

==Lets a viewer drop a timestamped note while watching a streamed or recorded
round== in the video player, ==tie it to a specific flow argument,
and jump straight back to it later==.

Find it at **/annotations**.

### What it shows

A form to drop a new annotation (the flow, speech and argument it belongs to,
a ==timestamp defaulting to the player's current position==, an optional note,
speaker, and tag). ==Every saved annotation is listed newest first== with
**Jump to** (switches videos if needed) and **Clear** actions. ==Search and
filter by speech, speaker, or tag==, plus a per-flow **Download annotations**
export.

Every cell in the live flow grid ==that has annotations shows a clock badge;
clicking it jumps to the earliest one==.

### Saving

==Annotations save to your account the moment you drop or clear one== when
you're signed in. ==They are yours alone, not shared with your team==, and ==one
saved on another device shows up the next time you reload==.

## Flow Cloud Save [#flow-cloud-save]

==Lets a signed-in user save an individual flow to their account and load it
back on any device==.

Find it in the Flow History dialog's "Saved to account" tab, and as a
cloud-upload icon on each flow chip in the "Rounds" tab.

### What it does

==Clicking a flow's cloud icon saves that flow to your account==. The "Saved to account" tab ==lists every saved flow with Load and
Remove actions==. ==A signed-out user sees a sign-in prompt== instead; ==local behavior is never blocked==.

==A search box at the top of the "Saved to account" tab narrows both the saved
flows and the saved rounds by name== as you type. Every word you type must
appear, in any order, and case doesn't matter. ==A flow saved as part of a
round also matches on that round's name== (tournament and round level), so
searching a tournament finds its flows. Clearing the box shows everything
again. It appears once you're signed in and your saved flows
have loaded.

==Saving never quietly overwrites a newer copy==: if a newer version was saved
from somewhere else since this browser last saw it, ==the save is rejected with a conflict instead
of silently overwriting it==, and the flow's cloud icon turns into a warning
that ==a second click (an explicit "save anyway") resolves==.

## Speech Transcript Summaries [#flow-summaries]

==Shows every saved flow summary, one card per round== — a concise
==per-argument summary of that round's flow, plus suggested cross-examination
questions and extension ideas== for anything still unanswered.

Find it at **/summaries**.

### What it shows

Each saved summary is its own card with the round's per-argument summary and,
for unanswered arguments, ==ranked cross-exam questions and extension ideas
(strongest opportunity first)==. A **Generate from raw speech text** form
==lets AI build a round's summary from one or more pasted transcripts==, with
microphone dictation and bulk multi-speech upload. Under each pasted
transcript, a "Possible fallacies" hint (ad hominem, straw man, false
dichotomy) appears when the wording matches; hints are prompts to look
closer, never verdicts.

### Good to know

==Microphone dictation transcribes live speech only== — you can't upload an
already-recorded audio or video file.

## Round Workspace — Tools for this round menu [#flow-tools-menu]

==Adds a quick-access menu to the round workspace== linking straight to ==the
flow-driven analysis tools that act on whatever flow is currently selected==.

Look for ==the wrench-icon button in the round workspace sidebar's
quick-action row==.

### What it does

Clicking the wrench icon opens a menu of tools that each have a "Generate
… for current round" action — Argument Tree Outline, AI Response-Outcome
Charts, Practice Drills, and AI Coach Mode — ==each disabled until a flow is
selected==. Each of those pages also shows an "Other round tools" row of
links to the rest.

==Only those four tools are listed==; other panels that read a flow are left
out on purpose.

## Prep Notes [#prep-notes]

==Shows every "Strategy Sync Notes" note across every flow==, ==grouped by status
(needs follow-up first)==, with a status button and an **Assign to** control
==for handing a note off as a task==.

Open it from the **Notes** tab of the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds#prep-workspace).

### What it shows

==Notes grouped into Needs follow-up / Open / Covered==, each with a priority
flag, an assignee, a status button, and a **Replies** thread. A related
**Notifications** page ==shows assignment digests per day==. Each note has ==a
"Jump to argument" link back into the live flow grid==, and ==you can create a
note directly from a badge on a flow grid cell==.

### Good to know

- A reply's "Your name" ==field is free-form text, not a verified identity== —
  signed in, it fills in your account name, but you can still type over it.
- ==Notifications are in-app only==, covering prep-note assignments.

## Shared, AI-Generated Debate Flow [#shared-flow-sync]

==Lets a contributor log a proposed edit to a specific flow argument== and
==previews how every logged edit merges into the round's flow==, ==flagging
edits that genuinely clash for a person to resolve==.

Find it in the Flow section of the [Coaching](/docs/features/ai-coach#coaching) page.

### What it shows

A **Flow Edit Log** form, grouped by flow, with a merge preview
(**Shared Flow Sync**) ==showing how many boxes would update and flagging
conflicts==. A **Live sync** toggle ==shares edits across contributors'
browsers as they work==, plus a =="who's editing now" indicator==. ==Conflicts
show as a word-by-word side-by-side comparison==.

### Good to know

- ==Live sync is opt-in, per flow, and off by default==, and ==it checks for new
  edits every few seconds rather than instantly==.
- Your logged edits sync to your account like any other tool's saved work, so
  ==signing in on another device carries your flow edit log with you==. That is
  separate from the Live sync toggle, which is about ==sharing *in-progress*
  edits with teammates in real time==.

## Speech Documents Cloud Save [#speech-documents-cloud-save]

Syncs the [Speech Documents](/docs/features/flow#speech-document-target)
==send log to a signed-in user's account==, ==alongside your other synced debate
work== (flows, rounds, word-count history, judge decisions, and Debate Editor
documents).

### What it does

==Your existing send log is merged into your account the first time you sign
in==, and every new send is saved from then on — ==a send made in any tab is
synced, not just the tab with the Speech Documents page open==. Entries from
another device ==land in their correct place by time sent== instead of
showing as the newest.

### Linked speech documents

Separate from the send log, ==the speech header's link picker== (which editor
document a speech reads its word counts from) ==follows a signed-in user to a
second device==. A link ==works there once that round and the linked document
have synced as well==; until then ==the speech falls back to the flow's own
speech doc==. Links saved before this feature ==are kept and start syncing on
your next change==.

## Speech Documents [#speech-document-target]

The **Speech Documents** page (**/speech-documents**) ==keeps a lasting log
of everything Debate Editor has sent== into your designated speech doc.

### How sending works [#speech-document-target-how-sending-works]

In Debate Editor, ==mark an open document as the speech doc== (File menu →
Speech), then ==send the current selection (or card or section) with the
backtick key==. ==It is inserted straight into the speech doc==, whether that
document is open in the same window or another tab. ==The speech doc is just an
ordinary open document== — ==Debate Editor keeps no separate copy==.

### What this page shows [#speech-document-target-what-this-page-shows]

A **send log**, not a document list: ==the plain text of what actually landed
for every send==, saved in this browser, ==newest first, with Remove on each
entry and a Clear history action==. ==The log is one history for all your
sends==, not grouped by speech doc.

### Fallacy hints across your sent speeches [#speech-document-target-fallacy-hints-across-your-sent-speeches]

Above the log, ==a short summary counts how often the fallacy hints fire
across everything you have sent==: how many sends and words were scanned,
==the count for each kind== (ad hominem, straw man, false dichotomy), hints
per 1,000 words, and ==the three sends with the most hints==. It is a nudge to
reread your phrasing, ==not a verdict==.

==Signed in, it covers the sends synced from all your devices==. Remove an
entry or clear the history and ==it drops out of the count straight away==.

### Linking a speech to an editor document [#speech-document-target-linking-a-speech-to-an-editor-document]

==A speech's header can point at one of your recent editor documents==, so ==its
word counts come from that file instead of the flow's own speech text==. ==Links
belong to the round== (or the flow, when it has no round), and ==a signed-in
user's links follow them to other devices==; signed out, they stay in this
browser.

==A link only works on another device once both the round and the document
it names have synced there too== — until then ==the speech falls back to its own
text==.
