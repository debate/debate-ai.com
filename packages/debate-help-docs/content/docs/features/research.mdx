## Common Argument Library — Saved Collections [#argument-library-collections]

==Save a set of tag filters as a named collection== and apply it again with
one click. Collections can be renamed or updated and sync to your account.
Find it at **/research/cards/argument-library**.

## Team Brainstorm Assist [#brainstorm-board]

==Submit, AI-generate and upvote ideas for an argument block==, grouped into
boards and ranked by votes, with near-duplicates flagged for merging. An
optional **Session timer** time-boxes a sprint for everyone. Open it from the
**Sprint** tab of the [Research Workspace](#research-collaboration).

## Research Progress Tracking [#research-progress-tracking]

==Each contributor's contributions, task completion and per-topic
breakdown==, with a team topic comparison (least covered first) and your own
research goal. Find it at **/coaching/progress**.

## Team Research [#team-research]

==Team Research is now part of the [Research Workspace](#research-collaboration)==.
Its tabs moved there (Topic Coverage, Sprint, Review, Routing, Rewards,
Level), Practice Partners got its own page, and old links open the matching
tab.

## Topic Coverage Dashboard [#topic-coverage-dashboard]

==Tracks a topic's argument checklist as missing, thin or covered==, based on
cards in the shared evidence library, with a "Needs the most work" list. Your
checklist is private to you. Open it from the **Topic Coverage** tab of the
[Research Workspace](#research-collaboration).

## Outline Filters and Argument Tree View [#argument-tree-outline]

==A filterable outline of each round's flow==, at **/outline**. Filter by
kind, side, speech, argument type, contributor, evidence status or
**Unanswered only**, and save filter presets.

- **Map views:** besides the outline, view the round as a tiered tree,
  sunburst, mind map, bubble map or Sankey chart. ==Claims are green (pro) or
  red (con) relative to their parent==; click one to focus it with its pros
  and cons.
- **Tagging:** right-click a row in the flow grid and choose **Tag
  Argument…**.
- **Export:** download the outline as `.txt`, or a map as SVG, PNG or PDF.

## Card Library Import [#card-library-import]

==How the evidence behind CARD search gets into the site==: admins import the
published card dump and openCaselist speech documents from the admin panel.
There's nothing for debaters to do; new cards are searchable straight away.

## How Debate Editor keeps your files safe [#cardmirror-embed-persistence]

==Debate Editor saves as you type, and never saves a blank page over a file
you've loaded==.

- The header shows **Saving…**, **Unsaved changes** or **Couldn't save —
  retrying…**; failed saves keep retrying on their own.
- Closing the tab saves anything still waiting.
- If a stored document can't be read, ==the editor stops saving it and offers
  a Download== so you can rescue what's on screen. The server copy is
  untouched.
- `.cmir` documents (Topic Starters, uploads) open in read mode.

### Overview [#cards-overview]

==Critical times call for critical thinkers to create a crowdsourced annotated research dataset==, for AI models to recommend research quotes, to evolve crowdsourced chain-of-thought reasoning, unlock faster ways to read long articles, to monitor developments in a knowledge graph by topic modeling, and to provide a public service of answers to research. ==Debate should be a war of warrants where victories are vectorized as weights== — weights which lead to ==the emergence of Collective Consciousness==.

### Features & Capabilities [#cards-features--capabilities]

==Search summaries, cites, highlighted and full text over millions of research quotes==, scout what teams and schools read, and sort by how often a card was read in speeches.

- **Quotes, Outlines and Debates** buttons switch between cards, uploaded documents and round videos. With no search term, the most recently popular cards show first.
- **Search tips:** cards whose tag matches rank first. Use `"double quotes"` for an exact phrase; press **Tab** or **→** to accept the greyed-out completion.
- **Source links:** each card shows whether its source link still works. **Full article** pulls the source's full text beside the card, where you can highlight passages and copy them with the citation to cut a new card.
- **On a phone**, tap a quote for its card, AI summary and full page tabs.

### 1. The Debate Singularity is Happening [#cards-1-the-debate-singularity-is-happening]

==AI now researches topics in depth, highlights key quotes, frames both sides of arguments==, coaches preparation, exposes flaws in evidence cards, and crafts strategic closing speeches that compare competing claims. ==Integrating AI into debate unlocks the next stage of emergent complexity== in socio-political governance.

### 2. Collective Thought Engine [#cards-2-collective-thought-engine]

Language Models can ==distill collective thought into a vector space where every point carries a weighted value==, reflecting its contribution to decision-making. ==AI agents learn which arguments and sources persuade different audiences==, and crowdsourced argument outlines, much like GitHub's reusable code, help AI evaluate complex decisions with greater depth.

### 3. Transparent Reasoning [#cards-3-transparent-reasoning]

==AI reveals the exact sentences and citations behind its reasoning==, so users can verify it. Debate Garden ==aggregates perspectives across the ideological spectrum==, ==equipping users with the strongest, most rigorously reasoned arguments on every side==.

### 4. Outcome Simulation Trees [#cards-4-outcome-simulation-trees]

AI-powered practice rounds ==map trees of possible outcomes and responses==, ==surfacing the most persuasive strategies==.

### 5. Outlines of Current News Issues [#cards-5-outlines-of-current-news-issues]

==Each debate card fits into a topic tree linking evidence, assumptions, and values==, shifting debate from isolated rounds to ==a shared model of research crowdsourced to the public==, like Wikipedia or GitHub.

### 6. Solving Post-Self Alignment [#cards-6-solving-post-self-alignment]

==Mapping the whole internet as a debate outline== shows how each idea fits into ==a whole greater than the sum of its parts==, countering the bias and tribalism of isolated news bubbles. This can ==reduce LLM hallucination and steer alignment with common social values==.

### 7. Topic Research Unified Tree Hierarchy (TRUTH) [#cards-7-topic-research-unified-tree-hierarchy-truth]

Metadata extracted for LLM analysis lets models ==detect logic flaws, flag overstatements, strengthen warrants==, and place each claim within the ==Topic Research Unified Tree Hierarchy (TRUTH)==, a consensus-driven system that ==seeks grounded truth and aligns AI reasoning with shared human values==.

## Shared Evidence Library [#evidence-library]

==The team's shared repository of cut cards and analytic blocks==, at
**/research/cards/library**. Submit cards with a live word count, and search
or filter by kind, topic, case area and tag. Editing a card scores the
revision for [Revision Incentives](/docs/features/gamification#revision-incentives).

- **Format citation** rewrites a pasted citation short-cite first (`Jane
  Smith, Professor at MIT, 2024` becomes `Smith 24 — …`). Check the guessed
  author before saving.
- **Check this page** tells you whether a URL has already been cut.
- The **Argument Library** groups library cards and tagged contributions into
  topic folders and tag collections.

## On-Page Card Reuse Search [#on-page-card-reuse-search]

==Checks whether a page has already been cut into a card before you cut a
duplicate==. Open the browser extension's popup on an article (or paste a URL

## Browser Extension: Reading Mode and Search [#browser-extension-reading-and-search]

==Tools the Debate AI browser extension adds to every page==. Its settings are
in the **Browser extension** tab of the app's Settings.

- **Reading mode:** on articles over 1,000 words, ==an arrow on the left edge
  opens the article beside an AI panel==. It also works on YouTube videos
  (timestamped transcript) and PDFs.
- **Citations:** every article gets a Chicago, MLA or APA citation. **Copy**
  (**Alt+C**) copies citation and article; **Copy highlighted** (**Alt+P**)
  copies only what you highlighted (**Alt+H**), formatting kept.
- **Backtick search:** ==select text and press the key under Escape== (`` ` ``) to search it on Debate AI
  cards, Google, Scholar, Wikipedia, YouTube and more.
- **Open a Word file:** right-click a `.docx` link and choose **Open in Debate
  AI editor** to import it into My Files.

The docs' Synergy Slayer papers have the same reading mode with no extension
needed: **Questions** (AI questions about the page), **References** (every
cited source in a side panel), full-screen **Reading mode**, **Export** (PDF,
Word, Markdown, Google Doc) and **Ask AI**.

## Card hover actions in the editor [#card-hover-actions]

==Hover over a card in Debate Editor for buttons beside it==:

- **Summarize**: a short AI summary of the claim, warrant and impact.
- **Find flaws**: the weaknesses an opponent would attack.
- **Read aloud**: reads the tag, the author and year, then the highlighted
  text. **View → Read whole document aloud** reads every card in order.

Set voices and speeds in **Settings → Sound & speech → Read aloud voice &
speed** (or the gear under Read aloud). ==The default Kokoro voices run in
your browser== after a one-time download of about 90 MB, with no daily limit;
Cloudflare voices run on the server when signed in, and your browser's own
voice is the fallback, so Read aloud always works.

AI answers use your own key if you've added one; otherwise each new answer
counts toward your daily card AI analyses.

## File Market [#file-market]

==Sell your research files, or buy others'==, at **/research/market**. Anyone
can read a file's table of contents for free; ==buying unlocks the full file
and saves it to your files==.

- **Wallet:** **Add $10** pays through Stripe. Sales go into the same wallet
  and can't be withdrawn yet.
- **Selling:** right-click a file in **Files** and choose **Sell…**, set a
  price from $0.50 to $500, and share the link. Buyers get the file as it was
  when you chose Sell; **Stop selling** takes it off the market.
- **Buying:** **Buy** pays from your wallet and opens the file. There are no
  refunds from the site.

## Read mode in the card editor [#cardmirror-read-mode]

The **Read mode** button ==shows only what you'd read aloud==: the tag, a short
**Smith 21** cite, and the highlighted text (or underlined text if a card has
no highlighting). It never edits the document. Turn on **Read mode: keep
entire cite** to see full citations. **Convert Cards to Read Mode** writes the
result into the document.

## Research Publishers [#research-publishers]

==Give your files an author, publisher and research group, and let others
subscribe==. Edit a file's details from **About this file** in the sidebar.

- **Camp publishers:** files are matched to the summer camp in their name
  (`Spending DA - DDI 2026.docx` is Dartmouth). Click the camp for its dates,
  cost and website, or **Follow** it.
- **Publisher pages** at **/research/publishers/**`name` list every file
  published (no card text).
- **Subscribe** pays one month from your wallet and lets you **Save a copy**
  of every file. Subscriptions don't renew by themselves.
