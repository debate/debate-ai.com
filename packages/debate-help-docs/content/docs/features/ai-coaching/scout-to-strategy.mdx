# Scout-to-Strategy Workflow

==Turns your opponent-scouting and judge-tendency profiles== into a
==case-choice and risk recommendation for an upcoming matchup==.

Open it from the **Strategy** tab of the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace).

## What it shows

A form (matchup, opponent and judge lookup, your side, case options) ==builds
a recommendation and adds it to that matchup's history==: ==recommended
case and ranking, judge adaptation notes, risk level and factors==, a **Case
comparison** table, and a **Get AI case-choice evaluation** action. **Send
to Pre-Round Briefing** ==exports a one-line summary==.

==Recommendation history is saved to your account==.
