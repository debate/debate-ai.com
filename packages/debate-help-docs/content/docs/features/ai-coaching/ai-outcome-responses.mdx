# AI Outcome Responses

During a live round, the speech timer's menu (the ⋮ next to the timer) has
an **AI Outcome Responses** item. ==It reads the whole round so far== and
==suggests three ways to give the speech you are on==, then plays each one
forward.

## What it shows

==Three candidate approaches side by side==. Each one has:

- a **success rate**: ==the AI's estimate that this approach wins the round==
  for your side, ==with the best bet marked==;
- a speech-ready **outline** that ==extends cards already read and answers
  the opponent's newest arguments== (copy button included). ==A "Possible
  fallacies" hint appears under the outline== when its wording matches an ad
  hominem, straw man or false dichotomy pattern; it is a prompt to look
  closer, not a verdict;
- the **opponent's likely answers** ==in their next speech==;
- the **judge decision**: ==who the predicted judge votes for on that path==,
  and why;
- the **issues** ==that could lose it== (drops, missing evidence, judge fit);
- **cards to find**: ==searches that run against CARDS in place== and
  show the highlighted text of the top hits, with a copy button and a link
  to open the full search.

==You can override the judge paradigm and add an optional focus== ("go for the
DA") before regenerating.

## What it reads

- **Prior speeches:** ==every speech before the current one==. A speech's
  linked document is used when it has one, otherwise the flow's own speech
  document. ==Highlighted card text is kept marked== so the AI can tell what
  was read aloud.
- **Flow:** ==every flow sheet in the round==, argument by argument, with
  ==unanswered arguments flagged==.
- **Judge:** ==the round judge with the most ballots in==
  [Judge Profiles](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/judge-profiles), voting under that profile's most-tagged
  paradigm. With no saved profile for the round's judges, the paradigm most
  common across your saved profiles is used, else a flow judge.

## Good to know

- ==Fallacy hints are a quick wording check==, and only look at the outline,
  not the opponent answers or issues.
- ==Results are not saved==; closing the dialog keeps them only until you
  switch speeches.
- ==Success rates are AI estimates, not calibrated against real ballots==.
- On the free plan ==the AI's reply is length-limited==, which can cut a
  long three-candidate reply short; the dialog then shows an error.
- ==Very long rounds are trimmed==, oldest speeches first.
