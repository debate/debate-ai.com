# Coaching

==One page for a coach's tools==. ==Six tools that used to have their own pages==
and sidebar entries sit side by side, one tab each:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Coach Workspace](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/training-tools) | Every round and coaching panel, grouped by stage of a round: flow, coaching, prep, scouting, and practice. |
| [AI Coach Mode](/docs/features/ai-coaching/coaching-sessions) | Extension, refutation, collapse, and weighing prompts generated from each round's flow. |
| [Coaching Programs](/docs/features/ai-coaching/coaching-programs) | Group coaching spaces scoped to a squad roster, with roster analytics and drill reviews. |
| [Team Calendar](/docs/features/team-collaboration/team-calendar) | Research tasks, assignments, deadlines, and group-only virtual tournaments for each group you coach. |
| [Coach Materials](/docs/features/ai-coaching/coach-materials) | Grounding materials for the team coach AI, with a preview of which ones answer a question. |
| Laptop-less Debating | Round-day setup for debating off just a phone: recommended gear and a step-by-step prep, flow, and speaking guide. |

Open it from **Coach Workspace** in the sidebar's **Practice Debates** section.

## Sample data

**Load sample data**, at the top of the page, ==fills the Coach Workspace with
a demo built from real cards== in the card database: ==opponent team and judge
scouting records== with their profiles, and ==a sample round flowed from those
cards== (aff cards as 1AC arguments, neg cards answering them). The sample
round is selected while the page is open, so ==every "Generate … for current
round" action works on it==.

==The sample stays in this browser and is never saved to your account==. It is
added beside your own records and never replaces one, and **Clear sample
data** ==removes only the sample==. The sample round is never saved with your
flows: ==it leaves the round workspace when you leave the page==.

## Tabs and links

Each tool used to have its own page. ==Those old links still work and open
the matching tab==. ==Your coaching sessions, programs, calendar items and coach
materials carry over unchanged and keep syncing to your account==.

==The page remembers the tab you had open==, and the address bar updates as you
switch tabs, so you can bookmark or share a link straight to one tool. The
Coach Workspace tab ==keeps its own stage strip== (Flow, Coaching, Prep,
Scouting, Practice).

## Good to know

  Coaching Programs, the Team Calendar and Coach Materials start empty.
