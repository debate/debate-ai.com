# Features Page

==One page that outlines every user-facing surface in the app==: each
feature's name, a one-line description, its route, and a link to its
long-form doc, ==grouped into eight categories and filtered by a search box==.

Find it at **/practice/features**.

## Why this exists

==Nothing else in the app lists everything it does== — the dock's Settings menu
is a flat list of forty-odd items, and the Research and Coaching workspaces
each cover only their own tools. ==This page is the map==.

## What it shows

The project's banner and badges, a count of features, ==a strip of the five
workspace screenshots== (CARDS, FIAT, LEARN, STREAM, REASON) each linking to its
page, the CARDS overview and seven-point vision, a **Downloads**
section under the badges (==the Chrome extension install button==, which used to
sit on the card search's empty state), a search box (matching each feature's name, description, address and
keywords), and a tab bar of the feature categories (Core Workspaces, Evidence &
Research, Team Collaboration, Flowing & Round Analysis, Pre-Round
Intelligence, Practice & Coaching, Recognition & Progress, Documents), each tab showing
its feature count. ==One category is shown at a time==: the first tab is
selected on load, ==clicking a tab shows only that category's feature cards==,
and a link to a category ==opens on that tab==. ==Searching narrows every tab's count==, and if the selected tab has no
matches the first tab that does is shown. ==Each feature card links to its
route and docs page==.

The **Documents** tab ==groups the document
surfaces== (Reason Docs, the Reason Editor and Speech Documents) and, under
their cards, ==embeds the shared Google Drive folder of PDF guides and
handouts==, with an **Open in Google Drive** link.

Under the badges, **Latest videos** ==scrolls the ten newest videos in the
library==. Each one ==opens that video's page in the site's video library (not
YouTube)==, and ==a round is named the way its page is titled== — year,
tournament, level and round, then the teams with their ranks and arguments
when known (`2026 NDT College Octas: Kansas #14 OW (K) vs CSULB #7 MO
(Policy)`) — rather than by its YouTube upload title. ==A lecture keeps its own
title==. **Popular cards this week** follows.

==Pointing at a card lights it up== — a gradient that follows the cursor, a
glowing border and a coloured title — in a shade of its own, so ==neighbouring
cards never come up the same colour==.

## Good to know

==The list is kept by hand==, so ==a brand-new page can take a little while to
appear on it==.
