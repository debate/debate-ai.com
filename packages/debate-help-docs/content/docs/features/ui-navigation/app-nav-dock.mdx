# App nav dock and tool sidebar

==The macOS-style icon dock that carries the app's main destinations==, and
==the sidebar it sits in on nearly every page==.

## The dock

==Five destinations== — Videos, Shared, Debate, Practice vs AI, Research — ==plus a
gear-icon Settings menu==, with **Alt+1** to **Alt+5** ==as shortcuts in dock
order==. ==Hovering an icon (or tabbing to it) shows its name==.

==The dock has two menus==, and either can be open on its own:

- **Practice vs AI opens the site menu instead of a page.** It starts with the
  Command Palette, then a submenu for each sidebar section — **Videos, Research,
  Practice Debates, Insights** — with the same links the sidebar has. ==The
  Practice vs AI page itself is the first link under Practice==, and **Alt+4**
  ==still jumps straight there==. On a phone, ==this menu is how you reach every
  sidebar link==.
- **The gear opens Settings**: Settings, the light/dark toggle and Theme
  submenu, **Hide Dock**, a Site Links submenu (Features, Docs, GitHub, Support,
  Privacy, Reddit and the other site links), and your account.

==Tools aren't dock icons==: they're in the sidebar's Research, Practice Debates
and Insights sections, and the full catalog is the **Features** row in Site
Links.

### Hiding the dock

Choose **Hide Dock** in the gear menu and ==the row of icons folds away to a small
arrow tab==: hanging from the top-left corner or the top of the sidebar, or
rising from the bottom of the screen on a phone. Hover the tab (or Tab to it, or
tap it) and ==the full dock slides back; move away and it folds up again==. ==It
stays out while one of its menus is open==, and the same menu's **Show Dock** puts
it back for good. ==The choice holds on every page and is remembered in this
browser==.

## The sidebar

==Every page draws the same sidebar==: the dock, then **Videos** and the Research,
Practice Debates and Insights sections, then a **Docs** section listing every
help page, then the site links on one compact line. In these docs, ==the docs'
own page list is a section of the sidebar== too. A few pages add their own
panels to it, such as the document panels in the card search and Debate Editor.

- **One section open at a time.** ==Opening a section closes the one that was
  open==, and ==the section for the page you're on opens by itself==. Clicking a
  section heading only opens or closes it; ==each section's main tool is its first
  link==. You can still open another section by hand.
- **Only the page you're on is highlighted**, and ==its row scrolls into view== if
  it's off screen. Team Rankings stays highlighted while you're reading a team or
  school page.
- **Every tool has its own small grey icon**, so ==a long section can be scanned
  by shape==.
- **Icons that move.** ==On by default==; turn off **Animate sidebar icons** in
  Settings → General to stop it. Hovering or tabbing to a row ==makes its icon
  wiggle, bounce, pulse==, spin and so on, ==a different one each time==, at most
  once every three seconds. If your system asks for reduced motion, ==the icon
  only changes colour==.

### The bottom of the sidebar

==The account menu is pinned at the bottom==, with a **Settings** button and the
**hide button** beside it. ==Settings opens the tab that fits the page you're on==.
The row shows your picture and name, ==never your email address==, with a badge
==counting unread notifications==. The account menu holds Settings, a
**Notifications** submenu (open a notification, clear it, or **Clear all**), a
**Switch account** submenu (see [Switching accounts](#switching-accounts)), an
**Organizations** submenu (see
[Contacts](/docs/features/team-collaboration/contacts#organizations)), a **Theme**
submenu (==synced to your account==), the site links, and Sign out. ==Signed out,
the row is a Sign in button== that ==opens the sign-in dialog without leaving the
page==.

**Docs** and **Settings** ==are always just above the account row==, on every
page, ==so you never have to open a menu to reach them==.

### Switching accounts

==You can stay signed in to up to five accounts in one browser== — say, your own
and a team or coaching account — ==and switch between them without signing
out==. Open the account menu and choose **Switch account**:

- ==Pick an account to switch to it==. The page reloads as that account.
- **Add another account** ==opens the sign-in dialog while keeping you signed in
  to the current one==. After you sign in, the new account is the active one.
- The **X** beside an account ==signs just that account out==. **Sign out of**
  *name* ==signs out the account you're using and switches to another==.

==With more than one account signed in, the menu's last item reads== **Sign out
of all accounts**, ==which signs every account on this browser out==.

### Resizing the sidebar

==Drag the thin line on the sidebar's right edge to make it wider or narrower==.
==The width is remembered in this browser== and ==every page opens the sidebar at
that width==. ==Double-click the line to reset it==. The line also takes keyboard
focus, and ==the arrow keys resize it==.

### Hiding the sidebar

==Hide the sidebar with the panel button at its bottom-right==, with **Ctrl+B**
(**⌘B** on a Mac), or by dragging its edge most of the way in. ==The page then
takes the full width==, and ==a small tab on the left edge brings the sidebar back==,
as do the same shortcut and dragging the edge out. ==The choice holds on every page
and is remembered in this browser==. While you're typing in an editor or a text
box, Ctrl/⌘+B stays **bold** ==and doesn't touch the sidebar==.

**Peeking.** ==While the sidebar is hidden, move the pointer to the very left edge
of the window== and ==it slides out *over* the page== without pushing it aside. Move
away (or press Esc) and it slides back; ==pin it with the panel button==, which
reads **Keep sidebar open** while peeking. Each time it comes or goes it ==plays
one of ten animations at random==, ==none if your system asks for reduced motion==.

==While the sidebar is hidden the dock floats at the top-left==, ==so the main
destinations stay one click away==.

**Pages with their own sidebar.** The flow workspace and the research docs ==have
a sidebar of their own==, so there ==the app sidebar starts hidden==. ==It still
peeks from the left edge==.

## On a phone

==The dock is a bar along the bottom of the screen==, starting with a **Sidebar**
button that ==opens a drawer from the left==. What the drawer shows depends on the
page: the sidebar sections on most pages, the document panels in the card search
and Debate Editor, or the flow workspace's own flows and rounds. ==The drawer
closes when you go to another page==.

## Video library

- **All Videos** ==is the first row under **Videos**, lists every round and lecture,
  and is the home page==. **Lectures** is a single row at the end of the section;
  ==the lecture categories are a bar of cards at the top of the lectures page==
  (see [Lecture categories](/docs/features/video-library/video-library#lecture-categories)).
- ==Only the collection you're browsing is highlighted== (All Videos, Lectures,
  College Policy, HS Policy, LD, Public Forum, Greatest of All-Time, My
  Favorites, Watch History).
- **Search floats over the results.** ==A small magnifier in the top-right corner
  of the results== opens the search box, filters, sort and grid/rows toggle over
  the results. ==It stays open while you're typing or a search is active==, closes
  on Escape or a click outside, and ==keeps what you typed==.
- **Results open as rows** — ==about four times as many per screen as the
  thumbnail grid==, which is one toggle away in the search panel.

## Moving between pages

==Switching pages keeps the dock, the sidebar and the video player in place==, so
==a lecture keeps playing as you move around==. The dock ==loads its destinations
ahead of time== so a switch is quick (but not on a data-saver or 2G connection).
==A slim bar across the top of the window shows a page switch in progress==, like
YouTube's; ==switches that finish almost at once don't show it==. The
[Features page](/docs/features/ui-navigation/features-page) opens inside the app
like any other page. ==These docs are a separate site==, so links into them load a
new page, and ==outside sites open in a new tab==.

## Good to know

- The flow workspace and the research docs ==still have their own sidebars
  instead of the shared one==.
- ==On a pen or touch screen that reports hover, a hidden dock opens on a tap==, and
  folds again on the next page or a second tap on the tab.
- ==In these docs the search box is in the header==, and with the sidebar hidden
  ==the docs have no dock==.
