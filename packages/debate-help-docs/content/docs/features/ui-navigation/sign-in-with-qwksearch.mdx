# Sign in with QwkSearch

Links your Debate AI account to your [QwkSearch](https://qwksearch.com)
account, so the research chat built into Debate AI runs as you on QwkSearch —
your saved chats, your history, and the models your QwkSearch plan includes —
instead of as a guest.

- **Nav:** the research workspace's **Settings → Account** tab (also the
  **Login** item in its menu)
- **Package:** `debate-webview` (`components/qwksearch/connect-auth.ts`,
  `Settings/Sections/Account.tsx`); routes in `apps/debate-ai.com`
  (`/api/qwksearch/*`)

## Signing in

1. Sign in to Debate AI (you'll be asked first if you aren't).
2. Press **Sign in with QwkSearch**. You go to qwksearch.com, sign in or create
   a QwkSearch account there, and press **Allow** to let Debate AI use it.
3. You land back where you started, with a "Signed in with QwkSearch" notice.

Cancelling on qwksearch.com brings you back unchanged.

## Your plan and upgrading

The Account tab shows your QwkSearch plan. On the free plan, **Upgrade
QwkSearch** opens QwkSearch's checkout for your QwkSearch account; when you
come back, the plan refreshes on its own (or press **Refresh plan**).

## Signing out

**Sign out of QwkSearch** unlinks the account: research goes back to guest mode
here. Your QwkSearch account and its chats are untouched. To cut Debate AI off
from QwkSearch's side, regenerate your API key in QwkSearch's settings — the
link stops working and you'll be asked to sign in again.
