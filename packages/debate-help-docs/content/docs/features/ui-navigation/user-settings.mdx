# User Settings

**Settings** ==is the Debate Editor's settings page==, and ==a signed-in user's
choices there follow them across devices instead of staying in one browser==.
The research agent's settings (account, language models, connectors, skills and
memory, search sources, search settings, cloud storage, rewrite modes, voice)
==are tabs of the same page==, ==listed after the editor's under a **Research
agent** heading==. Old links to the separate research settings pages ==open the
matching tab==.

## What it shows

==A searchable sidebar in three blocks==:

- **General** — ==your account and display preferences== (plan, debate style,
  font, colour theme, light/dark, tool data sync), your own AI key and sidebar
  icon animations; then **My team** (==the ranked team you represent in each
  division==, plus your coaching roster) and **My school** (==claim to coach or run
  a school==, and the schools you manage).
- **Sound & speech** — ==UI sound effects, the read-aloud voice and the
  on-device speech models==.
- **Privacy** — see below.
- Under an **Editor** heading: **Editor prefs** (workspace and Arrange Windows,
  navigation, layout on this device, editor behavior, the personal dictionary,
  word counts, backup, the performance benchmark and version info), **Files**,
  **Appearance**, **Accessibility**, **Editing**, **Keyboard**, **Comments &
  AI** and **Collaboration** — ==the same rows the editor's gear-icon settings
  show==.
- Under a **Research agent** heading: the research agent's sections.

==The page fills the whole window==. The sidebar's top has the **Settings** title
and a **Back** link, and its bottom has the site links (**Docs**, **Github**,
**Support**, **Privacy**, **Reddit**).

==Appearance and Accessibility appear *only* here==. ==Every other editor tab is the
same settings as the editor's gear-icon menu==, so ==a change here shows up in the
editor and the other way round==.

## Font family

The **Font family** picker on the General tab ==applies the moment you pick it==
and, when you are signed in, ==is also saved to your account==, so ==the same font
follows you to another device==. ==Signing in on a device with a different font
adopts the account's choice==; if the account has none yet, this device's font is
saved to it. ==Signed out, it stays on this browser only==.

==Every colour theme carries its own typeface==, and **Theme Default** (the first
option) uses it. ==Picking a colour theme resets the font to Theme Default==, so
switching theme switches the font too, in light and dark mode alike. Pick a
specific font afterwards to override the theme's again.

## What moved elsewhere

- **Favorite tools** — ==star a tool from its own page (the star at the end of its
  header) or the command palette==.
- **Word limit presets** — ==edit them on the Word Count page==, in the
  collapsible "Manage word limit presets" section.
- **Flow editor preferences** — ==open them with Cmd/Ctrl+, inside a flow==.
- **Default format for new speech documents** — ==a new speech document is always
  created as `.cmir`==, Debate Editor's own format, ==so autosave always applies to
  it==. ==Save As still exports a `.docx`== for teammates on Verbatim.

## Your own AI key

The **General** tab has a **Your own AI key** box. Paste an
[OpenRouter key](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) (`sk-or-…`) or an
Anthropic key (`sk-ant-…`) and the AI features — card AI analysis, AI coach and
judge, citation and OCR fixes, and the rest — ==run on your key instead of Debate
AI's shared one==. ==You set your own spending limit on that key== with OpenRouter
(or Anthropic), and ==Debate AI's daily AI request and card-analysis limits stop
applying to you==. That includes ==Suggest questions and reading mode's Ask in
these docs==. When you do hit a daily AI limit, ==the error message points at this
box==.

==The same box appears==:

- ==in the pricing dialog when you hit a daily AI limit, as an alternative to
  upgrading==, and
- in an **AI limit reached** dialog ==when Debate AI's shared AI itself has run out
  of credit==. ==That dialog offers the plans to upgrade first==, with bringing your
  own key as the other option. If the plans can't be loaded, ==only the key option
  shows==.

==The key is kept in this browser only — never in your account== — and is only
sent to Debate AI's own AI features. ==It's used for that one request and never
stored or logged==. ==Remove it to go back to the shared AI==.

## MCP server & API keys

The **MCP server & API** tab ==shows the address of Debate AI's hosted MCP
server==, links its [setup guide](/docs/features/ui-navigation/mcp-server), and
==lets you create, show, copy, reset and revoke the API keys an AI tool uses to act
as you==. ==A new key comes with a ready-to-paste setup snippet==; **Show** reveals
any listed key again, and **Reset** (after a confirmation) ==swaps in a new
secret==.

## What follows your account

When you're signed in, these follow you to your other devices:

- **Flow editor preferences** (Cmd/Ctrl+, in a flow): flow font, default grid
  zoom, the editing toggles (insert-paste, append-edit, scroll-zoom, align
  speeches, tooltips, Vim in the RFD pane), Debate Editor options, light/dark
  theme, side colours, and your custom flow editor shortcuts. ==What stays on the
  device==: the flows folder, whether the sidebar and RFD pane are open, and the
  live-collaboration switches, display name and contacts. ==A shortcut rebound on
  another device applies the next time the flow editor loads==.
- **Sound effects** (**Sound & speech → Sound effects**: All effects, Minimal or
  Off) ==applies the moment you pick it==. Going back to **All effects** on one
  device ==replaces a quieter choice made on another==.
- **Pinned debates** on the start screen's **Featured** section. A pin ==shows once
  its round is on that device==. ==Pins are capped at 12==, and a pin removed on one
  device while another is offline ==can come back== until that device syncs.
- **Debater level and XP**: your XP, level, daily challenge progress and recent
  XP log. ==XP earned on two devices while apart adds up==, as do today's
  per-activity counts. ==A daily challenge only reached by the combined count is
  paid the next time a tab loads or regains focus==, once. Rarely, two devices
  settling different challenges before they sync can lose one bonus; ==XP is never
  counted twice==.
- **The welcome dialog** ==appears the first time an account signs in, and never
  again on any device==. If your account can't be reached when you sign in, ==it
  stays hidden rather than risk showing twice==.

==Passwords and API keys in the editor's settings never leave this browser==, and
==only settings that are actually shown on this site are synced==, so a
desktop-only setting can't overwrite your account's copy.

The Debater Level page's **Mock data** button ==previews the page with sample
debaters== (a novice, a JV and a varsity debater). ==Mock profiles are never saved
or synced==, and ==logging practice and resetting are off== until you choose **Exit
mock data**.

## Saving flows and rounds

### Saved-to-account marker on flow tabs

Each flow tab in the sidebar's **Open Tabs** group ==shows a small cloud icon once
that flow has been saved to your account== (from the Flow History dialog,
including bulk saves): ==a plain cloud means the account copy matches what you
see==, ==a crossed-out amber cloud means you've edited it since==. ==A tab with no icon
hasn't been saved yet==. ==The marker survives a reload==, and ==edits made elsewhere
never show as saved==.

==The same cloud icon appears on the round cards== of the debate start screen
(Featured and Recent) once the round itself has been saved, and ==turns amber when
the round is edited afterwards==. ==Saving a round's flows doesn't by itself mark
the round==.

### Flow auto-save

Once a flow has been saved to your account, ==further edits to it are saved
automatically about five seconds after you stop typing==, and the tab's cloud
icon flips back to saved. ==Archived flows are skipped==. ==Auto-save never
overwrites==: if the account holds a newer copy (saved from another device), ==the
flow stays amber and you resolve it from Flow History==. ==A failed save is retried
the next time the flow changes==.

==Choose how far auto-save goes== under **Flow auto-save** in Settings → General:

- **Flows already on my account** (default) — ==the behavior above==.
- **All flows** — ==also uploads flows you have never saved==. If your account
  already holds a flow with that id, ==it is treated as a conflict and left alone==.
- **Off** — ==nothing is saved automatically==; save from Flow History.

==The choice applies immediately in this browser, including in other open tabs==,
and when you're signed in ==it's adopted on your other devices the next time
Settings loads==. Turning auto-save off ==cancels any save still waiting==. (There
used to be a second, device-only checkbox; if you had unticked it, this browser
starts on **Off**, unless your account already has a choice saved.)

### Opening a saved item by link

In **Settings → General → Saved to your account**, each saved flow, round and
practice debate has an open link:

- **A flow** ==opens the debate page on that flow's tab==.
- **A round** ==opens the debate page with that round's flows in front== and
  ==selects its first flow==.
- **A practice debate** opens **Debate History** on Practice vs AI ==with that
  round expanded to its transcript==.

==If the item isn't on this browser yet, the link does nothing== rather than show an
error.

## Privacy

The **Privacy** tab ==is where you decide what Debate AI may publish of your
recordings and how you are named on them==.

- **Recording release.** A few paragraphs explain what publishing means. Until
  you pick **I agree**, ==nothing you record is published==; **Don't publish my
  recordings** ==is the default and withdraws an earlier agreement==. Agreeing
  ==records the date and the version of the wording you agreed to==, and ==if the
  wording changes you are asked again==.
- **What is published:** **Audio and video**, or **Audio only**, ==which leaves
  your camera out==.
- **How you are credited:** **Anonymous contributor** ==(the default, your name
  hidden)==, a **Username** you type in, or your **Real name**, either in full or
  as first name and last initial ("Ada L."). ==The tab previews how you will
  appear==.
- **Privacy policy:** a link to the combined Terms of Service and Privacy
  Policy. ==Its last section says school and university names, logos and marks
  belong to their owners and that Debate AI is not affiliated with or endorsed by
  the schools shown==.
- **Request deletion of your data:** ==ask for everything (including your
  account) or only some of it== (published recordings, saved speech audio, flows,
  documents, comments, account settings), with a box for suggested deletions
  such as links to specific recordings. ==Requests go to the site's admins==, and
  your past requests and their status are listed under the form. ==Nothing is
  deleted automatically==.

==The release text is a plain-language draft that hasn't had legal review yet==, and
==no feature publishes recordings yet==.

## Saved to your account

**Settings → General → Saved to your account** ==shows how many documents, flows,
rounds==, word-count rounds, practice debates against the AI, judge decisions,
sent speeches and drill sets ==are stored under your account==. Below them, a
**Tool data** list ==shows how many records each tool that syncs to your account
holds== (for example Flow Editor Shortcuts). ==Each label links to the tool that
opens that kind of work==. ==Signed out, the section asks you to sign in instead==.

For documents, flows, rounds, practice debates, word-count rounds and drill
sets, a **Manage** button ==lists those items newest first== (100 at a time, with
**Load more**), each with **Export** (==downloads that item as a JSON file==) and
**Delete** ==behind a confirmation==. Documents, flows and rounds also link to
where they open. A **search box** above the list ==finds items by title== (practice
debates match on topic or opponent name), ==reaching past the first page==. Items
saved without a title ("Untitled …") can't be found by title.

Drill sets and word-count rounds ==have no **Delete** here==: remove one with
**Clear** on the Drill Sets or Word Count page, which clears it on your device
and in your account.

**Export all as JSON** ==downloads everything at once==, not just the 100 items
shown per page.

**Restore from export…** ==reads either kind of file back in== and ==only ever adds==:
anything still in your account is listed as skipped, so restoring the same file
twice is harmless. It brings back documents, flows, rounds, ==practice debates
against the AI==, word-count rounds and drill sets. A restored practice debate is
back in Practice vs AI history with its transcript and verdict, without adding
to your score, badges or streak again. Restored word-count rounds and drill sets
appear the next time you open their pages.

### Account sync status

Right under **Saved to your account**, an **Account sync** box ==says whether every
tool's data has synced to your account==: "Syncing your tools…" while it runs,
then "Every tool's data is synced", or ==a list of the tools that couldn't sync,
each linking to that tool, with the reason== (for example a record that's too
large). **Sync now** ==runs it again straight away==. Signed out, the box is hidden
— unless you turned off the sign-in reminders, in which case it ==offers to turn
them back on==.

**Where each tool saves**, collapsed in the same box, ==lists every tool that keeps
data of its own, grouped like the sidebar==, with where it saves and a status:
**On this device** while signed out, then **Syncing…**, **Synced** or
**Couldn't sync** (hover for the reason), or **Saved to account** for tools that
save straight to your account. Each name opens its tool, and a filter box narrows
the list. Tools that only show other data, such as Rankings, aren't listed.

==Tools save to your account in the background, with no per-tool badge or Save
button==. The Debate Editor status line ==only appears when a save fails==
("Couldn't save — retrying") or a read-only public Topic Starter is open.

## Good to know

- ==Judge decisions and sent speeches are counted but can't be listed one by one
  yet==.
- ==If the same setting is changed in two tabs at the same moment, the last change
  wins==. Lists such as favorite tools, recent tools, word-limit presets and
  outline filter presets ==keep both changes==.
