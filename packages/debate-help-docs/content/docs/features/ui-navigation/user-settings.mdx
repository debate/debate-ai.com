# User Settings

**Settings** ==is the Debate Editor's settings page==, and ==a signed-in user's
choices there follow them across devices instead of staying in one browser==.
The research agent's settings (account, language models, connectors, skills and
memory, search sources, search settings, cloud storage, rewrite modes, voice)
==are tabs of the same page==, ==listed after the editor's under a **Research
agent** heading==. Old links to the separate research settings pages ==open the
matching tab==.

## What it shows

==A searchable sidebar in three blocks==:

- **General** — ==your account and display preferences== (`debate-round`'s
  `UserSettingsPanel`: plan, debate style, font, colour theme, light/dark,
  tool data sync) and sidebar icon animations; then **My
  team** (==the ranked team you represent in each division==, plus your coaching
  roster) and **My school** (==claim to coach or run a school==, and the schools
  you manage). The old `?category=my-team` and `?category=my-school` links
  ==open this tab scrolled to that block==.
- **Debate Account** (`?category=account`) — ==passkeys, two-factor
  authentication, your API keys and the MCP server, and your own AI key==. See
  below. The old `?category=mcp` link ==opens this tab at the API keys==.
- **Sound & speech** — ==UI sound effects, the read-aloud voice and the
  on-device speech models==.
- **Privacy** — see below.
- Under an **Editor** heading: **Editor prefs** (workspace and Arrange Windows,
  navigation, layout on this device, editor behavior, the personal dictionary,
  word counts, backup, the performance benchmark and version info), **Files**,
  **Appearance**, **Accessibility**, **Editing**, **Keyboard**, **Comments &
  AI** and **Collaboration** — ==the same rows the editor's gear-icon settings
  show==.
- Under a **Research agent** heading: the research agent's sections.

==The page fills the whole window==. The sidebar's top has the **Settings** title
and a **Back** link, and its bottom has the site links (**Docs**, **Github**,
**Support**, **Privacy**, **Reddit**).

==Appearance and Accessibility appear *only* here==. ==Every other editor tab is the
same settings as the editor's gear-icon menu==, so ==a change here shows up in the
editor and the other way round==.

## Font family

The **Font family** picker on the General tab ==applies the moment you pick it==
and, when you are signed in, ==is also saved to your account==, so ==the same font
follows you to another device==. ==Signing in on a device with a different font
adopts the account's choice==; if the account has none yet, this device's font is
saved to it. ==Signed out, it stays on this browser only==.

==Every colour theme carries its own typeface==, and **Theme Default** (the first
option) uses it. ==Picking a colour theme resets the font to Theme Default==, so
switching theme switches the font too, in light and dark mode alike. Pick a
specific font afterwards to override the theme's again.

## What moved elsewhere

- **Favorite tools** — ==star a tool from its own page (the star at the end of its
  header) or the command palette==.
- **Word limit presets** — ==edit them on the Word Count page==, in the
  collapsible "Manage word limit presets" section.
- **Flow editor preferences** — ==open them with Cmd/Ctrl+, inside a flow==.
- **Default format for new speech documents** — ==a new speech document is always
  created as `.cmir`==, Debate Editor's own format, ==so autosave always applies to
  it==. ==Save As still exports a `.docx`== for teammates on Verbatim.

## Your own AI key

The **Debate Account** tab has a **Your own AI key** box (it used to sit in
General). Paste an
[OpenRouter key](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) (`sk-or-…`) or an
Anthropic key (`sk-ant-…`) and the AI features — card AI analysis, AI coach and
judge, citation and OCR fixes, every caller of `/api/reason-ai` and
`/api/card-ai-analysis` — ==run on your key instead of Debate AI's shared one==.
==You set your own spending limit on that key== with OpenRouter (or Anthropic), and
==Debate AI's daily AI request and card-analysis limits stop applying to you==.
That includes ==Questions and reading mode's Ask in these docs==, which
send the key too. When you do hit a daily AI limit, ==the error message points
at this box==.

==The same box appears==:

- ==in the pricing dialog when you hit a daily AI limit, as an alternative to
  upgrading==, and
- in an **AI limit reached** dialog ==when Debate AI's shared AI itself has run out
  of credit==. ==That dialog offers the plans to upgrade first==, with bringing your
  own key as the other option. If the plans can't be loaded, ==only the key option
  shows==.

==The key is kept in this browser only — never in your account== — and is only
sent to Debate AI's own AI features. ==It's used for that one request and never
stored or logged==. ==Remove it to go back to the shared AI==.

## Passkeys

**Debate Account → Passkeys** (`/settings?category=passkeys`) ==lets you sign in
with your fingerprint, face, screen lock or a security key instead of an email
link==. Name the passkey and press **Add passkey**; your browser or password
manager does the rest. ==Each passkey is listed with when it was added and
whether it syncs across your devices==, and the bin button removes it. On the
sign-in page, **Sign in with a passkey** ==appears in every browser that
supports passkeys==.

- ==A passkey belongs to the site you made it on==: one made on debate-ai.com
  works on debate-ai.com and www.debate-ai.com, not on ebate.app.
- ==Adding one needs a sign-in from the last day==; if it says to sign in again,
  sign out and back in first.
- ==Not available in the browser extension's options page or the desktop
  app's window==, which can't create passkeys for the site.

## Two-factor authentication

**Debate Account → Two-factor authentication** (`/settings?category=two-factor`)
==adds a 6-digit code from an authenticator app== (Google Authenticator,
1Password, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator…) ==after you sign in with Google,
Discord, LinkedIn, an email link or One Tap==.

1. Press **Set up an authenticator app**.
2. ==Scan the QR code==, or type the key shown beside it.
3. ==Enter the code the app shows== and press **Verify and turn on**.
4. ==Save the backup codes== (Copy or Download). ==Each one signs you in once
   if you lose your phone==, and they aren't shown again.

==From then on a sign-in stops at a code page== (`/login/two-factor`) until you
enter a code or a backup code. Tick **Don't ask again on this device for 30
days** to skip it on a computer you trust. ==Passkey sign-in skips the code==,
since a passkey is already two factors. **New backup codes** replaces the old
set, and **Turn off** (after a confirmation) removes 2FA; ==both need a sign-in
from the last day==.

- **Packages:** `debate-webview`
  (`components/settings/sections/AccountSecuritySection.tsx`,
  `components/layout/auth/PasskeySignIn.tsx`,
  `components/layout/auth/TwoFactorPage.tsx`), `apps/debate-web`
  (`lib/auth/security-plugins.ts`)
- **Engineering note:** `packages/debate-help-docs/dev-docs/internals/account-security.mdx`

## MCP server & API keys

The **API & MCP server** block of **Debate Account** (`/settings?category=mcp`
still opens it) ==shows the address of
Debate AI's hosted MCP server==, links its [setup guide](/docs/features/ui-navigation/mcp-server),
and ==lets you create, show, copy, reset and revoke the API keys an AI tool uses
to act as you==. ==A new key comes with a ready-to-paste client config==; **Show**
reveals any listed key again, and **Reset** (after a confirmation) ==swaps in a
new secret==. See
[MCP server](/docs/features/ui-navigation/mcp-server) for every client's setup.

## What follows your account

When you're signed in, these follow you to your other devices:

- **Flow editor preferences** (Cmd/Ctrl+, in a flow): flow font, default grid
  zoom, the editing toggles (insert-paste, append-edit, scroll-zoom, align
  speeches, tooltips, Vim in the RFD pane), Debate Editor options, light/dark
  theme, side colours, and your custom flow editor shortcuts. ==What stays on the
  device==: the flows folder, whether the sidebar and RFD pane are open, and the
  live-collaboration switches, display name and contacts. ==A shortcut rebound on
  another device applies the next time the flow editor loads==.
- **Sound effects** (**Sound & speech → Sound effects**: All effects, Minimal or
  Off) ==applies the moment you pick it==. Going back to **All effects** on one
  device ==replaces a quieter choice made on another==.
- **Pinned debates** on the start screen's **Featured** section. A pin ==shows once
  its round is on that device==. ==Pins are capped at 12==, and a pin removed on one
  device while another is offline ==can come back== until that device syncs.
- **Debater level and XP**: your XP, level, daily challenge progress and recent
  XP log. ==XP earned on two devices while apart adds up==, as do today's
  per-activity counts. ==A daily challenge only reached by the combined count is
  paid the next time a tab loads or regains focus==, once. Rarely, two devices
  settling different challenges before they sync can lose one bonus; ==XP is never
  counted twice==.
- **The welcome dialog** ==appears the first time an account signs in, and never
  again on any device==. If your account can't be reached when you sign in, ==it
  stays hidden rather than risk showing twice==.

==Passwords and API keys in the editor's settings never leave this browser==, and
==only settings that are actually shown on this site are synced==, so a
desktop-only setting can't overwrite your account's copy.

The Debater Level page's **Mock data** button ==previews the page with sample
debaters== (a novice, a JV and a varsity debater). ==Mock profiles are never saved
or synced==, and ==logging practice and resetting are off== until you choose **Exit
mock data**.

## Saving flows and rounds

### Saved-to-account marker on flow tabs

Each flow tab in the sidebar's **Open Tabs** group ==shows a small cloud icon once
that flow has been saved to your account== (from the Flow History dialog,
including bulk saves): ==a plain cloud means the account copy matches what you
see==, ==a crossed-out amber cloud means you've edited it since==. ==A tab with no icon
hasn't been saved yet==. ==The marker survives a reload==, and ==edits made elsewhere
never show as saved==.

==The same cloud icon appears on the round cards== of the debate start screen
(Featured and Recent) once the round itself has been saved, and ==turns amber when
the round is edited afterwards==. ==Saving a round's flows doesn't by itself mark
the round==.

### Flow auto-save

Once a flow has been saved to your account, ==further edits to it are saved
automatically about five seconds after you stop typing==, and the tab's cloud
icon flips back to saved. ==Archived flows are skipped==. ==Auto-save never
overwrites==: if the account holds a newer copy (saved from another device), ==the
flow stays amber and you resolve it from Flow History==. ==A failed save is retried
the next time the flow changes==.

==Choose how far auto-save goes== under **Flow auto-save** in Settings → General:

- **Flows already on my account** (default) — ==the behavior above==.
- **All flows** — ==also uploads flows you have never saved==. If your account
  already holds a flow with that id, ==it is treated as a conflict and left alone==.
- **Off** — ==nothing is saved automatically==; save from Flow History.

==The choice applies immediately in this browser, including in other open tabs==,
and when you're signed in ==it's adopted on your other devices the next time
Settings loads==. Turning auto-save off ==cancels any save still waiting==. (There
used to be a second, device-only checkbox; if you had unticked it, this browser
starts on **Off**, unless your account already has a choice saved.)

### Opening a saved item by link

```
debate-editor/settings.ts (the store, SETTING_METADATA, SECRET_SETTING_KEYS)
debate-editor/settings-ui.ts (buildEmbeddedSettingsPanel)
components/settings/EditorSettingsPanel.tsx + lib/hooks/useEditorPreferencesSync.ts (hydrate, debounced push of changed keys)
  → GET/PUT /api/settings { editorPreferences }
lib/editor-preferences.ts (EDITOR_SETTINGS_TABS, EDITOR_PREFERENCE_KEYS, validation)
components/settings/debate-editor/DebateEditorSettingsPanel.tsx (the iframe wrapper, self-sizing)
```

==The local store stays authoritative==: ==the page hydrates from the account on
load== (a `401` just means signed out), and ==every change writes localStorage
immediately and mirrors to the account on a 600ms debounce==, sending only the
keys that differ from what the account holds. ==A failed mirror never undoes
the local change==.

==If the item isn't on this browser yet, the link does nothing== rather than show an
error.

## Privacy

The **Privacy** tab ==is where you decide what Debate AI may publish of your
recordings and how you are named on them==.

- **Recording release.** A few paragraphs explain what publishing means. Until
  you pick **I agree**, ==nothing you record is published==; **Don't publish my
  recordings** ==is the default and withdraws an earlier agreement==. Agreeing
  ==records the date and the version of the wording you agreed to==, and ==if the
  wording changes you are asked again==.
- **What is published:** **Audio and video**, or **Audio only**, ==which leaves
  your camera out==.
- **How you are credited:** **Anonymous contributor** ==(the default, your name
  hidden)==, a **Username** you type in, or your **Real name**, either in full or
  as first name and last initial ("Ada L."). ==The tab previews how you will
  appear==.
- **Privacy policy:** a link to the combined Terms of Service and Privacy
  Policy. ==Its last section says school and university names, logos and marks
  belong to their owners and that Debate AI is not affiliated with or endorsed by
  the schools shown==.
- **Request deletion of your data:** ==ask for everything (including your
  account) or only some of it== (published recordings, saved speech audio, flows,
  documents, comments, account settings), with a box for suggested deletions
  such as links to specific recordings. ==Requests go to the site's admins==, and
  your past requests and their status are listed under the form. ==Nothing is
  deleted automatically==.

==The release text is a plain-language draft that hasn't had legal review yet==, and
==no feature publishes recordings yet==.

## Saved to your account

**Settings → General → Saved to your account** ==shows how many documents, flows,
rounds==, word-count rounds, practice debates against the AI, judge decisions,
sent speeches and drill sets ==are stored under your account==. Below them, a
**Tool data** list ==shows how many records each tool that syncs to your account
holds== (for example Flow Editor Shortcuts). ==Each label links to the tool that
opens that kind of work==. ==Signed out, the section asks you to sign in instead==.

For documents, flows, rounds, practice debates, word-count rounds and drill
sets, a **Manage** button ==lists those items newest first== (100 at a time, with
**Load more**), each with **Export** (==downloads that item as a JSON file==) and
**Delete** ==behind a confirmation==. Documents, flows and rounds also link to
where they open. A **search box** above the list ==finds items by title== (practice
debates match on topic or opponent name), ==reaching past the first page==. Items
saved without a title ("Untitled …") can't be found by title.

For documents, flows, rounds, practice debates, word-count rounds, drill sets, judge decisions and sent speeches, a **Manage** button next to
the count ==lists those items newest first== (title only, 100 at a time, with a **Load more** button when there are further items) with an **Export**
link (==downloads that one item's full content as a JSON file==) and a **Delete**
==action behind a confirmation==. Documents, flows and rounds also link to where they open. A **search box** above the
list ==finds items by title== (case-insensitive "contains"; practice debates match on
topic or opponent name); press **Search** or Enter to run it, and clear the box to list
everything again. The search runs on the server, so it ==reaches items past the first
page==, and **Load more** keeps the search. Items saved without a title (shown as
"Untitled …") have no stored title to match. This is backed by
`GET` (`?offset=<n>&limit=<n>` pages the list via `listOwnSavedItemsPage`, returning `hasMore`; `?q=<text>` filters by title through `titleContains`, which matches `%`, `_` and backslashes literally and is capped at 100 characters; `?id=<n>` exports one row via `exportOwnSavedItem`) / `DELETE`
`/api/settings/saved-data/[kind]` (`saved-data-items.ts`), ==which only ever touches rows owned by the signed-in user==.

**Export all as JSON** ==downloads everything at once==, not just the 100 items
shown per page.

Word-count rounds work the same way: listed as "Word-count round · N speeches",
linking to `/word-count`, with **Export** but ==no **Delete** here== — remove one
with **Clear** on the Word Count page. `useWordCountRounds` pushes every local
round the account lacks back up, so `wordCountRounds` is in `ITEM_KINDS` but not
`DELETABLE_ITEM_KINDS` (405 on `DELETE`). Their title is derived from the stored
JSON, so search filters the caller's rows in memory rather than in SQL. Rows are
keyed by SQL row `id`; the export carries the round's own id as `roundId`.

Judge decisions and sent speeches are read-only here too. A judge decision is
listed by who won and under which judge, such as "Aff wins · Tabula Rasa", and
links to the AI Judge Decision page. A sent speech is listed by the first line
of what you sent (up to 80 characters) and links to Speech Documents. Both have
**Export** but ==no **Delete** here==: clear a decision with **Clear** on the AI
Judge Decision page, and a sent speech with the trash button or **Clear
history** on Speech Documents. Engineering note: `judgeDecisions` and `speeches`
are in `ITEM_KINDS` but not `DELETABLE_ITEM_KINDS` (405 on `DELETE`), because
`useJudgeDecisions` and `useSpeechSendLogSync` push every local entry the
account lacks back up. Titles come from the stored JSON (`judgeDecisionTitle`,
`speechSendTitle`), so a search filters the caller's rows in memory; without a
search the page is read with SQL `LIMIT` / `OFFSET`. Rows are keyed by SQL row
`id`; a decision's export carries its `roundId`, and both carry the full record
as `data`.

An **Export all as JSON** link under the counts ==downloads every kind at once==
(`GET /api/settings/saved-data?export=all` → `exportAllOwnSavedData`): one file with
`docs`, `flows`, `rounds`, `practiceRounds`, `wordCountRounds`, `drills`, `judgeDecisions` and `speeches` arrays holding each item's full content.
The export reads every row (a page of 100 per query), so ==it is not limited to the
100 items shown per== **Manage** page. ==It also carries your **Tool data**== —
pinned debates, opponent and judge profiles, flow editor shortcuts and every
other tool that syncs to your account — under `toolRecords`, one list per tool,
each record exactly as the tool saved it. Engineering note: `exportOwnToolRecords`
(`saved-data-items.ts`) reads `saved_tool_records` by row id, 500 rows per
query, up to 5,000 records (`toolRecordsTruncated` says when more were left),
and leaves out keys no longer in `TOOL_RECORD_COLLECTIONS`. A File Sources
record holds only what `redactFileSource` let through, so no storage password
or access token is in the file.

**Restore from export…** ==reads either file back in== — one item's **Export** or
**Export all as JSON** — and ==only ever adds==: anything still in your account is
listed as skipped, so restoring the same file twice is harmless. It brings back
documents, flows, rounds, ==practice debates against the AI==, word-count rounds,
drill sets, judge decisions, sent speeches ==and tool data==. A restored practice debate is back in **Practice vs AI** history
with its transcript and verdict; it doesn't add to your score, badges or streak
again. Restored word-count rounds, drill sets, judge decisions and sent speeches
appear on the Word Count, Drill Sets, AI Judge Decision and Speech Documents
pages the next time you open them, because those pages adopt anything your
account has and this device doesn't. Engineering note: `POST /api/settings/saved-data` →
`restoreOwnSavedData` (`apps/debate-web/lib/saved-data/saved-data-restore.ts`);
word-count rounds and drill sets go through `isValidWordCountRoundRecord` /
`isValidDrillSetRecord` and the same size caps as their own `PUT` routes, keyed
by the record's `roundId`. Judge decisions and sent speeches go through
`isValidJudgeDecisionRecord` / `isValidSpeechSendLogEntry` and the size caps of
`PUT /api/judge-decisions/[decisionId]` / `PUT /api/speech-send-log/[entryId]`,
keyed by the record's own `id`. Practice debates go through `isValidDebateVsBotRecord`
from `@debate-ai/practice-vs-ai` (see the Practice vs AI internals note).
Judge decisions and sent speeches go through `isValidJudgeDecisionRecord` /
`isValidSpeechSendLogEntry` and the same size caps as
`PUT /api/judge-decisions/[decisionId]` and `PUT /api/speech-send-log/[entryId]`,
keyed by their own `id`; one whose id the account already has is skipped.

Restored tool data ==syncs to the device you restored on right away==: the
restore starts the same merge as **Sync now**, so a restored pin or opponent
profile shows up in its tool without a reload. Other devices pick it up the next
time they sync. Skipped tool records are summed into one line per tool and
reason (for example "Pinned Debates · 3 records — Already in your account.")
rather than listed one by one. Engineering note: `restoreOwnToolRecords`
(`saved-data-restore.ts`) runs each record through the collection's `redact`,
`isSyncableToolRecord` and the `MAX_TOOL_RECORD_BYTES` cap, the same checks
`PUT /api/tool-records/[collection]` applies; a collection no longer in the
allowlist is skipped whole. One `SELECT` finds the ids the account already
holds and the inserts go out through `db.batch()` (`onConflictDoNothing`), so a
large restore spends a handful of D1 queries. The response adds
`restoredToolRecords`; `RestoreSavedData` then calls `resync()` from
`lib/tools/tool-sync-store`.

Known gaps: ~~judge decisions and sent speeches are counted but can't be listed one by one yet~~
— **fixed**: both are listed, exported and restored like
word-count rounds (read-only here). ==There is deliberately no per-tool "saving" badge==: ==background auto-sync is the model==, and ==the REASON editor status line only reports a failed save==.

### Account sync status

Right under **Saved to your account**, an **Account sync** box ==says whether
every tool's data has synced to your account==: "Syncing your tools…" while the
first merge of the tab runs, then ==how many tools are synced== ("All *N* tools
that save data are synced to your account" — only tools that save something
count, not device-only ones), or ==a list of the tools that couldn't sync, each
linking to that tool, with the reason== (for example a record over the 200 KB
limit). **Sync now** ==re-runs the merge straight away==; while a merge runs it
reads **Syncing…** and ==can't be clicked again until the merge finishes==.
Under the status line, ==**Last synced 3 minutes ago** says when your tools last
finished syncing== in this tab (hover for the exact time). It counts only a sync
that reached your account cleanly, so after one that failed it keeps the time of
the last good one, and it is hidden while a sync is running. Signed out, the box is hidden — unless you turned off the
sign-in reminders, in which case it ==offers to turn them back on==.

**Where each tool saves**, collapsed in the same box, ==lists every tool that keeps
data of its own, grouped like the sidebar==, with where it saves and a status:
**On this device** while signed out, then **Syncing…**, **Synced** or
**Couldn't sync** (hover for the reason), or **Saved to account** for tools that
save straight to your account. Each name opens its tool, and a filter box narrows
the list. Tools that only show other data, such as Rankings, aren't listed.

Engineering note: `ToolSyncStatusPanel` (`debate-webview`), mounted by
`EditorSettingsPanel`; the tool list comes from `buildToolStorageOverview`
(`lib/tools/tool-storage-overview.ts`). It reads the app-wide sync state through
`useToolSyncSnapshot` rather than driving the sync itself. The **Last synced**
time is the `toolRecordSyncHydratedAt` session mark the merge already writes
(`readLastToolSyncAt` / `formatLastToolSync`, `lib/tools/tool-sync-last-merge.ts`),
re-read every 30 seconds.

## Known gaps

No optimistic-concurrency handling in general — ==two tabs editing the *same*
field at once has the last PUT win==. `favoriteTools` ==no longer has this
problem==: every code path that changes it (`toggleFavorite`/`removeFavorite`'s
single add/remove op, and `pruneUnknown`'s batch `removeFavoriteTools` op)
==now resolves against the account's row as it stands at write time==, not a
client-computed whole-list replace, so two tabs adding, removing or pruning
different entries at once ==can no longer drop each other's change==. A plain
whole-list `favoriteTools` replace is still accepted by the route for a
caller that genuinely needs one, but ==nothing in the app sends one anymore==.
`recentTools` (see Command Palette's
"Recent" section) ==joins it as an op-based field== — each tool visit sends a
single `recordRecentTool` op, resolved the same read-then-write way — since
==it writes far more often than any picker-style field here== (every tool open,
not just an explicit Save — ==opening a tool page by any route counts==, whether
from the palette, the sidebar, a bookmark or a link) and two tabs opening different tools in quick
succession would otherwise race the same way favorites used to.

`editorPreferences` is merged key by key on the server, and since 2026-11-15
==the page pushes only the settings that changed==: `useEditorPreferencesSync`
compares each rendered key with what this tab last read from or wrote to the
account (`changedEditorPreferences`) and sends just the differences. Before
that, every push carried every rendered key, so ==a second tab still showing an
old value overwrote every other setting when it changed one==. What is left is
the narrow case: ==two tabs changing the *same* setting end at whichever
debounce landed second==, and a setting the account doesn't hold yet is sent
the first time anything is pushed. A push that fails or is refused leaves the
tab's baseline alone, so the change goes out again with the next push. Every other
field (`debateStyle`, `colorTheme`, etc.) ==is a plain whole-value replace with
no such protection== — `debateStyle`/`fontSize`/`colorTheme`/`themeMode` are
written from `/settings`' General tab and the dock's Theme submenu, both of
which apply and (when signed in) sync a single field at a time rather than a
whole form Save, so ==this mostly shows up as "two tabs pick a different
Debate style within the same second."==

`wordLimitPresets` (`debate-round`'s `WordLimitPresetsPanel`, rendered from
`/word-count` rather than from this page — see "What it no longer shows"
above) ==is also op-based now==: `addPreset`/`updatePreset`/`removePreset` each
send a single `addWordLimitPreset`/`updateWordLimitPreset`/
`removeWordLimitPreset` op, resolved against the account's current list at
write time instead of a client-computed whole-list replace, mirroring
`favoriteTools`'s fix above. A duplicate name, an unknown preset, or an
invalid word limit ==is refused with a== `400` ==rather than silently corrupting
the stored list==. A plain whole-list `wordLimitPresets` replace is still
accepted by the route for a caller that genuinely needs one, but
`useWordLimitPresets.ts` no longer sends one.

`outlineFilterPresets` (`debate-practice-drills`'s named Outline filter
presets bar — see Argument Tree Outline)
==is op-based the same way==: `addPreset`/`removePreset` each send a single
`addOutlineFilterPreset`/`removeOutlineFilterPreset` op, resolved against
the account's current list at write time instead of a client-computed
whole-list replace, mirroring `wordLimitPresets`'s fix above. A duplicate
name, an at-capacity list, or an invalid filter is refused with a `400`
rather than silently corrupting the stored list. A plain whole-list
`outlineFilterPresets` replace is still accepted by the route for a caller
that genuinely needs one, but `useOutlineFilterPresets.ts` no longer sends
one.

## Pinned debates

The debates you pin to the start screen's **Featured** section ==follow your
account==. Each pin is a `{ roundId, pinnedAt }` record in the `pinnedDebates`
collection of the tool-record sync (`debate-search-evidence`'s
`TOOL_RECORD_COLLECTIONS`, stored in `saved_tool_records`), ==so it syncs like
every other tool store== and appears under **Settings → Account → Tool data**
as "Pinned Debates". A round's id is its `saved_rounds.client_id`, which a
cloud save keeps, so ==the pin names the same round on every device==; a pin whose
round isn't on this device yet ==is skipped until the round arrives==. Browsers
that stored pins as a bare list of ids ==are upgraded to records the first time
the list is read==.

Known gap: ==pins are capped at 12 per browser==, and the merge on sign-in is a
union, so ==a pin removed on one device while another is offline can reappear==
until that device syncs.

## Flow editor shortcuts

==Shortcuts you rebind in the flow editor sync to your account== as the
`flowKeymap` collection (listed in **Tool data** as "Flow Editor Shortcuts"),
==one record per rebound action==. They are stored under `ebb-keymap-settings` and
adapted by `flow-keymap-codec.ts` in `debate-search-evidence`.

==A rebinding made on another device applies in an open flow editor== as soon as
your account sync brings it in (on page load, when you come back to the tab, or
on **Sync now**), and ==a change made in another tab applies straight away== — no
reload needed. The same goes for the display preferences that follow your
account (`ebbSyncedSettings`, listed as "Flow Editor Settings": font, default
zoom, theme, colours and the editing toggles). ==Device-local display values==
in `ebb-display-settings`, such as the flows folder path and panel layout, ==are
not synced==.

Engineering note: `useFlowSettingsLiveUpdate` (`debate-flow`,
`src/lib/config/settingsLiveUpdate.ts`), mounted by `AccountSettingsSync`,
listens for `storage` events on `ebb-keymap-settings` and `ebbSyncedSettings` —
the synthetic one `writeLocalToolRecords` dispatches after a merge, or the
browser's own from another tab. The new value is validated with
`normalizeFlowEditorSettingsPatch` (the `/api/settings` rules; invalid fields
are dropped one by one) and only the fields that differ from the open editor
go to `applyAccountSettings`. A removed key or `localStorage.clear()` is
ignored rather than resetting the editor.

Known gap: a rebinding still reaches an open editor only when the sync runs
(see **Account sync status**); there is no push channel.

## Debater level and XP

==Your XP, level, daily challenge progress and recent XP log sync to your account==
as the `debaterLevel` collection (listed in **Tool data** under Practice as
"Debater Level & XP"), stored under `debaterLevel` as a single `progress`
record and adapted by `debater-level-codec.ts` in `debate-search-evidence`.

==Each browser keeps its own XP ledger== (`ledger` on the stored state, keyed by a
per-browser id kept under `debaterDeviceId`, which is not synced). ==A device only
ever grows its own entry==, so when two devices both earned XP while apart the
ledgers merge entry by entry and your total and lifetime counts are rebuilt as
the pre-ledger base plus every device — ==XP earned on both devices adds up==.
Each entry also carries a per-day tally (`daily`: a UTC `dayKey` plus counts), so
==today's per-activity counts add across devices the same way==.

==The rest of the state is still merged field by field== (`mergeDebaterLevel`): ==the
union of completed milestones==, today's challenge progress when both are from the
same UTC day (otherwise the later day), and the union of the recent XP log.
==A daily challenge whose target is only reached by the combined count is paid==
by `settleDailyChallenges` the next time the tab loads or regains focus (not at
the instant of the merge): the bonus event id is `settle:<day>:<challenge>` and
the XP goes to the ledger `base` (merged by max), so ==two devices settling the
same merged state pay it once==.
Known gaps: ==two devices settling different challenges before they sync can lose
one bonus== to the max-merge; entries written before per-day tallies existed, and copies from an older client without
a ledger, fall back to the larger side. ==That can under-count but never
double-counts==.

The Debater Level page's **Mock data** button ==previews the page with sample
debaters== (a novice, a JV and a varsity debater from
`lib/debater-mock-profiles.ts` in `debate-contributor-progress`), each with some
challenges, milestones and judge awards earned and others partway there. ==Mock
profiles are display-only==: ==they are never saved or synced==, and ==logging practice
and resetting are disabled== until you choose **Exit mock data**.

## Save status on each tool

==Tool data saves to your account automatically, with no badge== or **Save now**
button. The per-tool "Saving to your account…" badge and its **Save now**
button ==were removed from every tool header==, the `/debate` start screen and
speech controls bar, the video library, the `/research/cards` search
workspace and the `/research/docs` workspace. The REASON editor's status line
==now appears only when a save fails== ("Couldn't save — retrying") or a read-only
public topic starter is open. ==The auto-sync itself is unchanged==; **Tool data**
on `/tools` ==still shows sync failures and a retry==. Starring a tool as a
favorite ==uses the star at the end of the header row==.

The `startScreenActions` / `roundActions` props on `DebateFlowPage` and
`headerActionsSlot` on `LecturesPage` ==stay as generic host slots; nothing
passes them now==.
