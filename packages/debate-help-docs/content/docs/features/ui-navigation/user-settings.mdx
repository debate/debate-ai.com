# User Settings

**Settings** ==is the Debate Editor's settings page==, and ==a signed-in user's
choices there follow them across devices instead of staying in one browser==.

The **Research agent** settings (account, language models, connectors, skills
and memory, search sources, search settings, cloud storage, rewrite modes,
voice) ==are listed after the editor's under a== **Research agent** heading in
the same sidebar.

## What it shows

==A searchable sidebar in three blocks==:

- **General** — ==your account and display preferences== (plan, debate style,
  font, colour theme, light/dark, tool data sync), the AI coaches' remembered
  coaching styles and sidebar icon animations;
  then **My team** (==the ranked team you represent in each division==, plus
  your coaching roster) and **My school** (==claim to coach or run a school==,
  and the schools you manage).
- **Debate Account** — ==passkeys, two-factor authentication, your API keys and
  the MCP server, and your own AI key==. See below.
- **Sound & speech** — ==UI sound effects, the read-aloud voice and the
  on-device speech models==.
- **Privacy** — see below.
- Under an **Editor** heading: **Editor prefs** (workspace and Arrange Windows,
  navigation, layout on this device, editor behavior, the personal dictionary,
  word counts, backup, the performance benchmark and version info), **Files**,
  **Appearance**, **Accessibility**, **Editing**, **Keyboard**, **Comments &
  AI** and **Collaboration** — ==the same rows the editor's gear-icon menu
  shows==.
- Under a **Research agent** heading: the research agent's sections.

==The page fills the whole window==. The **Settings** title and a **Back** link
sit at the top of the sidebar, and the site's footer links (**Docs**,
**Github**, **Support**, **Privacy**, **Reddit**) sit at its bottom.

==Appearance and Accessibility appear *only* here==. ==Everything else is
shared with the editor==, so ==a change here shows up in the editor and the
other way round==.

## Font family

The **Font family** picker on the General tab ==applies the moment you pick it==
and, when you are signed in, ==is also saved to your account==, so ==the same
font follows you to another device==. ==Signing in on a device with a different
local font adopts the account's choice==; if the account has none yet, this
device's font is saved to it. ==Signed out, it stays on this browser only==.

==Every colour theme carries its own typeface==, and **Theme Default** (the
first option) uses it. ==Picking a colour theme resets the font to Theme
Default==, so switching theme switches the font too, in light and dark mode
alike. Pick a specific font afterwards to override the theme's again.

## What moved elsewhere

- **Favorite tools** — ==a tool is starred from its own page or the command
  palette==.
- **Tool data** — ==syncs in the background with no per-tool badge== (see
  "Save status on each tool" below).
- **Word limit presets** — ==now editable directly on the Word Count page==, in
  a collapsible "Manage word limit presets" section below the word-count form.
- **Ebb Flow preferences** — ==reachable with== `Cmd/Ctrl+,` inside a flow,
  where they apply.
- **Default format for new speech documents** — ==a new speech document is now
  always created in Debate Editor's own format==, ==so autosave always applies
  to it==. ==Save As still exports a== `.docx` for teammates on Verbatim.

## Your own AI key

The **Debate Account** tab has a **Your own AI key** box. Paste an
[OpenRouter key](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) (`sk-or-…`) or an
Anthropic key (`sk-ant-…`) and the AI features — card AI analysis, AI coach and
judge, citation and OCR fixes — ==run on your key instead of Debate AI's shared
one==. ==You set your own spending limit on that key== with OpenRouter (or
Anthropic), and ==Debate AI's daily AI request and card-analysis limits stop
applying to you==. That includes ==Questions and reading mode's Ask in these
docs==. When you do hit a daily AI limit, ==the error message points at this
box==.

==The same box appears==:

- ==in the pricing dialog when you hit a daily AI limit, as an alternative to
  upgrading==, and
- in an **AI limit reached** dialog ==when Debate AI's shared key itself has run
  out of credit==. ==That dialog offers the plans to upgrade your account
  first==, with bringing your own key as the other option.

==The key is kept in this browser only== — ==never in your account== — and is
sent only with your own AI requests. ==The server uses it for that one request
and never stores or logs it==. ==Remove it to go back to the shared key==.

## Passkeys

**Debate Account → Passkeys** ==lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face,
screen lock or a security key instead of an email link==. Name the passkey and
press **Add passkey**; your browser or password manager does the rest. ==Each
passkey is listed with when it was added and whether it syncs across your
devices==, and the bin button removes it. On the sign-in page, **Sign in with a
passkey** ==appears in every browser that supports passkeys==.

- ==A passkey belongs to the site you made it on==: one made on debate-ai.com
  works on debate-ai.com and www.debate-ai.com, not on ebate.app.
- ==Adding one needs a sign-in from the last day==; if it says to sign in again,
  sign out and back in first.
- ==Not available in the browser extension's options page or the desktop
  app's window==, which can't create passkeys for the site.

## Two-factor authentication

**Debate Account → Two-factor authentication** ==adds a 6-digit code from an
authenticator app== (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, Microsoft
Authenticator…) ==after you sign in with Google, Discord, LinkedIn, an email
link or One Tap==.

1. Press **Set up an authenticator app**.
2. ==Scan the QR code==, or type the key shown beside it.
3. ==Enter the code the app shows== and press **Verify and turn on**.
4. ==Save the backup codes== (Copy or Download). ==Each one signs you in once
   if you lose your phone==, and they aren't shown again.

==From then on a sign-in stops at a code page== until you enter a code or a
backup code. Tick **Don't ask again on this device for 30 days** to skip it on
a computer you trust. ==Passkey sign-in skips the code==, since a passkey is
already two factors. **New backup codes** replaces the old set, and **Turn
off** (after a confirmation) removes 2FA; ==both need a sign-in from the last
day==.

## MCP server & API keys

The **API & MCP server** block of **Debate Account** ==shows the address of
Debate AI's hosted MCP server==, links its [setup guide](/docs/features/ui-navigation/mcp-server),
and ==lets you create, show, copy, reset and revoke the API keys an AI tool uses
to act as you==. ==A new key comes with a ready-to-paste client config==; **Show**
reveals any listed key again, and **Reset** (after a confirmation) ==swaps in a
new secret==. See
[MCP server](/docs/features/ui-navigation/mcp-server) for every client's setup.

## Flow editor preferences follow your account

The Ebb flow editor's display preferences (`Cmd/Ctrl+,`) and keybinding
overrides ==sync to your account when you are signed in==. ==What follows you==:
flow font, default grid zoom, the editing toggles (insert-paste, append-edit,
scroll-zoom, align speeches, tooltips, Vim in the RFD pane), Debate Editor
options, light/dark theme, side colours, and every keymap override.

==What stays on the device it was set on==: the flows folder, whether the sidebar
and RFD pane are open, and the live-collaboration switches, display name and
contacts. ==A pull from the account never resets those==.

==A rebinding made on another device applies in an open flow editor== as soon as
your account syncs (on page load, when you come back to the tab, or on **Sync
now**), and ==a change made in another tab applies straight away== — no reload
needed.

## Sound effects follow your account

**Sound & speech → Sound effects** (All effects, Minimal or Off) ==applies the
moment you pick it== and, ==when you are signed in, follows you to your other
devices==. Picking **All effects** again is saved too, so ==going back to the
default on one device replaces a quieter choice made on another==. Signed out,
the level stays on this browser only.

## Coaching styles

**General → Coaching styles** ==lists every AI coach that remembers the style you
last picked== — **Get AI feedback** (AI Coach Mode), **Ask the coach** (Coach
Materials), and Practice vs AI's **Coach debrief** and **Judge feedback style**
— ==with the style each one starts on==. ==Pick another style for any of them
here==, or follow the link to the tool. **Reset all to Standard** ==puts every
coach back on Standard==. Signed in, ==the styles follow you to your other
devices==; signed out, they stay on this browser.

## Saved-to-account marker on flow tabs

Each flow tab in the sidebar's **Open Tabs** group ==shows a small cloud icon
once that flow has been saved to your account== from the Flow History dialog
(including bulk saves): ==a plain cloud means the account copy matches what you
see==, ==a crossed-out amber cloud means you've edited it since==. ==A tab with no icon
has not been saved yet==. ==The marker survives a reload==, and edits made
elsewhere (or by a different account on the same browser) ==never show as
saved==.

==The same cloud icon appears on the round cards== of the debate start screen
(Featured and Recent) once the round itself has been saved to your account from
the Flow History dialog, and ==flips to the amber crossed-out cloud when the round
is edited afterwards==. ==Saving a round's flows does not by itself mark the round==.

### Opening saved work by link

In **Settings → General → Saved to your account**, each saved flow, round and
practice debate has an open link:

- ==A flow link opens the debate page on that flow's tab==.
- ==A round link opens the debate page with that round's flows in front== and
  ==selects the round's first flow==.
- ==A practice debate link opens **Debate History** on Practice vs AI with that
  round already expanded to its transcript==.

If the flow or round isn't loaded in this browser yet, the link does nothing
rather than show an error.

### Auto-save for flows already on your account

Once a flow has been saved to your account, ==further edits to it are saved
automatically about five seconds after you stop typing==, and the tab's cloud
icon flips back to saved. ==Flows you have never saved are never uploaded
automatically==, and ==archived flows are skipped==. ==Auto-save never overwrites==: if
the account holds a newer copy (saved from another device) ==the flow stays
amber and you resolve it from Flow History==. ==A failed save is retried after
the flow changes again==.

==Choose how far auto-save goes== under **Flow auto-save** in Settings → General:

- **Flows already on my account** (default) — ==the behavior above==.
- **All flows** — ==also uploads flows you have never saved==. If your account
  already holds that flow, ==it is treated as a conflict and left alone==.
- **Off** — ==nothing is saved automatically==; save from Flow History.

==The choice applies immediately in this browser, including in other open
tabs==. When you are signed in it is also saved to your account and ==adopted
on your other devices the next time Settings loads==.

## How editor settings sync

==Your settings always save on this device first==: ==the page loads your
account's settings when it opens==, and ==every change is saved here
immediately and then copied to your account a moment later==. ==A failed copy
never undoes the change on this device==.

==API keys and other credentials you enter in the editor's settings stay in this
browser== and are never copied to your account.

## The welcome dialog shows once per account

The "Welcome to Debate AI" dialog of key links ==appears the first time an
account signs in, and never again on any device==. ==A second account signing
in on a browser that already showed it does not see it==.

## Privacy

The **Privacy** tab ==is where you decide what Debate AI may publish of your
recordings and how you are named on them==.

- **Recording release.** A few paragraphs explain what publishing means. Until
  you pick **I agree**, ==nothing you record is published==; **Don't publish my
  recordings** ==is the default and withdraws an earlier agreement==. Agreeing
  ==records the date and the version of the wording you agreed to==, and ==if the
  wording changes you are asked again==.
- **What is published:** **Audio and video**, or **Audio only**, ==which leaves
  your camera out==.
- **How you are credited:** **Anonymous contributor** ==(the default, your name
  hidden)==, a **Username** you type in, or your **Real name**, either in full
  or as first name and last initial ("Ada L."). ==The tab previews how you will
  appear==.
- **Privacy policy:** a link to the combined [Terms of Service and Privacy
  Policy](/legal/privacy). ==Its last section says school and university names,
  logos and marks belong to their owners and that Debate AI is not affiliated
  with or endorsed by the schools shown==.
- **Request deletion of your data:** ==ask for everything (including your
  account) or only some of it== (published recordings, saved speech audio,
  flows, documents, comments, account settings), with a box for suggested
  deletions such as links to specific recordings. ==Requests go to the site's
  admins==, and your past requests and their status are listed under the form.
  ==Nothing is deleted automatically==.

Known gaps: ==the release text is a plain-language placeholder that has not had
legal review==. ==No feature publishes user recordings yet==, so the settings take
effect as publishing features read them.

## Saved to your account

**Settings → General → Saved to your account** ==shows how many documents, flows,
rounds==, word-count rounds, practice debates against the AI, judge decisions, sent
speeches and drill sets ==are stored under your account==. Below them, a **Tool
data** list ==shows how many records each tool that syncs to your account holds==
(for example Flow Editor Shortcuts). ==Each label links to the tool that opens
that kind of work==. ==Signed out, the section tells you to sign in instead==.

For each kind, a **Manage** button next to the count ==lists those items newest
first== (by title, 100 at a time, with **Load more**), each with an **Export**
link (==downloads that one item as a JSON file==) and a **Delete** ==action
behind a confirmation==. Documents, flows and rounds also link to where they
open. A **search box** above the list ==finds items by title== (practice debates
match on topic or opponent name); press **Search** or Enter to run it, and
clear the box to list everything again. ==The search reaches every item, not just
the first page==. Items saved without a title (shown as "Untitled …") can't be
found by search.

Some kinds can't be deleted here, because the page that owns them would put them
back from your device. ==Remove them where they live instead==:

- **Drill sets** (listed as "Round N · K drills") — **Clear** on the Drill Sets
  page.
- **Word-count rounds** (listed as "Round 12 · N speeches") — **Clear** on the
  Word Count page.
- **Judge decisions** (listed as, say, "Aff wins · Tabula Rasa") — **Clear** on
  the AI Judge Decision page.
- **Sent speeches** (listed by their first line) — the trash button or **Clear
  history** on Speech Documents.

An **Export all as JSON** link under the counts ==downloads every kind at once==
in one file, ==not limited to the 100 items shown per== **Manage** page.

Each row under **Tool data** has its own **Export** link next to the count. It
==downloads just that tool's synced data== (say, your Pinned Debates or Flow
Editor Shortcuts) as a JSON file, which is handy for keeping or moving one
tool's data without the whole archive.

**Restore from export…** ==reads any of these files back in== — one item's
**Export**, one tool's **Export**, or **Export all as JSON** — and ==only ever adds==: anything still in your account is
listed as skipped, so restoring the same file twice is harmless. A restored
practice debate is back in **Practice vs AI** history with its transcript and
verdict; it doesn't add to your score, badges or streak again. Restored
word-count rounds, drill sets, judge decisions and sent speeches appear on
their own pages the next time you open them.

### Account sync status

Right under **Saved to your account**, an **Account sync** box ==says whether
every tool's data has synced to your account==: "Syncing your tools…" while the
first sync runs, then ==how many tools are synced==, or ==a list of the tools that
couldn't sync, each linking to that tool, with the reason== (for example a
record over the 200 KB limit). **Sync now** ==syncs again straight away==.
Signed out, the box is hidden — unless you turned off the sign-in reminders, in
which case it ==offers to turn them back on==.

### Where each tool saves

Open **Where each tool saves** in the same box to see ==every tool that keeps
data of its own, grouped like the sidebar==, with one line on where it is saved
— "Saved to your account", or "Saved to your account when you're signed in, and
kept on this device otherwise" — and a status: ==On this device== while signed
out, then **Syncing…**, **Synced** or **Couldn't sync** (with the reason). Each
tool name opens that tool, and a filter box narrows the list. Tools that only
show public or other tools' data, such as Rankings, are not listed.

## Known gaps

==Two tabs changing the *same* setting at the same moment end with whichever
saved last==. Different settings changed in different tabs no longer overwrite
each other, and favorites, recent tools, word limit presets and outline filter
presets merge safely across tabs.

## Pinned debates

The debates you pin to the start screen's **Featured** section ==follow your
account== and appear under **Tool data** as "Pinned Debates". ==A pin names the
same round on every device==; a pin whose round isn't on this device yet ==is
skipped until the round arrives==.

Known gap: ==pins are capped at 12 per browser==, and ==a pin removed on one
device while another is offline can reappear== until that device syncs.

## Debater level and XP

==Your XP, level, daily challenge progress and recent XP log sync to your account==
(listed in **Tool data** under Practice as "Debater Level & XP"). ==XP earned
on two devices adds up==, and so do today's per-activity counts. ==A daily
challenge reached only by the combined count is paid the next time the page
loads==, and only once.

Known gap: ==two devices settling different daily challenges before they sync
can lose one bonus==. ==XP can be under-counted but is never double-counted==.

The Debater Level page's **Mock data** button ==previews the page with sample
debaters== (a novice, a JV and a varsity debater), each with some challenges,
milestones and judge awards earned and others partway there. ==Mock profiles
are display-only==: ==they are never saved or synced==, and ==logging practice
and resetting are disabled== until you choose **Exit mock data**.

## Save status on each tool

==Tool data saves to your account automatically, with no badge== or **Save now**
button. The REASON editor's status line ==appears only when a save fails==
("Couldn't save — retrying") or a read-only public topic starter is open.
**Account sync** in Settings ==still shows sync failures and a retry==. Starring
a tool as a favorite ==uses the star at the end of the header row==.
