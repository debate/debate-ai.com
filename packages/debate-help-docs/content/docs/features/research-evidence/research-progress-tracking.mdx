# Research Progress Tracking

==Shows each contributor's progress across topics, task completion, and
contribution history==.

Find it at **/coaching/progress**.

## What it shows

A roster (contributions, tasks, per-topic breakdown), a **Download report**
export, a **Topic comparison** section ==rolling per-topic counts up across
the whole team (least-covered first)==, and, when you're signed in, a **My
research goal** section ==with a progress meter that follows you across
devices==.

## Good to know

- ==There are no contributor permissions== — signing in only highlights your
  own row.
- ==Your completed-task history syncs to your account== when you're signed in;
  signed out, it stays in this browser.
