# Learn Review Log Cloud Sync

==Syncs Debate Editor's Learn review log== — ==the history of every grade you
give a flashcard== — ==to your account when you're signed in==, the same way
[Learn Flashcards Cloud Sync](/docs/features/research-evidence/learn-cards-cloud-sync)
syncs the cards themselves and
[Learn Decks Cloud Sync](/docs/features/research-evidence/learn-decks-cloud-sync) syncs
custom decks.

## What it does

When the editor opens, ==your review log is merged once with your account==:
==reviews logged on another device but missing here are added==, and reviews
that exist only on this device are uploaded — ==neither side ever overwrites
the other, it only fills gaps==. After that, ==every review you grade is saved
to your account the same way==, ==and removed when its card is deleted== or a
file's cards are cleared with "forget this file". Signed out, or in the
desktop app, ==the review log keeps working exactly as before, on this device
only==.

==Syncing review history never changes a device's own schedule==: due dates and
intervals stay per device, so ==restoring history on a new device doesn't
change what is due there==.

The **Manage flashcards** overlay's **History** button ==lists every logged
review, newest first==, with ==the card's current front text, the grade, and
when it happened==, plus an overall "Synced to your account" / "Not synced"
line.

## Good to know

==A review synced from another device can belong to a card that hasn't reached
this device yet== — the History list shows =="(deleted card)" for it rather than
the card's front text== until the card arrives.
