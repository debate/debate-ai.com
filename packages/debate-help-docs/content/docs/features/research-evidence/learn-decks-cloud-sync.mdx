# Learn Decks Cloud Sync

==Syncs your Debate Editor Learn custom decks== (each deck's name and the cards
in it) to your account when you're signed in, the same way
[Learn Flashcards Cloud Sync](/docs/features/research-evidence/learn-cards-cloud-sync)
syncs the cards themselves. Before this, decks ==lived on one device only==.

## Managing decks

The **Decks** button, next to **History** in the **Manage flashcards**
overlay, ==lets you create, rename and delete decks== (delete takes two clicks),
and ==each deck expands into a card picker==: add a card from a dropdown of
cards not yet in the deck, or remove one with its own button. ==Each deck shows
a small "Synced" or "Not yet synced" badge==, and the overlay shows an overall
=="Synced to your account" / "Not synced — sign in to sync" line==.

## What it does

When the editor opens, ==your decks are merged once with your account==: ==a deck
saved from another device but missing here is added==, and a deck that exists
only on this device is uploaded — ==neither side ever overwrites the other, it
only fills gaps==. After that, ==every deck you create, rename, delete, or add
cards to is saved to your account==. ==Changes made on two devices at once are
both kept==, rather than the last one overwriting the first. Signed out, or in
the desktop app, ==decks keep working exactly as before, on this device only==.

==A deck can list a card that hasn't reached this device yet==; it shows up
once that card syncs.

## What stays on this device

Review schedules, links to places in your files, AI threads, and notes ==stay on
this device==. The **review log** ==syncs separately== — see
[Learn Review Log Cloud Sync](/docs/features/research-evidence/learn-review-log-cloud-sync).
