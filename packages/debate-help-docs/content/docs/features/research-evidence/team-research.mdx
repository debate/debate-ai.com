# Team Research

==Team Research is now part of the
[Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration)==
(`/research`). It used to be a second tabbed page beside it, at
`/research/team`, whose tabs mostly repeated panels the Research Workspace
already mounts. ==The two tab strips are now one==, and Practice Partners moved
to the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace), beside Practice
Drills.

| Old Team Research tab | Now |
| --- | --- |
| [Practice Partners](/docs/features/team-collaboration/practice-partners) | Prep Workspace, `/practice/prep?section=partners` (Judge Pool below it) |
| [Topic Coverage](/docs/features/research-evidence/topic-coverage-dashboard) | Research Workspace, `/research?section=coverage` |
| [Prep Room](/docs/features/team-collaboration/collaboration-prep-room) | Research Workspace, `/research?section=sprint` |
| [Review Queue](/docs/features/team-collaboration/review-queue) | Research Workspace, `/research?section=review` |
| [Task Inbox](/docs/features/team-collaboration/task-inbox) | Research Workspace, `/research?section=routing` |
| [Contributions](/docs/features/team-collaboration/contributions-feed) | Research Workspace, `/research?section=rewards` |
| [Brainstorm](/docs/features/research-evidence/brainstorm-board) | Research Workspace, `/research?section=sprint` |
| Debater Level | Research Workspace, `/research?section=level` |

- **Package:** `debate-webview` (`components/research/ResearchHub.tsx`; the
  old-tab mapping is `components/research/research-section-ids.ts`)

## Old addresses

==Every old address still works and permanently redirects to the tab that
now holds it==: `/research/team?section=…` (in bookmarks and in
notifications already stored with that link), `/research/cards/coverage`,
`/research/cards/prep-room`, `/research/cards/reviews`,
`/research/cards/inbox`, `/research/cards/contributions`,
`/research/cards/brainstorm`, `/practice/level` and `/practice/partners`.
A `#judge-pool` fragment survives the redirect.

Outside Next (the browser extension's router), ==the old routes render the
new page opened on the matching tab instead of redirecting==.

## Known gaps

- `/research/team` with no `?section=` lands on the Research Workspace's
  last-opened tab, not a particular one.
