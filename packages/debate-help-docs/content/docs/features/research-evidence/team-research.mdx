# Team Research

==Team Research is now part of the
[Research Workspace](/docs/features/research-evidence/research-collaboration)==.
It used to be a second tabbed page beside it, whose tabs mostly repeated
panels the Research Workspace already had. ==The two tab strips are now one==,
and Practice Partners moved to the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace),
beside Practice Drills.

| Old Team Research tab | Now |
| --- | --- |
| [Practice Partners](/docs/features/team-collaboration/practice-partners) | Its own page, with the Judge Pool below it |
| [Topic Coverage](/docs/features/research-evidence/topic-coverage-dashboard) | Research Workspace, Topic Coverage tab |
| [Prep Room](/docs/features/team-collaboration/collaboration-prep-room) | Research Workspace, Sprint tab |
| [Review Queue](/docs/features/team-collaboration/review-queue) | Research Workspace, Review tab |
| [Task Inbox](/docs/features/team-collaboration/task-inbox) | Research Workspace, Routing tab |
| [Contributions](/docs/features/team-collaboration/contributions-feed) | Research Workspace, Rewards tab |
| [Brainstorm](/docs/features/research-evidence/brainstorm-board) | Research Workspace, Sprint tab |
| Debater Level | Research Workspace, Level tab |

## Old links

==Every old link still works and opens the tab that now holds it==, including
links in bookmarks and in notifications you already have. An old Team Research
link that names no tab opens the Research Workspace on the tab you last had
open.
