# Card hover actions in the editor

==Hover over any card in Debate Editor and a small column of buttons
appears beside it==:

- **Summarize** ==asks the AI for a two-to-four sentence summary of the card==: ==its
  claim, its warrant, and the impact it supports==.
- **Find flaws** ==asks the AI for the weaknesses an opponent would attack==, the
  same find-flaws-and-extensions analysis as the evidence search's AI panel.
- **Read aloud** ==reads the card out loud in a natural AI voice==: the
  tag, then the citation (the author and year), ==then the highlighted
  text (what you would read in round)==, ==or the underlined text when
  nothing is highlighted==. In read mode it reads only what read mode shows
  of the card. ==Click it again to stop==.

==AI answers open in a small panel next to the card==, with **Copy** and **Close**
buttons; Escape closes it too. ==The buttons only appear with a mouse==, so they
never get in the way on a phone or tablet.

## Read the whole document aloud

**View → Read whole document aloud** ==reads every card in the document the same
way, top to bottom==, with a short pause between cards. ==The card being read is
outlined and scrolled into view==. Choose **View → Stop reading document**, press
Escape, or click Stop on a card's buttons to end it.

## Read aloud voices

**Read aloud** just reads: ==its speed and voice are set in== **Settings → Sound &
speech → Read aloud voice & speed**. ==The speed slider is 2x by default== and
changes speed while a card is playing. ==The voices are grouped by engine==:

- **Kokoro (on this device)**, the default: ==13 voices (American and British)
  that run in your browser==.
- **Cloudflare Workers AI**: ==12 Deepgram Aura voices spoken on the site's
  server==, with nothing to download. ==They need you signed in==; signed out, the
  card is read through QwkSearch's free allowance instead.
- **Browser built-in**: ==your browser's own speech voice==.

Pick the engine, then the voice; ==the change applies to every open editor at once==.
==Both settings are remembered in this browser==.

==Kokoro voices run right in your browser==: the first time you read a card
aloud, ==the Kokoro voice model (about 90 MB) downloads once and is kept by your
browser==, and from then on ==reading needs no server and has no daily limit==.
==Kokoro makes the text one paragraph at a time==: a small **Kokoro · paragraph
2 of 5 · 42%** box in the corner shows how far the paragraph being made has
come, ==each paragraph plays as soon as it is finished==, and the next one is
made while it plays. The voice is generated in the background, so ==the page
keeps scrolling and typing normally while a card is read==.

==Paragraphs are shared==. When you're signed in, every paragraph your Kokoro
finishes is saved to debate-ai.com's storage, and ==anyone who reads the same
text in the same voice later, signed in or not, hears that saved copy at once==
instead of waiting for their own device. Speed isn't part of it: the same copy
plays at whatever speed each reader picked. Signed out, you still hear saved
paragraphs; you just don't add new ones. ==To get the download out of the way before a round==, use
**Settings → Sound & speech → On-device speech models**, which shows whether the
voice is downloaded and how much space it takes, and can remove it.

If your browser can't run Kokoro, or the download fails, ==the card is read by
a server instead==. Those voices come from QwkSearch (Kokoro or Deepgram), or from Cloudflare's
Deepgram Aura voices when the site has Cloudflare AI set up. Signed out, you
==share QwkSearch's free allowance of 10 reads a day==; ==link your QwkSearch account
in settings to read on your own account==. When an AI voice isn't available, or
the daily allowance is used up, the card is read with your browser's voice
instead, ==so Read aloud always works==.

## Which AI it uses

If you have turned on AI features with your own Anthropic or OpenRouter key in
the editor's settings, ==the buttons use that key==. Otherwise they use
debate-ai.com's AI: ==each new answer counts toward your plan's daily card AI
analyses==, ==a card that already has a saved answer costs nothing==, and ==the
summary needs you to be signed in==.

## Where it lives

- **Routes:** `/reason-editor` and Flow's speech-doc panels (==anywhere the
  Debate Editor embed is mounted==)
- **Package:** `debate-editor` (`overlay/editor/card-hover-actions.ts`,
  `overlay/editor/card-ai-client.ts`, `overlay/editor/card-tts-client.ts`,
  `overlay/editor/kokoro-browser-tts.ts`, `overlay/editor/kokoro-paragraph-audio.ts`,
  `overlay/editor/kokoro-worker.ts` (Kokoro runs in this Web Worker, off the page's main thread),
  registered in `overlay/react/singleton.ts`)
- **API:** `/api/card-ai-analysis`, `/api/tts` (`packages/debate-webview/src/lib/tts/voice.ts`),
  `/api/tts/cache` (shared Kokoro paragraphs, `overlay/editor/kokoro-paragraph-audio.ts`
  and `overlay/editor/kokoro-audio-key.ts`; stored in the `SPEECH_AUDIO` R2 bucket
  under `tts-cache/kokoro/`)

## Known gaps

- ==Shared paragraphs are trusted as uploaded==. The server files each upload
  under the hash of the text it was sent and checks it is a WAV of a plausible
  length, but it can't tell whether the audio really says that text, so a
  signed-in account could store a wrong clip. The first upload for a paragraph
  is kept; there is no admin tool to remove one yet (delete it from the
  `SPEECH_AUDIO` bucket under `tts-cache/kokoro/`).
- Making a whole paragraph before it plays means a long paragraph waits a few
  seconds longer to start than the old sentence-by-sentence playback did.
