# Shared Evidence Library

==A submission form plus a free-text/kind search panel over the team-wide
evidence repository== — ==cut cards and reusable analytic blocks==.

- **Where:** **/research/cards/library**

## What it shows

A submission form (kind, topic, case area, argument block, citation, tags,
==body text with a live word count==) plus a search box, kind filter, and
topic, case-area and tag filters over everything in the library.
==Each result has Edit and Delete actions==; ==editing scores the revision== for
the [Revision Incentives](/docs/features/gamification/revision-incentives) leaderboard. A **Check this
page** box ==checks whether a pasted URL has already been cut==, ==both in your
library and against the shared reuse index== the browser extension uses.

The **Argument Library** (**/research/cards/argument-library**) ==combines this
library with tagged Contributions Feed submissions== into topic and case-area
folders and tag collections that cut across them, ==with rename, merge and bulk
tag-editing tools==.

## Formatting a citation

Under the Citation field, **Format citation** ==rewrites a pasted full citation
short-cite first==: `Jane Smith, Professor at MIT, 2024, …` becomes
`Smith 24 — Jane Smith, Professor at MIT, 2024, …`. Undated sources get `ND`
(`Smith ND`), organizations keep their full name (`Brookings Institution 23`)
and two authors are joined (`Doe & Roe 19`). An affiliation written as an
acronym is not mistaken for a second author: `Jane Smith, MIT, 2024` becomes
`Smith 24`. ==If the Source URL field is filled
and the citation doesn't already contain that link, it is added at the end==.
Pressing it again changes nothing, and the result stays editable before you
save. When no author can be found the citation is left as it is, with a note
asking you to start it with a last name.

## Search

==Library search ranks results by relevance==, and ==stays fast as the library
grows==.

## Links to a search

==Other pages can link straight to a card search== with the term and filters
(year, event, school, team or tournament) already filled in — the topics
explorer, for one, ==links every resolution to its cards==. ==The address bar
doesn't update as you change the search afterwards==.

## The first search is ready on open

Opening the card search with nothing filled in ==shows the default list==
(newest cards, quotes) ==from your last visit straight away==, while the page
fetches the current list and ==swaps it in without a spinner==. ==Every search
still counts toward your daily search limit==.

## A new search always wins

Typing waits a moment before searching, but changing the term, sort or filters
==cancels the previous search at once==, so ==results for an older search can't
land after a newer one==. Pressing Enter, clicking a suggestion or accepting the
inline completion ==searches right away==.

The search box ==shows the top completion inline as greyed-out text==, accepted
with Tab or →. ↑ and ↓ step through the suggestions, and ==stepping quickly
through the list costs one search, not one per suggestion==. ==Results aren't
loaded ahead for suggestions you haven't reached==, since every search counts
toward your daily limit.

## Good to know

- ==Tags are matched exactly==, though autocomplete ignores capitals, ==typed
  tags take the casing of an existing tag==, and the library browser shows and
  merges any variants that slip through.
- ==**Format citation** produces one style only== (debate short cite plus the
  pasted citation); there is no MLA / APA / Chicago output, and the author is
  guessed, so an unusual citation can get the wrong last name — check the
  short cite before saving.
