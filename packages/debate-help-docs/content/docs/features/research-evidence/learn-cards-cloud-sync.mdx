# Learn Flashcards Cloud Sync

==Syncs your Debate Editor Learn flashcards== (each card's front and back) ==to
your account when you're signed in==, the same way [Quick Cards Cloud Save](/docs/features/ui-navigation#quick-cards-cloud-save)
does for your reusable snippets. Before this, flashcards ==lived on one device
only==.

## What it does

When the editor opens, ==your flashcards are merged once with your account==:
==a card saved from another device but missing here is added (never
duplicated)==, and ==a card that exists only on this device is uploaded== —
==neither side ever overwrites the other, it only fills gaps==. After that,
==every card you create, edit, or delete== (including a file's cards being
cleared with "forget this file") ==is saved to your account the same way==.
Signed out, or in the desktop app, ==flashcards keep working exactly as
before, on this device only==.

==An edit never overwrites a newer one==: if a card was changed on another
device and that change hasn't reached this browser yet, ==this browser takes
the newer version instead of losing it==.

## What stays on this device

==Only a card's content syncs== — its review **schedule** (due date,
interval, lapses), links to places in your files, AI threads, and private
notes ==all stay on this device==. ==A card added on a new device starts with no
reviews due==: ==restoring a card is not meant to restore another device's
review history==. **Custom decks** and the **review log** ==each sync
separately== — see [Learn Decks Cloud Sync](/docs/features/research-evidence/learn-decks-cloud-sync) and
[Learn Review Log Cloud Sync](/docs/features/research-evidence/learn-review-log-cloud-sync).
