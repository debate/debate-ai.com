# Research Publishers

==Give your files an author, a publisher and the students who wrote them==, and
==let other debaters subscribe to your research group==. Every research
publisher has its own page, and ==every file published under it shows who
published it, right in the editor's sidebar==.

## A file's details

Open one of your files. Under **My Personal Documents** in the sidebar, the
**About this file** note shows its details: the author, ==who published it==,
the research group and its students, the seasons it covers and the date it
was published. ==Choose== **Edit details** ==to change them in a dialog==.

- **Published by** lists the publishers you run. Pick **New publisher…** to
  ==create one right there==, with a name and a monthly price.
- ==Click the publisher's name in the note to open its page==.

## A publisher's page

Find it at **/research/publishers/**`name`. It shows ==the research group, its
students, the seasons it has published== and ==every file published under it==,
with each file's authors, dates and size. ==Card text is never shown there==.

If you run the publisher, **Edit page** changes its name, description,
research group, students, seasons, website and monthly price.

## Subscribing

**Subscribe** ==pays one month (30 days) from your wallet to the publisher==.
==A free publisher costs nothing to follow==. ==While your subscription lasts,
every file on the page has== **Save a copy**, which ==puts the whole file in your
own files and opens it==. Add money to your wallet from the same page if it
doesn't cover the price (see [File Market](/docs/features/research-evidence/file-market)).

## Good to know

- ==Subscriptions don't renew by themselves==; choose **Renew** once a month
  runs out.
- ==You can only publish under a publisher you run==.
- ==Files you copy are yours to edit==; later changes by the publisher don't
  reach your copy.
