# Topics & Statistics

==One page combining the research-area topic explorer==, ==a searchable list of every
season's debate resolutions==, and ==the YouTube channel view and upload charts==.

Find it at **/practice/statistics**, or as "Topics & Video Statistics" in the
sidebar's Practice section and the video library's quick-link tiles. The old
Debate Topics Explorer page ==now opens here==.

## What it shows

==Three sections, stacked==, with Debate Topics by Year at the top:

- **Debate Topics by Year** (`DebateTopicsExplorer`) — a timeline
  (`components/timeline/Timeline.tsx`, adapted from the shadcn-space
  "timeline-02" block): ==a scrollable rail of every season, newest first==,
  beside an animated pane for the selected year. ==The pane shows that year's
  Policy, College (NDT), LD and PF resolutions==, each behind a style badge
  (LD and PF list each month's resolution with its short start month, such
  as "Sep" or "Nov", under the badge rather than in the resolution text),
  and — once `/api/youtube-stats` resolves — ==that year's video count, total
  views and average views== from its `byYear` rows. Click a year, use the
  arrow / Home / End keys on the rail, or the up/down buttons beside it to
  scroll through every season; ==the selection survives filtering when the
  year is still shown==. ==A search box filters by year or by resolution text==. The data
  is the same `topics` field `/api/videos/meta` already returns for the video
  cards' topic tooltips (`debate-rankings`'s `src/topics/debate-topics.ts` formatters), passed down
  from `LecturesPage` rather than fetched again here. ==Every resolution is a
  link==: clicking one ==opens the CARDS search== (`/research/cards`) for that topic's short
  name, ==filtered to that season's year and that format== (Policy → CX,
  College → NDT, LD, PF), ==listing every card, outline and round that matches==.
  At the top of the rail, above the newest season, sits ==the coming season==
  (e.g. **2028** while 2026–27 is in progress). The page still opens on the
  newest season with resolutions; clicking the coming year swaps the
  resolutions for a **Next season** entry (`NextSeasonPanel`,
  e.g. "Next season · 2027–28"), ==shown before any of that season's
  resolutions are announced==. ==It holds a poll== — "Which topic area would you
  most like to debate next season?" ==It's ranked-choice: click up to five
  areas in order of preference== (click again to remove one) and press
  **Submit ranking**. ==Each area scores Borda points== — 5 for a first choice
  down to 1 for a fifth — and ==the list is ordered by points, then first
  choices==. ==Anyone can see the tally; voting needs a sign-in==, and ==each user has
  one ballot per season that they can update at any time==. The coming year
  is the season the poll is open for (`nextPollSeason`), and it drops off the
  rail once that season's resolutions are added to the data.
- **Research Areas** (`TopicAreasExplorer`) — every NDT, Policy, LD and PF
  ==resolution since 2000, split into 44 focused research areas==. ==Each format is
  its own collapsible panel with its own selection==, so comparing an area's
  Policy history against its PF history ==is two clicks rather than a filter
  toggle that throws the first answer away==. Within a panel ==the areas are
  ranked by resolution count as a bar chart==; ==selecting one lists its
  resolutions newest first and draws its year-by-year trend line==. ==The area
  assignments are heuristic research categories, not official NSDA or NDT
  classifications== — the section says so in its footer.
- **YouTube Channel Statistics** (`YouTubeStatsCharts`) — the top-channels
  table and the views/videos/avg-views-over-time charts the old `YouTubeStatsModal`
  drew in its dialog (since removed), ==extracted so they can render inline on a full
  page instead==. The four totals charts (views, videos and average views over
  time, and average views by elim round) ==are compact and sit four to a row on
  wide screens==, two on tablets and one on phones. Backed by `useYouTubeStats()` (`GET /api/youtube-stats`,
  itself optional furniture — a deployment with no YouTube API key just
  ==leaves this section off rather than erroring==).

  Above the format panels sits a **Next season** entry (for example "Next season ·
  2027–28"), ==shown before that season's resolutions are announced==. ==It holds a
  poll== — "Which topic area would you most like to debate next season?" ==It's
  ranked-choice: click up to five areas in order of preference== (click again to
  remove one) and press **Submit ranking**. ==A first choice is worth 5 points down
  to 1 for a fifth==, and ==the list is ordered by points, then first choices==.
  ==Anyone can see the tally; voting needs a sign-in==, and ==you have one ballot per
  season that you can change any time==. ==The poll moves on to the following season
  in July==.
- **YouTube Channel Statistics** — the top-channels table and charts of views,
  videos and average views over time, and average views by elim round. ==The charts
  sit four to a row on wide screens==, two on tablets and one on phones. ==If channel
  statistics aren't available, the section is left off==.

## Good to know

==Topic links search by the topic's short name== (for example "Water"), not the full
resolution, since ==a whole resolution sentence would match almost nothing==. ==A few
older seasons' short names are still vague==, so their searches can be broad.
