# Topics & Video Statistics

==One page combining the research-area topic explorer==, ==a searchable list of every
season's debate resolutions==, and ==the YouTube channel view and upload charts==.

Find it at **/practice/statistics**, or as "Topics & Video Statistics" in the
sidebar's Practice section and the video library's quick-link tiles. The old
Debate Topics Explorer page ==now opens here==.

## What it shows

==Three sections, stacked==, with Debate Topics by Year at the top:

- **Debate Topics by Year** — ==a scrollable timeline of every season, newest
  first==, beside a pane for the selected year. ==The pane shows that year's Policy,
  College (NDT), LD and PF resolutions==, each behind a style badge (LD and PF list
  each month's resolution with its start month, such as "Sep" or "Nov"), and
  ==that year's video count, total views and average views==. Click a year, use the
  arrow / Home / End keys on the timeline, or the up/down buttons beside it; ==the
  selection stays when you filter and the year is still shown==. ==A search box
  filters by year or resolution text==, and an **All / Policy / College / LD / PF**
  row above it ==shows only one style's resolutions==, hiding years without one.
  ==Every resolution is a link==: clicking one ==opens the CARDS search== for that
  topic, ==filtered to that season and format==, ==listing every card, outline and
  round that matches==.
- **Research Areas** — every NDT, Policy, LD and PF ==resolution since 2000, split
  into 44 focused research areas==. ==Each format is its own collapsible panel with
  its own selection==, so comparing an area's Policy history against its PF
  history ==is two clicks==. Within a panel ==the areas are ranked by resolution count
  as a bar chart==; ==selecting one lists its resolutions newest first and draws its
  year-by-year trend line==. ==The areas are rough research categories, not official
  NSDA or NDT classifications==.

  Above the format panels sits a **Next season** entry (for example "Next season ·
  2027–28"), ==shown before that season's resolutions are announced==. ==It holds a
  poll== — "Which topic area would you most like to debate next season?" ==It's
  ranked-choice: click up to five areas in order of preference== (click again to
  remove one) and press **Submit ranking**. ==A first choice is worth 5 points down
  to 1 for a fifth==, and ==the list is ordered by points, then first choices==.
  ==Anyone can see the tally; voting needs a sign-in==, and ==you have one ballot per
  season that you can change any time==. ==The poll moves on to the following season
  in July==.
- **YouTube Channel Statistics** — the top-channels table and charts of views,
  videos and average views over time, and average views by elim round. ==The charts
  sit four to a row on wide screens==, two on tablets and one on phones. ==If channel
  statistics aren't available, the section is left off==.

## Good to know

==Topic links search by the topic's short name== (for example "Water"), not the full
resolution, since ==a whole resolution sentence would match almost nothing==. ==A few
older seasons' short names are still vague==, so their searches can be broad.
