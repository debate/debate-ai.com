# Topics & Statistics

==One page combining the research-area topic explorer==, ==a searchable list of every
season's debate resolutions==, and ==the YouTube channel view and upload charts==.

Find it at **/practice/statistics**, or as "Topics & Video Statistics" in the
sidebar's Practice section and the video library's quick-link tiles. The old
Debate Topics Explorer page ==now opens here==.

## What it shows

==Three sections, stacked==, with Debate Topics by Year at the top:

- **Debate Topics by Year** — ==a scrollable rail of every season, newest first==,
  beside a pane for the selected year. ==The pane shows that year's
  Policy, College (NDT), LD and PF resolutions==, each behind a style badge
  (LD and PF list each month's resolution with its short start month, such
  as "Sep" or "Nov", under the badge rather than in the resolution text),
  ==each resolution's short name, icon and research-area category==,
  and ==that year's video count, total views and average views==. Click a year,
  use the arrow / Home / End keys on the rail, or the up/down buttons beside it
  to scroll through every season; ==the selection survives filtering when the
  year is still shown==. ==A search box filters by year, resolution text or category==.
  ==Every resolution is a link==: clicking one ==opens the CARDS search== for
  that topic's short name, ==filtered to that season's year and that format==
  (Policy → CX, College → NDT, LD, PF), ==listing every card, outline and round
  that matches==.

  At the top of the rail, above the newest season, sits ==the coming season==
  (e.g. **2028** while 2026–27 is in progress). The page still opens on the
  newest season with resolutions; clicking the coming year shows a **Next
  season** entry (e.g. "Next season · 2027–28"), ==shown before any of that
  season's resolutions are announced==. ==It holds a poll== — "Which topic area
  would you most like to debate next season?" ==It's ranked-choice: click up to
  five areas in order of preference== (click again to remove one) and press
  **Submit ranking**. ==A first choice is worth 5 points down to 1 for a
  fifth==, and ==the list is ordered by points, then first choices==. ==Anyone
  can see the tally; voting needs a sign-in==, and ==you have one ballot per
  season that you can change any time==. The coming year drops off the rail
  once that season's resolutions are announced.
- **Research Areas** — every NDT, Policy, LD and PF
  ==resolution since 1927–28, split into 44 focused research areas==. ==Each format is
  its own collapsible panel with its own selection==, so comparing an area's
  Policy history against its PF history ==is two clicks==. Within a panel ==the
  areas are ranked by resolution count as a bar chart==; ==selecting one lists
  its resolutions newest first and draws its year-by-year trend line==. ==The
  area assignments are heuristic research categories, not official NSDA or NDT
  classifications== — the section says so in its footer.
- **YouTube Channel Statistics** — the top-channels table and charts of views,
  videos and average views over time, and average views by elim round. ==The charts
  sit four to a row on wide screens==, two on tablets and one on phones. ==If channel
  statistics aren't available, the section is left off==.

## Good to know

==Topic links search by the topic's short name== (for example "Water"), not the full
resolution, since ==a whole resolution sentence would match almost nothing==. ==A few
older seasons' short names are still vague==, so their searches can be broad.
