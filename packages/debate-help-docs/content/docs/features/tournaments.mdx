# Tournaments

Tournament invitations, pairings and results, without leaving the app. There is
one entry for all of it under **Practice** in the sidebar: **Tournaments**
(`/practice/tournaments`), with Tabroom's own site available from a button at
the top of that page.

## What you can see

- **Upcoming tournaments**, searchable by name, state, circuit or event. A
  **Host Tournament** button opens the creation form, and a **Tabroom** button
  frames beta.tabroom.com over the list.
- **Invite**: dates, location, registration deadline, events with entry counts
  and fees, posted documents, contacts and the tournament's info pages.
- **Pairings**: every published round, grouped by event. Opening a round shows
  each room's entries (sides or speaker order) and judges.
- **Results**: every published result set, with places, entries and schools.
- **Tabroom**: when viewing a tournament, a tab in the nav opens Tabroom's own
  page for that tournament in a frame, so you can register or enter ballots
  without leaving the app.

## Hosting your own tournament

**Host Tournament** on the upcoming page (or `/practice/tournaments/host`)
creates the tournament in this site's own API — the vendored Tabroom tables in
the app's database — rather than sending you to Tabroom first. Give it a name,
dates, where it is held, and pick the formats it runs:

- **Virtual** — fully remote, rounds run online via video.
- **In-Person** — at a single physical venue.
- **Long-Term Online** — online, scheduled across multiple days or weeks.

### The three styles

A tournament runs any combination of **Policy**, **LD** and **Public Forum**,
each of which becomes its own division you can customize:

- **Policy** — 1AC/2AC/1AR/2AR against 1NC/2NC/1NR/2NR, four cross-examination
  periods.
- **LD** — 1AC/1AR against 1NC/2NR, no speaker points.
- **Public Forum** — CON/AFF/NS/AS/NAF/NEG with a shared grand
  cross-examination, where the flip decides who speaks first.

Per style you choose the division it runs in (Open, Novice or JV), how entries
are written in pairings, a cap on entries per school, an entry fee, and a note
that appears on the public invite.

## Good to know

- Pairings and results are refreshed at most once a minute, so reload during a
  tournament to see the latest round.
- You are recorded as the tournament's owner, and registered in every format it
  runs, so the first pairing has somebody in it. Everything after creation —
  schools, rooms, rounds and ballots — is still done in Tabroom, from the framed
  view.
- Signing in to Tabroom happens on Tabroom, inside the framed view; the
  **Tournaments** view shows public information only.
