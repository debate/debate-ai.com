# Tournaments

Tournament invitations, pairings and results from Tabroom, without leaving the
app. There are two ways in, both under **Practice** in the sidebar.

- **Tournaments** (`/practice/tournaments-beta`): the tournament pages drawn by
  Debate AI itself, in your current theme. Live data comes from Tabroom.
- **Tournaments (Tabroom)** (`/practice/tournaments`): Tabroom's own beta site,
  shown inside the app exactly as it looks on beta.tabroom.com.

## What you can see

- **Upcoming tournaments**, searchable by name, state, circuit or event.
- **Invite**: dates, location, registration deadline, events with entry counts
  and fees, posted documents, contacts and the tournament's info pages.
- **Pairings**: every published round, grouped by event. Opening a round shows
  each room's entries (sides or speaker order) and judges.
- **Results**: every published result set, with places, entries and schools.

## Good to know

- Pairings and results are refreshed at most once a minute, so reload during a
  tournament to see the latest round.
- Both views are read-only. To register, enter results or tab a tournament,
  use Tabroom itself (the framed view, or tabroom.com).
- Signing in to Tabroom happens on Tabroom, inside the framed view; the
  **Tournaments** view shows public information only.
