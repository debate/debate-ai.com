# Quest Streaks

==A roster of every contributor's daily-quest streak and milestone badges==,
plus ==a button to work out today's mission result==.

Find it at **/research/cards/streaks**.

## What it shows

Current and longest streak, badges, a **streak freeze** ("grace day") action
==for a missed day (up to 2 per rolling 30 days)==, and ==an opt-in
streak-lapse reminder banner==. The mission-result history streaks and
badges are worked out from ==follows you to another device when you're signed
in==, for every contributor's row this browser knows about, and your own
streak freezes and reminder setting follow you too.

## Good to know

- ==Today's mission result is worked out when you press the button==; it
  ==doesn't run on its own==.
- A contributor's row only appears once some device has worked out a result
  for them; ==a row can't appear from nowhere==.
- Streak freezes and the reminder setting ==only sync for your own row==,
  never another contributor's.
