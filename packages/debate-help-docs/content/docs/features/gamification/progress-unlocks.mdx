# Progress Unlocks

==Shows every contributor's unlock tier==, the research-task skill level that
tier grants, ==every badge earned, their current streak, and how far they
are from the next tier==.

Find it at **/research/cards/progress**.

## What it shows

A roster table (tier, unlocked tasks, streak, tasks completed, badges,
next-tier progress bar), your own row highlighted when you're signed in, and
==a celebration pop-up when your own row newly earns a badge==.

==Badges you've already been celebrated for are remembered on your account==,
so ==a badge celebrated on one device isn't celebrated again as "new" on
another==.

## Good to know

==There are no contributor permissions== — signing in only highlights your
own row.
