# Select YouTube Start

==A speech in the round workspace can come from a recording of the whole
round instead of its own recording==: a YouTube video, or an MP3 of the round
such as the joined MP3 of every recorded speech. Each speech's ⋮ menu has
**Select YouTube Start** right under **Upload Speech Audio**. ==It opens a
modal for that speech==.

- **Where:** live round workspace, speech timer menu
- **Package:** `debate-round` (`dialogs/YouTubeSpeechDialog.tsx`,
  `dialogs/SpeechStartLabeler.tsx`,
  `round/youtube-speech-client.ts`), `debate-timer`
  (`recorder/youtube-speech-segments.ts`), `debate-speech-writer`
  (`prompts/youtube-speech-timestamps.ts`)

## What it does

- **Upload audio or pick a video.** The "Upload audio" tab ==saves an audio
  file as the speech's recording==, the same as the menu item. The "YouTube
  video" tab ==takes a pasted YouTube link== (a `t=` start is kept) or a search,
  ==which looks through the site's own video library==. The "Round audio (MP3)"
  tab ==plays the round's joined MP3, when there is one, or any MP3 link==.
  ==Both tabs label speeches the same way==.
- **Preview and mark the speeches.** ==The video plays in the modal, above a
  list of every speech in the format with its start and stop==. Type `m:ss`,
  press the crosshair to use where the video is now, nudge ±5 s, jump to a
  start, or clear it. ==It is the same list as== **Adjust speech starts** on the
  joined round MP3 and **Speech starts** on a video's watch page, with stops
  added. ==This speech's row is highlighted==: **Play clip** ==plays just its
  stretch== and **Save for** keeps the clip for it.
- **AI suggests every speech.** **Suggest all speeches with AI** ==reads the
  video's captions, finds where words are spoken and where there is silence==,
  and ==fits the format's speeches to them==: all 12 in Policy, cross-examinations
  included, or the 7 in LD. ==Speech lengths come from the format but are
  allowed to vary==, and ==prep time between speeches is expected==. The AI then
  ==checks the captions for cues like a roadmap or "cross-ex" and adjusts==.
  Pick a different format if the video isn't the round's format; ==picking a
  video from the library sets it for you==. ==If you set a start point first,
  the suggestion starts there==.
- **Save them all.** ==A suggestion fills in the list, where any row can be
  fixed by hand==. **Save all** ==keeps a clip for every speech that has a start==;
  ==a speech with no stop runs to the next speech's start==.

## Good to know

- If the AI can't be reached, ==the suggestion is placed from the caption
  pauses alone==. If the video has no captions, ==speeches are laid out by their
  lengths from your start point, so set one first==.
- ==Clips are saved in this browser==, beside the speech's recordings.
- Saving all speeches on a YouTube video ==also marks their starts on that
  video's watch page timeline==, when the format's speech order there matches.
- ==An MP3 has no captions unless the round's recording provides them==; without
  them, speeches are laid out by length from your start point.
