# Live Stream (Round Radio)

Check **Live stream** when you create or edit a round and ==the round is
broadcast as audio, like radio==. ==Only the speeches go out, each one in the
speaker's own voice==, back to back. Anyone can ==tune in from **Videos**== while
the round is on, and ==the replay stays there afterwards==.

- **Where:** **Debate** → **Create New Round** or **Edit Round** → **Live stream**.
  Listen from **Videos** (the **Live rounds** row) or on the round's own page.

## What it does

- **One checkbox.** ==**Live stream** sits under the Private/Public switch== in
  both the create and the edit menus. Turning it off later ==stops the broadcast
  and keeps the replay==.
- **Only your speeches, only your voice.** ==Your browser goes on air when your
  speech timer starts== (with recording on, which it is by default) ==and stops
  when the timer stops==. Prep time and the gaps between speeches are never
  sent.
- **The speaker's device, nobody else's.** Everyone in the round runs a timer,
  so ==the site works out who is speaking from the round's invite list==: the
  1A broadcasts the 1AC and 1AR, the 2N the 2NC and 2NR, an LD debater every
  speech on their side. ==Judges and spectators never broadcast==. If the
  debaters were typed as names rather than emails, ==the device running the
  timer broadcasts==.
- **On air badge.** ==A pill in the corner of the round shows **Live stream**,
  and turns red with **On air · 1AC** while you are broadcasting==. Click it to
  open the listening page and share its link.
- **Tuning in.** ==A live round starts you at the beginning of the speech being
  given==, a few seconds behind live, so you hear it from its first word. When
  you catch up, ==the player waits for the next speech and carries on by itself==.
  **Go live** skips ahead to the newest speech, and ==each speech is a chip you
  can jump to==.
- **Replays.** ==A round that has finished (or has been quiet for two hours)
  shows as a replay== and plays from the 1AC to the last rebuttal.
- **Private rounds.** ==A private round's stream isn't listed in Videos==. Its
  link works only for the people on the round: debaters, judges and spectators,
  signed in.

## Known gaps

- ==Audio goes out in 5-second pieces==, so listeners are at least that far
  behind, and there can be a very short gap between pieces in some browsers.
- ==Cross-ex is broadcast only from the device whose timer is running for it==
  (the format's speaker for that cross-ex); the other debater's questions come
  through that mic.
- ==The broadcast uses the default microphone==, not the one picked in the
  recording menu.
- ==Anyone with a debater's email on the invite can broadcast into the stream==,
  and the stream is keyed by the round's link, so two rounds with the same
  tournament, round and teams share a stream.
- ==Streams have no delete button yet==; switching Live stream off before the
  first speech hides one.
