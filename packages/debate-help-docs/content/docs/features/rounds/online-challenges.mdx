# Online Challenges

==Challenge another player, or the AI, to a written debate on any resolution
the site lists, in any format==. Rounds are asynchronous: ==each side has 3
days to answer the other's speech==. When the last speech is in, ==an AI judge
picks the winner and explains why==. ==Wins earn Debater Level XP, a
win-streak boost and trophies==.

- **Route:** `/practice/challenges`, the first row of the sidebar's
  **Practice** section (with [Practice Drills](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/drill-sets)
  and [Practice Partners](/docs/features/team-collaboration/practice-partners))
- **Package:** `debate-practice-rounds` (`src/debate-challenges/`)
- **Engineering note:** `dev-docs/internals/online-challenges.mdx`

## Sending a challenge

1. **Opponent.** Pick **AI opponent** and a level (Novice, Varsity or
   Champion), or **Another player** and find them by name.
2. **Format.** Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, College Policy (NDT),
   Parliamentary or Worlds.
3. **Resolution.** ==Every topic in the site's topic list for that format,
   newest season first==, labelled like "2026-27 · Sep · 💾 Hyperscale Data
   Centers". Choose **Write my own…** for any other motion (Parliamentary and
   Worlds have no fixed topics, so they always start there).
4. **Side.** Affirmative or Negative. ==The Affirmative speaks first, so an Aff
   challenger writes the opening speech before sending==.
5. An optional note to a player.

## How a round runs

Four speeches, alternating: Affirmative constructive, Negative constructive,
Affirmative rebuttal, Negative rebuttal (up to 9,000 characters each).

- ==The opponent's first speech is how they accept==. They can also
  **Decline**. Until they answer, the challenger can **Withdraw**.
- ==Every speech restarts the other side's 3-day clock==. The board shows how
  long is left on each round.
- ==Missing your window forfeits the round to the other side==. A challenge
  nobody answered just expires, with no winner.
- Either debater can **Concede** a round in progress.
- ==Against the AI, its speeches are written the moment it is its turn==, in
  the style of the level you picked.
- After the fourth speech, ==the AI judge decides on the arguments made in the
  round== and writes a short reason for decision.

You get a notification for every challenge, speech, forfeit and decision.

## XP, boosts and trophies

| Result | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Finish a round (win or lose) | 20 |
| Win | +40 × your boost |
| Lose by running out of time | 0 |

==The boost is your win streak==: the second win in a row pays 1.5×, the third
and fourth 2×, and the fifth and every one after 3×. A loss resets it. The
board shows the boost your next win will get.

==XP is added to your Debater Level automatically== the next time you open the
page after a round ends, once per round (a second device doesn't collect it
again).

Trophies: **First Victory** (first win), **Iron Debater** (10 rounds),
**Hat Trick** (3 wins in a row), **On Fire** (5 in a row), **Contender** (10
wins), **Champion** (25 wins), **Machine Breaker** (beat the Champion AI),
**Format Master** (5 wins in one format), **All-Rounder** (wins in 3 formats)
and **Crowd Favorite** (beat 5 players).

## Limits

- 10 challenges to players waiting on a first answer at once, and one at a
  time per opponent.
- 10 new AI challenges a day.
- Guest accounts can debate the AI but not challenge players.
