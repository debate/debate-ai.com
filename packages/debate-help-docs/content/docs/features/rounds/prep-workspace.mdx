# Prep Workspace

==One page for getting ready for a round==. The five pre-round prep tools
==sit side by side, one tab each==. Practice Drills and Practice Partners were
tabs here for a while; ==they now have their own pages in the sidebar's==
**Practice** ==section==, beside [Online Challenges](/docs/features/rounds/online-challenges):

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Pre-Round Briefings](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/pre-round-briefings) | One briefing per upcoming round: opponent scouting, judge tendencies, the head-to-head record, and team prep notes. |
| [Scout-to-Strategy](/docs/features/ai-coaching/scout-to-strategy) | Ranks your case options against the saved opponent and judge profiles, with a judge-adaptation note each and a matchup risk level. |
| [Opponent Team Profiles](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/opponent-team-profiles) | Records, Aff/Neg side tendencies, and the arguments and cases each scouted team runs most. |
| [Judge Profiles](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/judge-profiles) | Side-vote bias, average speaker points, speed tolerance, and theory receptiveness for every saved judge. |
| [Prep Notes](/docs/features/flow-speech/prep-notes) | Live prep notes across every flow, needs-follow-up first, with handoff to a teammate. |

Open it from **Prep Workspace** in the sidebar's **Research** section.

## Tabs and links

Each tool used to have its own page. ==Those old links still work and open the
matching tab==, and links to the old Practice Drills and Practice Partners tabs
open their own pages. ==Your saved briefings, profiles, recommendations and notes
carry over unchanged and keep syncing to your account==.

==The page remembers the tab you had open==, and the address bar updates as you
switch tabs, so you can bookmark or share a link straight to one tool. The round
workspace's "Tools for this round" menu ==links straight to== **Practice Drills**,
and practice-challenge and Judge Pool notifications ==open Practice Partners==.

## Good to know

- Opening a saved record from the Tools page's recent items ==lands on
  the right tab, not on the record itself==.
- The Coach Workspace still splits these tools across its **Prep**,
  **Scouting** and **Practice** sections; its "open as its own page" chips
  ==lead to the matching Prep Workspace tab==.
