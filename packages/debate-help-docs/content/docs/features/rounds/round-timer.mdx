# Round Timer & Shared Clock

Every device in a round runs on the same clock. When anyone plays, pauses,
resets or edits a speech or prep timer, everyone else with the round open sees
the same change. The sidebar also has a **Timer** section with the circular
progress timer from the browser extension, which can pop out into its own
small window.

- **Nav:** `/debate`, left sidebar, the **Timer** section under the selected
  round (above **Cameras**)
- **Package:** `debate-round` (`layout/RoundTimerPanel.tsx`,
  `layout/SidebarSectionsMenu.tsx`, `utils/sidebar-sections.ts`,
  `hooks/useRoomTimerSync.ts`, `hooks/useTimerSync.ts`,
  `webcam/useWebcamRoom.ts`), `debate-timer`
  (`timers/circular/CircularTimerFace.tsx`), app Worker
  (`lib/webcam/debate-room.ts`)

## What it does

- **Play/pause reaches the whole room.** Starting or stopping a timer on one
  laptop starts or stops it on the judge's and every debater's laptop too.
  Resets, cross-ex switches, edited times and both prep clocks travel the same
  way. A running timer is sent as how long it has been running, so each device
  counts down on its own and a laptop whose clock is a few seconds off still
  shows the same time.
- **Late joiners catch up.** Opening the round after it started pulls in the
  timers that have already been used. Opening it never resets anyone else's
  timers.
- **Circular timer in the sidebar.** The **Timer** section shows the dial for
  the speech in view: the ring empties as time runs out and the face darkens
  while it runs. Click anywhere on the dial to play or pause. The two numbers
  under the count are the Aff and Neg prep clocks; click one to show it on the
  dial, and click the speech name to go back. Starting prep pauses a running
  speech, and starting a speech pauses running prep. The section collapses
  like Cameras, and shows the running time in its header while collapsed.
- **Pop out.** The picture-in-picture button next to the section header opens
  the dial in a small always-on-top window (Chrome and Edge), so it stays
  visible over other apps. In other browsers the dial floats in the page's
  bottom-right corner. Click the button again, or **Bring it back**, to close
  it.
- **All timers.** The list button next to the pop-out button opens every
  speech and both prep clocks with the time used out of the time allowed and a
  bar for each, so you can check the round's timing between speeches.
  **Speech order** lists them as the round runs; **Most used** ranks them by
  time used. Click a prep row to show that clock on the dial.
- **Turn sidebar sections on and off.** The **Sidebar sections** button (the
  panel icon in the row of buttons at the top of the sidebar) has a checkbox
  for each section: Speeches & prep, Circular timer, Cameras, Shared headings
  & timers, and Open tabs. Unchecking one hides it; **Show all** brings
  everything back. The choice is saved on this device.
- **Same dial as the extension.** The browser extension's timer window uses
  this same circular timer face.

## Known gaps

- When two people press play or pause at almost the same moment, each device
  keeps whichever update reached it last, so they can disagree until the next
  press.
- The shared clock needs the round to have been saved (it has a round id), and
  every device must be signed in. A flow without a round keeps its timers on
  this device only.
- The Picture-in-Picture window needs the Document Picture-in-Picture API
  (Chrome and Edge 116+). Firefox and Safari get the in-page floating dial.
- Sidebar section choices are saved per browser, not to your account, so a
  second device starts with every section shown.
- In **All timers**, clicking a speech row doesn't switch to that speech; use
  the round group's speech list.
- The dial counts down on its own, but a speech's timer only rings and is
  marked done by the round group's speech bar for the speech in view.
