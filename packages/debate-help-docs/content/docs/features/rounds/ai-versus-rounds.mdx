# Online Debate Versus AI

==Lets a debater start a full practice round against an AI opponent== — pick a
format and side, then ==submit their own speeches one at a time, in turn order==.

It lives in the [Coaching](/docs/features/ai-coaching/coaching) page. For a full
timed round with an AI opponent, see [Practice vs AI](/docs/features/rounds/practice-vs-ai).

## What it shows

A form to start a round: a round ID, a format, and which side you're speaking
(formats with only one side, like Congress, only offer that side). ==Starting a
round lays out its full speech order==.

Once a round is active, ==the panel shows its full turn order== — each speech
tagged "You" or "AI" and marked Delivered, Next or Pending — and, when it's
your turn, ==a text box to type and submit the next speech==. On the AI's
turn, **Generate AI speech** ==asks the AI for its speech== and saves it in
that slot.

==Every delivered AI speech has its own **Regenerate** button==. It asks again
==from the same earlier speeches the AI saw the first time== and replaces that
speech in place — ==every other speech is untouched==.

**Download transcript** appears once ==every speech is delivered, giving a
plain-text transcript== labelled "You" or "AI" per speech.

The speech box also has a **🎤 Record** / **Stop recording** button ==to
dictate straight into it==, ==greyed out in browsers that can't do it==.

While you type (or dictate), a **Possible fallacies** hint appears under the
box when the draft reads like an ad hominem, straw man or false dichotomy.
It's a prompt to look closer before you submit, never a verdict, and nothing
is stored.

## Good to know

==Speeches are typed or dictated==; there is ==no way to transcribe an
already-recorded audio or video file==.
