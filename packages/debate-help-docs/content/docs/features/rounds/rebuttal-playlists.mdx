# Rebuttal Playlists

==Ten curated playlists of the greatest recorded rounds — finals, famous elims
and rounds decided on a kritik or on theory — by format, level and style==.
For each round you ==redo the final rebuttal, Aff or Neg, paired with another
student who takes the other side== (or the AI when nobody is around). ==Ask the
AI coach for help on your draft==, and ==the AI judge decides whose rebuttal
won and gives each side one thing to fix==. A rank and a progress chart show
how much of every playlist you've done.

Open it at the top of the **Practice Drills** tab of **Practice Debates**
(`/practice/debates?section=drills`), above the flow-based
[Practice Drills](/docs/features/tournaments-judge/drill-sets).

Filter the cards by level, then open one to see its ten rounds. Each row shows
the tournament, round and teams, which sides you've already redone, and ==how
many students are waiting for a partner on each side==.

## Running a drill

1. Pick a round and **Redo Aff** or **Redo Neg** — the 2AR / 2NR in policy, the
   2AR / NR in LD, the Final Focus in PF.
2. Choose **Find a student partner** (or **Join a waiting student** when someone
   is already waiting on the other side) or **Vs AI**.
   - ==With a partner, you're matched with the student who has waited
     longest on the other side of the same round==. If nobody is waiting, your
     drill stays open for 7 days for someone to join, and you get a
     notification when they do.
   - ==You can write and turn in your rebuttal while you wait==, and switch to
     the AI at any time with **Don't wait — debate the AI**.
3. **Watch round** plays the video right in the drill. Write your rebuttal
   (80 to 7,000 characters).
4. **Ask the AI coach** up to 3 times per drill. ==The coach points out what's
   working and the three most important fixes; it doesn't write the speech for
   you==.
5. **Turn in rebuttal.** ==Neither side sees the other's rebuttal until the
   drill is judged==. Once both are in, the AI judge decides and you're both
   notified.

Guests can drill against the AI; pairing with other students needs an account.
You can have 12 drills open at once and start 15 AI drills a day.

## Progress and levelling up

- ==A round counts as done on a side as soon as you turn in a rebuttal for
  it==, win or lose. The chart shows each playlist's rounds redone on one side
  and on both.
- ==Your rank climbs with every rebuttal==: Novice, JV (5), Varsity (15), Bid
  Debater (35), Elim Regular (60), Finalist (100), Champion (150) and GOAT
  (200 — every round of every playlist from both sides).
- ==Each rebuttal earns Debater Level XP== ("Redid a rebuttal"), and each
  judged win earns "Won a practice round".
