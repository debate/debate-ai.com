# Community Research Hub

A searchable directory of every shared research, collaboration, and
pre-round/practice space in the app, grouped into categories, so a debater
doesn't need to already know a route exists to find it.

- **Route:** `/community-hub`
- **Package:** `debate-card-search`

## What it shows

A search box (matched against a space's title or description) over a
static registry of every space, grouped into five categories — Evidence &
Cards, Team Collaboration, Pre-Round Intelligence, Practice & Coaching, and
Recognition & Progress. A **For You** strip lists already-starred spaces.

This is pure navigation: the hub itself persists nothing.

## Not in scope

A real browser extension for idea #7's remaining follow-up, and a dedicated
per-entry News Stream source, are both still open.
