# Gamification

==Daily quests, streak tracking, progress unlocks, and revision incentives==.

## Daily Best Card Challenge [#daily-best-card]

==Highlights the single most helpful card submitted each calendar day== (UTC),
and ==lets a day's winner be frozen as an official "announced" result==.

Find it at **/research/cards/best-card**.

### What it shows

**Today's leader**, an **Announce today's winner** action, a **Winner
history calendar**, **Announced history**, a **Best of the week** rollup,
and a per-day **comment thread**.

### Good to know

- ==Winners are not announced automatically==; someone has to press
  **Announce today's winner**.
- A comment's "Your name" field ==is filled in for you but can be changed; it
  isn't a verified identity==.
- ==Announced winners and the comment thread sync to your account== when you're
  signed in, but they are ==yours alone, not shared with the team==.

## Daily Quests [#daily-quests]

Team goals like "find 5 solvency cards" or "add 3 frontline answers today"
— ==a quest board that tracks live progress against real, same-day
contributions==.

Find it at **/research/cards/quests**.

### What it shows

Every saved quest's ==progress for the current calendar day== (UTC), an
optional expiry or repeat schedule, an easy/medium/hard difficulty ==worth an
escalating point value==, and a **Your streak** section that ==records today's
mission result and shows the reward==.

==Completing every quest on the board posts a celebration to the News Stream==
automatically.

### Good to know

- ==Anyone can count toward a quest==; there are no contributor permissions.
- Contributions made before quests tracked submission time and argument block
  ==don't count toward quests==.

## Progress Unlocks [#progress-unlocks]

==Shows every contributor's unlock tier==, the research-task skill level that
tier grants, ==every badge earned, their current streak, and how far they
are from the next tier==.

Find it at **/research/cards/progress**.

### What it shows

A roster table (tier, unlocked tasks, streak, tasks completed, badges,
next-tier progress bar), your own row highlighted when you're signed in, and
==a celebration pop-up when your own row newly earns a badge==.

==Badges you've already been celebrated for are remembered on your account==,
so ==a badge celebrated on one device isn't celebrated again as "new" on
another==.

### Good to know

==There are no contributor permissions== — signing in only highlights your
own row.

## Quest Streaks [#quest-streaks]

==A roster of every contributor's daily-quest streak and milestone badges==,
plus ==a button to work out today's mission result==.

Find it at **/research/cards/streaks**.

### What it shows

Current and longest streak, badges, a **streak freeze** ("grace day") action
==for a missed day (up to 2 per rolling 30 days)==, and ==an opt-in
streak-lapse reminder banner==. The mission-result history streaks and
badges are worked out from ==follows you to another device when you're signed
in==, for every contributor's row this browser knows about, and your own
streak freezes and reminder setting follow you too.

### Good to know

- ==Today's mission result is worked out when you press the button==; it
  ==doesn't run on its own==.
- A contributor's row only appears once some device has worked out a result
  for them; ==a row can't appear from nowhere==.
- Streak freezes and the reminder setting ==only sync for your own row==,
  never another contributor's.

## Revision Incentives [#revision-incentives]

==Rewards contributors for improving weak cards, strengthening citations,
and refreshing stale evidence==, and ==ranks them by total reward points
earned==.

Find it at **/research/cards/revisions**.

### What it shows

A leaderboard (revisions, rewarded count, reward points, weak cards
improved), a **Stale evidence digest** (most urgent first), and a **Recent
revisions** section with ==a word-by-word before/after comparison==.

==A revision earns points from a quality-score gain, a more complete citation,
and citing newer evidence== than the version before it.
