# Gamification

==Daily quests, streak tracking, progress unlocks, and revision incentives==.

## Daily Best Card Challenge [#daily-best-card]

==Highlights the most helpful card submitted each day==, with a winner
history calendar, a weekly rollup and a comment thread. Find it at
**/research/cards/best-card**.

- ==Winners aren't announced automatically==: press **Announce today's
  winner**.

## Daily Quests [#daily-quests]

Team goals like "find 5 solvency cards today", ==tracked live against
same-day contributions==. Harder quests are worth more points, and finishing
the whole board posts a celebration to the News Stream. Find it at
**/research/cards/quests**.

## Progress Unlocks [#progress-unlocks]

==Shows every contributor's tier, badges, streak and progress to the next
tier==, with your own row highlighted when you're signed in. Find it at
**/research/cards/progress**.

## Quest Streaks [#quest-streaks]

==Current and longest daily-quest streaks with milestone badges==. A
**streak freeze** covers a missed day (up to 2 per 30 days), and you can turn
on a reminder before a streak lapses. Find it at **/research/cards/streaks**.

- ==Press the button to work out today's result==; it doesn't run on its own.

## Revision Incentives [#revision-incentives]

==Rewards improving weak cards, completing citations and refreshing stale
evidence==, with a points leaderboard, a stale-evidence digest and
before/after comparisons. Find it at **/research/cards/revisions**.
