# Judge Profiles

==Shows every saved judge profile as a roster== — ==side-vote bias, average
speaker points, delivery-speed tolerance, theory receptiveness==, and
most-tagged paradigm — ordered by rounds judged.

Open it from the **Judge Profiles** tab of the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace).

## What it shows

A roster table, a "Log a judged round" form (==creates/updates a profile from
ballot history==), a **Bulk import (CSV)** section ==for pasting a whole
spreadsheet of judged rounds at once==, a logged-rounds list with
Edit/Delete/Undo/Redo actions, and a **Compare judges** section ==for
prepping a panel round== (side leans, recommended pace, theory risk, paradigm
conflicts).

## Good to know

- ==Ballots aren't pulled in automatically== — ==every round is entered by hand==,
  one at a time or in a batch with Bulk import.
- Profiles and their logged rounds sync to your account when you're signed in,
  but ==they are yours alone, not shared with the team==.
