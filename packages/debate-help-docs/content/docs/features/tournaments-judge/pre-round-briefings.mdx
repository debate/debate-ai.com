# Pre-Round Briefings

Shows every persisted Pre-Round Intelligence Panel briefing — opponent
scouting, judge tendencies, head-to-head record, and team prep notes
combined into one briefing per round.

- **Route:** `/practice/prep?section=briefings`, a tab of the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace) (the old `/practice/briefings` redirects there)
- **Package:** `debate-round`

## What it shows

A "create briefing" form (round ID, tournament, division, side, optional
opponent/judge pick, prep notes), every persisted briefing as its own card,
a "Log a round" form for this team's own round history, a "Download"
plain-text export, a freshness ("pending N hours") badge, and a manual
"Pairing schedule" section (account-synced across devices).

## Data flow

```
state/preRoundBriefings.ts (localStorage: preRoundBriefings)
  → buildPreRoundBriefingRecordFromDraft(draft)
      → buildPreRoundBriefingFromStores(...) — round/pre-round-briefing.ts
  → panels/PreRoundBriefingsPanel.tsx
  → apps/debate-ai.com/app/briefings/page.tsx
```

## Known gaps

No real data source for tournament results/ballots — scouting/judge data is
entered by hand; pairings have a dedicated manual-entry form as the
practical stand-in for still-blocked live Tabroom data.

## Debater Stats

Once you have logged rounds, the **Debater Stats** tab of the Prep Workspace
(`/practice/prep?section=stats`, also shown in the Coach Hub's Prep section)
turns them into your overall win rate, Aff and Neg win rates, results by
division, by argument and by case name, your current win or loss streak, and a recent-form trend (needs at least ten logged
rounds). Stats follow your account, like the rounds themselves.

If you fill in the optional **Words spoken** box when logging a round (or pick one of your saved flows
to count the words you flowed for the side you selected), a
**Speech length** section also shows your average words per round and how that
average differs between rounds you won and rounds you lost.

When the word count came from a saved flow, the logged round keeps a link to that flow and shows an
**Open flow** link in your logged-rounds list.

The optional **Arguments you ran** box takes comma-separated tags (for example
`Capitalism K, Topicality`). Duplicates (ignoring case) are dropped, and each tag
feeds the results-by-argument breakdown in Debater Stats.
