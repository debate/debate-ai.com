## Practice Drills [#drill-sets]

==Shows every drill set the AI Drill Generator made for you==, grouped by round —
==overview, frontline, cross-ex, and collapse-scenario prompts derived from
that round's flow==.

Find it in the Practice Drills tab of **Practice Debates**, in the sidebar's
**Practice Debates** section.

### What it shows

Each drill set shows as its own card with kind badges
(Overview/Frontline/Cross-Ex/Collapse) and a difficulty rating
(easy/medium/hard, ==derived from the argument's vulnerability score==). Each
drill has a "Get AI script" action, ==a "Mark practiced" toggle with a
progress meter==, and a "Review reminder" date field with a "Due" badge. A
"Practice tier" card ==feeds practiced-drill counts into the Progress
Unlocks tier system==.

### Saved to your account

==Every drill set — scripts, completion state, reminders — is synced to your
account across devices==.
Signed in, your synced drill sets are also listed under **Settings → General →
Saved to your account → Drill sets**, where each one can be opened or exported
as JSON. Opening one goes straight to that drill set: the card is scrolled
into view and outlined, or a note says it isn't on this device yet. Remove a drill set with **Clear** here: Settings offers no Delete for
drill sets, because a copy still on another device would sync it back.

### Known gaps

The "Practice tier" card ==counts only drills from this page==, not your
progress across every tool in Progress Unlocks.

## Judge Profiles [#judge-profiles]

==Shows every saved judge profile as a roster== — ==side-vote bias, average
speaker points, delivery-speed tolerance, theory receptiveness==, and
most-tagged paradigm — ordered by rounds judged.

Open it from the **Judge Profiles** tab of the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace).

### What it shows

A roster table, a "Log a judged round" form (==creates/updates a profile from
ballot history==), a **Bulk import (CSV)** section ==for pasting a whole
spreadsheet of judged rounds at once==, a logged-rounds list with
Edit/Delete/Undo/Redo actions, and a **Compare judges** section ==for
prepping a panel round== (side leans, recommended pace, theory risk, paradigm
conflicts).

### Good to know

- ==Ballots aren't pulled in automatically== — ==every round is entered by hand==,
  one at a time or in a batch with Bulk import.
- Profiles and their logged rounds sync to your account when you're signed in,
  but ==they are yours alone, not shared with the team==.

## Opponent Team Profiles [#opponent-team-profiles]

==Shows every saved opponent scouting profile as a roster== — overall and
Aff/Neg side record, ==a "notably stronger" side badge==, and ==the team's most
commonly run argument tags and cases==.

Open it from the **Opponent Team Profiles** tab of the [Prep Workspace](/docs/features/rounds/prep-workspace).

### What it shows

A roster table (rounds recorded, record, side record, common
arguments/cases), a "Log a scouted round" form, a **Bulk import (CSV)**
section, a logged-rounds list with Edit/Delete/Undo/Redo, a "Download
report" export, and a **Compare vs. opponent** section (==shared arguments,
us-only/opponent-only arguments==) with its own download.

### Good to know

- ==Round history isn't pulled in automatically== — ==every round is entered by
  hand or with a CSV import==.
- Profiles and their logged rounds sync to your account when you're signed in,
  but ==they are yours alone, not shared with the team==.
