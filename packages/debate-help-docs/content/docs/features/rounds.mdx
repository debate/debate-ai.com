## Create New Round — Registered-User Invites & Notifications [#round-invites-and-notifications]

In **Create New Round**, ==typing a debater, judge or spectator suggests
registered users==. Saving the round invites everyone on it: ==registered
users get an in-app notification, anyone else an email==. Your notifications
are at **/notifications**.

## Online Debate Versus AI [#ai-versus-rounds]

==A practice round against an AI opponent where you type or dictate your
speeches in turn order== and the AI writes its own. Any AI speech can be
regenerated, and you can download the transcript at the end. It lives in
[Coaching](/docs/features/ai-coach#coaching). For a timed round, see
[Practice vs AI](#practice-vs-ai).

## Featured Rounds [#featured-rounds]

==Real rounds anyone can open from the Featured section of the round
workspace==, with each speech's doc filled in and timers set to the format.
The first is the **NDT 2015 Finals** (Northwestern MV vs Michigan AP) at
**/debate/2015-ndt/northwestern-mv-michigan-ap**. Once opened, the round is
yours to flow and edit.

On that round's video page, a **Speech docs** tab beside the player lets you
flip through each speech's doc while you watch.

## Live Stream (Round Radio) [#live-stream]

Check **Live stream** when you create or edit a round and ==the speeches are
broadcast as audio, like radio==. Listeners tune in from the **Live rounds**
row in **Videos**, and the replay stays there afterwards.

- ==Each debater's browser broadcasts only their own speeches== while the
  speech timer runs; prep and gaps are never sent. Judges and spectators never
  broadcast.
- An **On air** badge shows while you're broadcasting; click it to share the
  listening link.
- ==Private rounds aren't listed== and can only be heard by people on the
  round.
- Listeners are a few seconds behind live.

## Practice Round Simulator [#practice-round-simulator]

==Set up a simulated tournament round with a format, side, AI judge paradigm
and AI opponent persona==, then track AI speeches, feedback and the judge's
decision. A **Compare your past attempts** chart shows your trend. It's in the
collapsed **Round simulator** section of [Practice vs AI](#practice-vs-ai).

### Matchup simulator [#practice-round-simulator-matchup-simulator]

==Pick any two ranked teams and see who the ratings favor==: single-round win
odds, odds on a 3- or 5-judge panel, and **Simulate 1,000 rounds**. It's in
Practice vs AI and on every team profile.

## Practice tools [#practice-tools]

==Ways to debate when there's no tournament==. Most don't need a flowed round,
and all except Practice Partners work signed out.

- **[Practice vs AI](#practice-vs-ai)**: a full timed round against an AI
  opponent, judged at the end.
- **[Online Challenges](#online-challenges)**: written debates against a
  player or the AI over several days.
- **[Practice Partners](/docs/features/team-collaboration#practice-partners)**:
  find a human opponent, judge or coach.
- **[Topic Sign-ups](/docs/features/team-collaboration#topic-sign-ups)**:
  sign up to debate any resolution, find partners, start a research squad
  and add research.
- **[Prediction Markets](/docs/features/ai-coach#prediction-markets)**:
  bet play-money points on rounds, tournaments and ratings.
- **[Word-Count Speeches](#word-count-rounds)**: cap speeches by words instead
  of time.
- **AI Judge Decision** (`/practice/judge-decision`): an AI decision for a
  saved round under its paradigm, or for speeches you paste in. Each
  pasted-speech verdict is saved with its speeches under **Saved verdicts**,
  where you can show it again, load its speeches back into the editor, or
  delete it. Signed in, your verdicts are saved to your account and appear
  on your other devices. The 30 newest are kept, and a verdict over 200 KB
  (very long speeches) stays on the device where you judged it. A saved
  round's decision history keeps its 20 newest decisions, ==also after it
  merges with your other devices==.

The AI judges come in three built-in styles (a tech-over-truth policymaker, a
critique judge and a middle-ground flow judge), and opponents in four built-in
personas, or you can describe your own.

**Suggested loop:** scout the judge and opponent in the
[Prep Workspace](#prep-workspace), rehearse in the simulator, pressure-test in
Practice vs AI at a higher difficulty, then flow the round and run the
[training tools](/docs/features/tournaments-debaters#training-tools) on it.

## Practice vs AI [#practice-vs-ai]

![Practice vs AI — the opponent picker, a live timed round, and the scorecard](https://i.imgur.com/2awjrUp.png)

==A full timed round against an AI opponent==. Find it at
**/practice/versus-ai**; no sign-in needed, but signing in saves rounds to
Debate History and earns points and badges.

Setup is five steps:

1. **Difficulty**, Easy through Legends.
2. **Topic** and side, and whether the round uses evidence. ==It starts on the
   national health insurance topic with you running single payer==; you can
   also write your own resolution.
3. **Opponent**: one of 13 personas.
4. **Prep**: the opponent finds cards and summarizes both sides.
5. **Arguments**: ==tick exactly which arguments you and the opponent run==.

Then debate through opening, cross-examination and closing, and ==get a
scorecard with each phase scored out of 10 and drills to practice==. Pick a
**Judge feedback style** and ask for a **Coach debrief** on what would have won
you the most points. With evidence, the opponent cites real cards by author
and year and never invents one.

- ==Uses your own OpenRouter or Anthropic key if you've added one==, otherwise
  the site's AI.
- ==Debate History keeps the arguments each side chose== on step 5, so you can
  see what you ran when you reopen a round.
- ==Search Debate History by topic or opponent, or filter it by result== (Won,
  Lost, Draw, Conceded, In progress). The line above the list shows how many
  rounds match and your overall record.
- ==Remove a round from your account== by opening it in Debate History and
  choosing **Remove from account**. It asks first and can't be undone; points
  and badges the round earned stay.
- Reloading mid-round resumes it. A round on consecutive days builds your
  streak.

## Round Cloud Save [#round-cloud-save]

==Save a whole round (tournament, debaters, judges and its flows) to your
account and load it on any device==. Use the cloud icon in Flow History's
**Rounds** tab, or **Save all rounds**. As with
[Flow Cloud Save](/docs/features/flow#flow-cloud-save), ==a save never
silently overwrites a newer copy==.

Signed in, your pinned rounds and your flow editor preferences (font, zoom,
theme, side colors, shortcuts) also follow your account.

## Round Sidebar Speeches & Captions [#round-sidebar-speeches]

The round's sidebar ==lists every speech with its timer and word totals==.
Click a speech to open it. From each row you can:

- **Grade** it on five 1–5 categories with a radar chart and a /25 total.
- **See signposts**: hover to see the H1/H2 headings from its speech doc.
- **See fallacy hints**: an amber button appears when wording looks like ad
  hominem, straw man or false dichotomy. ==Hints are prompts, not verdicts==.
- **Show captions**: a ticker of the recorded speech's words; click a word to
  jump there, or open the full transcript and split it into AI sections.
- **Ask for clarity** (judges only): pop a "be more clear" message on the
  speaker's laptop.

Your own speeches get an amber outline and a **You** tag. Captions need a
browser with speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari).

## Word-Count-Only Speech Format [#word-count-rounds]

==Type each speech against a live word-count limit instead of a clock==, at
**/word-count**. Manage per-style limits on the same page, see your
word-count trend and a fallacy-hint summary across saved rounds. Signed in,
rounds save to your account and appear on your other devices.

- ==A round deleted on one device comes back if another device still has
  it==; clear it on each device.

## Joined Round Recording [#round-recording]

**Join round recording** (in the speech recording menu) ==puts every recorded
speech in order in one MP3== and plays it back with captions and webcams.

- Optionally add spoken speech names and captions, or run the on-device
  **Moonshine** model (about 63 MB, downloaded once) for better captions.
- **Adjust speech starts** to mark where each speech begins.
- **Play with speech doc** ==scrolls each speech's doc to the card being read==,
  with live captions and an outline to flow from.
- **Record webcam video** records each debater's camera with their speeches.
  Camera tiles are labeled with your seat (1A, 2N, 1J…) from the round's
  invite.
- **Save to cloud** uploads the MP3, captions and clips to private storage
  only you can stream. Files over 95 MB are refused.
- ==The playback speed you pick is remembered for every recording==. Signed
  in, it's saved to your account, so your other devices play at the same
  speed. If you change it on two devices, the one you picked last wins.

## Online Challenges [#online-challenges]

==Challenge another player or the AI to a written debate on any listed
resolution, in any format==. Open it from the first tab of **Practice
Debates**.

- Four speeches alternate Aff and Neg (up to 9,000 characters each). ==Each
  side has 3 days to answer or forfeits==.
- After the last rebuttal, ==an AI judge picks the winner and explains why==.
- Finishing earns 20 XP, and a win adds 40 XP times your win-streak boost (up
  to 3×). Trophies include **First Victory**, **Hat Trick** and **Machine
  Breaker** (beat the Champion AI).
- Up to 10 open challenges to players and 10 new AI challenges a day. Guests
  can only debate the AI.

## Prep Workspace [#prep-workspace]

==One page for getting ready for a round==, one tab per tool. Open it from

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Pre-Round Briefings](/docs/features/tournaments-debaters#pre-round-briefings) | Opponent scouting, judge tendencies, head-to-head record and prep notes for each round. |
| [Scout-to-Strategy](/docs/features/ai-coach#scout-to-strategy) | Ranks your case options against the opponent and judge. |
| [Opponent Team Profiles](/docs/features/tournaments-debaters#opponent-team-profiles) | Records and the arguments each scouted team runs. |

The open tab is in the address, so you can bookmark or share it. Old tool
links open the matching tab.

## Rebuttal Playlists [#rebuttal-playlists]

==Ten playlists of the greatest recorded rounds, by format, level and style.
Redo the final rebuttal against another student or the AI==, then an AI judge
decides whose was better. Open it at the top of the **Practice Drills** tab of
**Practice Debates**.

1. Pick a round and **Redo Aff** or **Redo Neg**.
2. Choose a student partner (your drill waits up to 7 days) or **Vs AI**.
3. Watch the round, write your rebuttal, and **Ask the AI coach** up to 3
   times. ==The coach gives fixes but won't write the speech for you==.
4. **Turn in rebuttal**. Neither side sees the other's until it's judged.

Every rebuttal raises your rank, from Novice to GOAT (all 200 rounds from both
sides), and earns Debater Level XP.

## Rebuttal Redos & Fiat Points [#rebuttal-redos]

==After a round, the judge sends each debater a "rebuttal redo": an AI-drafted
assignment to re-give their last rebuttal, plus the judge's own instructions.==
Debaters record the redo and turn it in; the judge reviews it whenever they get
to it and leaves feedback. Open it at the top of the **Practice Drills** tab of
**Practice Debates**, or from **Tools for this round** in the round workspace.

**For judges.** Any round in your round workspace that lists your email as a
judge shows up here. ==The newest one is drafted for you automatically== from
your flow (each debater's 1AR/2AR/1NR/2NR, NR/2AR, or Summary/Final Focus).
For each debater you can read the AI assignment, add your own instructions,
change the speech time and due date, then **Assign** — or **Skip this
debater**. Turned-in redos appear under **To review** with the recording and
outline; rate it 1–5 stars and send feedback.

**For debaters.** Your redos appear under **Your redos** once the judge assigns
them. Record against the timer (going over is allowed — your judge will see),
add an outline if you like, and **Turn in redo**. You can turn in a new take
until the judge reviews it.

**Fiat points.** ==10 for turning a redo in, 5 more if it's on time, and 5 per
star the judge gives it.== Badges: First Redo, On the Clock (5 on time),
Comeback (5 reviewed), Judge's Pick (a 5-star redo), Rebuttal Rebuilt (20
reviewed) and Fiat 500. Each redo also earns Debater Level XP.

Debaters are matched to redos by the email on the round, so add their emails in
**Create New Round**. Someone without an account yet sees their redos as soon as
they sign up with that email.

## Round Timer & Shared Clock [#round-timer]

==Every device in a round shares the same clock==: when anyone plays, pauses,
resets or edits a speech or prep timer, everyone sees it. Late joiners catch up
without resetting anyone.

The sidebar's **Timer** section shows a circular dial: click it to play or
pause, and use the numbers around it for cross-ex and each side's prep.
==The pop-out button opens it in a small always-on-top window== (Chrome and
Edge). The **Sidebar sections** button hides sections you don't need.

- ==The shared clock needs a saved round and every device signed in==.

## Select YouTube Start [#youtube-speech-start]

==Use a recording of the whole round (a YouTube video or an MP3) for a
speech==. Open **Select YouTube Start** in a speech's ⋮ menu, pick or upload
the recording, and mark each speech's start and stop. **Suggest all speeches
with AI** ==fits the format's speeches to the video's captions and pauses==;
fix any row by hand, then **Save all**. Clips are saved in this browser.
