## Card Sharing & Co-editing [#card-sharing-co-editing]

==Send cards into a teammate's editor and edit a document together live==,
using the account you're signed in with. There's nothing to set up.

- **Sending cards:** add teammates in [Contacts](#contacts) and use **Share
  with contacts**. Cards you receive land in the **Receive** pill and wait up
  to a week.
- **Co-editing:** start a session from **Share with contacts** or **Start
  Collaboration Session**. ==Everyone sees each other's cursors live==, and
  people you invite can join from the link without an account.
- ==Everything is end-to-end encrypted== before it leaves your browser.

## Collaboration Prep Room [#collaboration-prep-room]

==A topic's prep space==: search its evidence and draft blocks, see research
tasks routed to contributors, who's active now, a shared checklist and an
activity timeline. Open it from the **Sprint** tab of the
[Research Workspace](/docs/features/research#research-collaboration).
Each teammate keeps their own copy.

## Contribution Leaderboard [#contribution-leaderboard]

==Ranks contributors by a blended helpfulness score==, with their tier,
contributions, streak and badges. Filter by time range or category. Find it at
**/coaching/leaderboard**.

## Contributions Feed [#contributions-feed]

==Submit a card, summary, highlight, annotation, argument or refutation==, and
see every contribution ranked by helpfulness with Like, Save, Endorse and
comments. Open it from the **Rewards** tab of the
[Research Workspace](/docs/features/research#research-collaboration).

## Top Contributor Awards [#contributor-awards]

==The top contributor in each category== (Best Evidence Finder, Best Explainer
and so on), with live standings, a Hall of Fame and peer nominations. Press
**Announce today's awards** to freeze the day's result. Find it at
**/research/cards/awards**.

## Group Challenges [#group-challenges]

==A coach sets a squad challenge==, "reach N contributions" or "reach N wins",
with a roster and a time window. Live standings mark the MVP with 🏆. Find it
at **/research/cards/group-challenges**.

## Review Queue [#review-queue]

==Peer review for submitted cards before they go live==: draft, in review,
changes requested, approved, published. Blocking comments must be resolved,
and you can't approve your own card. Open it from the **Review** tab of the
[Research Workspace](/docs/features/research#research-collaboration).

## Task Inbox [#task-inbox]

==Research tasks routed to contributors, grouped by topic==. Mark a task done
and ==a different contributor must verify it== before it counts. Includes
reassign, priority, **My tasks** and a team capacity view. Open it from the
**Routing** tab of the
[Research Workspace](/docs/features/research#research-collaboration).

## Team Collaboration Mode [#team-collaboration-mode]

==Leave live prep notes against a shared topic sprint==, grouped by topic with
status and assignees, plus an "active now" roster and an end-of-sprint
retrospective download. Find it at **/research/cards/collaboration**.

## Contacts & Shared Cards [#contacts]

==A contacts list on your account for sharing documents and co-editing==. Find
it at **/contacts** (tabs: Contacts and Shared cards).

- **Contacts:** add people by name or email; they accept or decline. **Block**
  removes someone and stops all sharing between you, without telling them. A
  dot shows who's online.
- **Sharing:** in Debate Editor, **Share with contacts** starts a live session
  and notifies each contact you pick (plus an email). The card appears in
  their **Shared cards** tab. Shares only reach accepted contacts.
- **Opening a share** creates a fresh document, so it never overwrites what
  you had open. Co-editing needs the desktop layout.

### Organizations [#contacts-organizations]

==An organization is a shared group, like a team or school squad==. Picking
one makes the Contacts list show its members and lets you share with them.
Owners and admins add members from their own contacts. There's currently no
button to create or switch organizations.

## News & Forums [#news-stream]

==What's new in the community, in one place==, at **/news**.

- **News:** new round videos, new caselist files and forum activity, newest
  first. **Following** shows updates from teams and schools you follow.
- **Forums:** anyone signed in can start a thread in a category (Policy, LD,
  PF, College, Tournaments and more). Sort by recent activity, new or top;
  like threads and replies; fold reply trees.

Likes and read items sync to your account.

## Practice Partners [#practice-partners]

==Find a practice opponent, judge or coach between tournaments==. Open it from
the **Practice Partners** tab of **Practice Debates**.

1. **Set up a profile:** switch on **Open to challenges**, **Volunteer to
   judge**, or both, and pick formats, styles, speed and level.
2. **Find me a match:** ==you're paired with one of the most compatible
   debaters, shown anonymously== (level, formats, styles, fit). **Find another**
   draws again.
3. **Send request** with a format, resolution and time. ==Names are revealed
   once they accept==, and accepted rounds get a webcam room code for the
   round workspace's Cameras panel.

- Judge volunteers see accepted rounds that still need a judge.
- **Volunteer to coach** or **Team that needs a coach** lists you on the
  Online coaching board.
- **Judge Pool**, below, lists judges for hire at tournaments by weekend,
  state and format. Pay is agreed directly; nothing is charged on the site.
- Guests can't take part, and blocked people never see each other.

## Topic Sign-ups & Research Squads [#topic-sign-ups]

==Click any resolution to sign up to debate it==: every past Policy, College,
LD and PF resolution and each of the 700 philosophical resolutions on
[Topics & Statistics](/practice/statistics) opens its board in the **Topic
Sign-ups** tab of **Practice Debates**. Each resolution has one board, however
it's written in the lists.

1. **Sign up:** choose **Debate it** (Aff, Neg or either side) or **Research
   only**, and add a short note such as your level and free times. Change it
   or **Withdraw** any time.
2. **Find partners:** everyone else signed up for that topic is listed by
   name.
3. **Research squads:** ==start a squad for the topic or join one== (up to
   eight members, two squads per person per topic). Members are told when
   someone joins; the last one out closes the squad.
4. **Add new information:** post evidence, arguments, links or questions to
   the topic for everyone debating it. You can delete your own.

- Without a topic, the tab lists the most active topics and lets you open a
  board for any resolution you type in.
- Sign-in is needed to see a board; guests can't post, and blocked people
  never see each other.

## Claiming a School Page [#school-pages]

==Coaches and school administrators can claim their school's page== and then
manage its teams, students and logo.

1. Open **Settings → General → My school** and pick your school (or create it).
2. Choose **Coach** or **School administrator**.
3. Verify with a code sent to your school email (not Gmail or similar), or
   explain your connection.
4. A moderator approves it, and you're notified.

Owners then see **Teams**, **Students** and **Set logo** controls on the
school's page (**/@** followed by its name). ==Every school ranked in the last
ten seasons has a page==, even without an owner.

## Team Calendar [#team-calendar]

==A month calendar for each group you coach==: schedule tasks, assignments and
group-only virtual tournaments on a date, assign them to members, and tick
off who has finished. Overdue work leads the upcoming list. Open it from the
**Team Calendar** tab of [Coaching](/docs/features/ai-coach#coaching).

- Groups are your [Coaching Programs](/docs/features/ai-coach#coaching-programs)
  rosters. Members are names, not accounts, so nobody is notified.

## Team Rankings [#team-rankings]

==Rankings of debate teams and debaters for every season since 2015-16==, for
PF, LD, Policy and college policy (NDT), at **/rank**. Each format has its own
address, such as **/rank/VCX** for Policy or **/rank/PARLI** for Parli, and
`?season=all-time` opens the All time view. Ratings
come from Tabroom results and openCaselist; see
[How Team Ratings Are Computed](/docs/features/ai-coach#bradley-terry-debate-rankings).

- **Division tables:** rank, places moved, rating (0–10.9), TOC bids, aff and
  neg win rates. Click any header to sort; the ⓘ explains each number.
- **Schools tab:** schools ranked by a **Balanced** score that favors a
  strong top three plus depth. College Policy keeps a **Top 10** of the last
  decade; each high school division has a **Top 20** chart: the twenty
  programs with the most points over the last 20 years, numbered by them (#1
  leads that window), with their rank charted over those same 20 years.
- **Legendary rows:** the top few in each list get a gold border and a
  crowned label; any row rated 8.0+ gets a thin gold border.
- **Past seasons:** college seasons from 2009-10 show
  [debatedata](https://debatedata.weebly.com)'s top 100 with a win probability
  calculator.
- **All time:** the first option in the season picker swaps the season for
  the greatest teams ever, as the whole page, in the event tab showing: the
  **All seasons** NDT ranking for College Policy, or the high school
  **Greatest of all time** top 100 for Forum, Individual (LD) and Policy.
  The Teams/Schools toggle stays: **Schools** shows the all-time NDT schools
  for College Policy and, for each high school event, the top 100 programs
  of all time by the same program points as the Top 20 chart and school
  pages. The high school team lists use the college list's weights, so
  college counts for more: each debater's own NDT points in full, plus high
  school finishes at half the college list's high school points (a TOC title
  is 10 to an NDT title's 30) and 0.6× before 2000.
  The link keeps it (`?season=all-time`).
- **Event tabs:** College Policy, then HS Policy, Forum, Individual, then
  Parli.
- **Parli:** the last event tab on **/rank**, after Individual, lists the
  National Parliamentary Debate League's national team and school rankings
  for a season, with ranks and ties as the league publishes them. It uses
  the same Teams/Schools toggle and season picker as the other events
  (every season the league has published, 2017-18 on; there is no "All
  time"), and the link keeps it (**/rank/PARLI**, `?by=schools` for
  Schools).

#### Team labels [#team-rankings-team-labels]

==Everywhere the site names a team, it uses the same label==: a bordered box
with the school's logo (or name) and the debaters' last names (or initials
when unknown). The border is blue for Aff and red for Neg. Ranked teams in the
video library also carry a **#N** badge.

==Hover any team label to see its stats card==: badges (👑 #1, Top 10 / 25,
🎖️ GOAT for an all-time top-25 NDT or high school pair or debater, national
titles, TOC bids), this season's rank, rating, rounds and aff/neg and elim
win rates, and its all-time records (NDT team and debater ranks, the school's
all-time NDT rank, high school all-time rank and best TOC/NDCA/Barkley/Cal
finishes), with links to the team and school pages. It works in the videos
library, watch pages, the mini player, the rankings tables and tournament
pairings and results. The all-time records load the first time you hover.

### Team and school pages [#team-rankings-team-and-school-pages]

==Every team and school has a profile at an `@` handle==: a team is its school
plus last names (`/@strath-haven-yang-smith`), a school its short name
(`/@michigan`).

- **Team pages** show a record card per division, grouped stats, a radar
  chart (with an optional school-average overlay), round videos and the
  team's **Caselist** section with disclosed rounds, files and cites.
- **Rating by month:** on the first rankings update of each month the site
  saves every team's rating and rank as that month's record, for good. Once a
  team has a saved month, its record card shows a small line of its rating
  month to month (green when it rose, red when it fell; hover for the
  numbers), and each of its rows on the school page shows the same line next
  to the rating. Teams with no saved month show no chart.
- **School pages** show logo, an info box (city, state, public or private,
  website), a high school's academics rank (its position in Niche's 2027
  combined public and private top 200, and its place among that list's
  schools in its state; "—" when it isn't listed), a college's academics
  rank (its U.S. News 2027 Best National Universities rank, for the 16 top
  debate programs that have one; no tile otherwise), the claimed roster, a scrolling row of
  round videos, and a **Historical legend**: program strength by season,
  championships, and NDT, CEDA, TOC, NDCA, Barkley, Cal Invitational
  (UC Berkeley) and Glenbrooks records. A high school's Championships card
  counts its TOC, NDCA, Cal LD and PF, and Glenbrooks Policy, LD and PF
  titles. Glenbrooks results the sources only imply are marked "probable".
  Program strength scores NDCA, Cal and Glenbrooks finishes like TOC finishes
  (a finalist as a runner-up), each in the season it belongs to.
- College pages have an **All seasons** ranking of every NDT team and school
  since 1947; high school pages have a **Greatest of all time** list per
  event.
- Turn off school logos or mascots in [Settings → Preferences](/settings/preferences).

#### Past-season team pages [#team-rankings-past-season-team-pages]

==College teams no longer ranked still get a page== from older sources
(debatedata, NDT history, CEDA champions), at an address like
`/@kansas-katz-robinson`. They show each season's rank and NDT and CEDA
results, but no radar or simulator.

### Following a team or school [#team-rankings-following-a-team-or-school]

==Click Follow on any team or school page== to add its new rounds, caselist
research, tournament results and monthly recaps to the **Following** filter of
[News & Forums](#news-stream). You can follow up to 50.

### Matchup simulator [#team-rankings-matchup-simulator]

==Pair any two ranked teams and see who the ratings favor==, on each side and
in prelims or elims, with **Simulate 1,000 rounds per side**. Each team's
rating blends the pair's own rating, each debater's other entries and the
school's average; the recommended mix is one click. **Settings → General → My
team** pre-fills your team, and every team profile has **Simulate vs. any
ranked team**.

When creating a round, picking a school suggests its ranked teams as badges
that fill in the debaters.

## Emails [#emails]

==The emails Debate AI sends==. Each has one button to the page it's about.

| Email | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited to collaborate on a file** | A contact shares a card or document with you |
| **Invited to a debate** | Someone adds your email to a round and you don't have an account yet |
| **Welcome** | An admin sends it to a list of addresses |
| **Coach features** | Written, not sent automatically yet |

Registered users invited to a round get an in-app notification instead.
Admins can preview every email and send tests from the admin panel's
**Emails** section.
