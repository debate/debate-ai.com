# Tools

## Debate Software 
- [Debate AI](https://debate-ai.com/) — Debaters in PF, LD & Policy can ==streamline AI research management, live speech sharing==, round and lecture videos, and rankings .
- [Tabroom](https://www.tabroom.com/) — ==The official NSDA repository for tournament registration and balloting history==. Essential for tracking standings and circuit shifts.
- [Open Caselist](https://opencaselist.com/) — ==The central wiki where teams disclose evidence==. ==Essential for scouting opponent strategies== and researching meta-trends.
- [CardMirror](https://debate-decoded.ghost.io/) — Cross-platform ==open-source ProseMirror editor matching Verbatim functionality== on Windows, Mac, Linux, and Web.
- [Verbatim](http://paperlessdebate.com/) — == Word template with ==high-speed formatting macros== and card organization used globally.
- [College Policy Debate Results](https://collegedebateresults.vercel.app/) — ==Comprehensive college policy debate results and career statistics==. Look up debater records, browse tournament standings and elimination brackets, and explore team performance with AI-powered database queries.
- [ebb](https://github.com/shreerammodi/ebb) — ==Open-source keyboard-first flowing editor==. Cascade reads and writes its .ebb files, so flows move between the two.
- [PrepSync](https://prepsync.net/) — ==AI-driven card and evidence finder==. Precision search utility for high-level competitive preparation.
- [Haku](https://haku.cards/) — Card search engine ==indexing 3.5 million openCaselist cards== (2013–2026) across high school and college policy, LD, PF, and NFA-LD. Filter by year, event, school, team, side, tournament, and round.
- [Debate Flow (Vercel)](https://debate-flow.vercel.app/) — ==Cloud-based digital flowing platform==. Supports collaborative notation and technical clarity in live rounds.
- [Calico Tab System](https://calicotab.com/) — ==Lightweight web tabulator== optimized for rapid small-circuit middle school and novice competitions.

## Learning Resources 

- [NSDA Resources](https://www.speechanddebate.org/) — The National Speech & Debate Association's ==official rules, topic announcements, and learning materials==.
- [Circuit Debater](https://circuitdebater.org/) — ==Massive repository of historical cases and documents== from elite circuit debaters across LD and Policy.
- [Basics Google Course](https://classroom.google.com/c/ODI2NDIzNzc5MDk3) — lessons plans with videos, 10 basics on argument refutation
- [NDCA Open Evidence (UIL guide)](https://www.uiltexas.org/speech/debate/ndca-debate-open-evidence-project) — Explanation of the NDCA Debate Open Evidence Project and ==how to access the camp evidence released each year==.
- [Champion Briefs](https://www.championbriefs.com/) — ==Topic analyses and evidence sets for PF and LD==, written by former national champions.
- [Baylor Briefs](https://www.baylor.edu/debate/briefs) — ==University-produced policy debate handbook== providing research on national topics.
- [Debatabase](https://idebate.net/resources/debatabase/) — ==Argument banks for hundreds of motions==, written by experienced debaters and judges. Good for fast case construction.
et a- [DebateUS](https://debateus.org/) — ==Daily-updated evidence files, topic analysis, and curricula== across PF, Policy, LD, Congress, and Extemp.
- [Debate101 Lecture Lab](https://debate101.org/#lectures) — ==Exhaustive lecture archive from modern students==, active in the debate space. Explores virtually all topics covering LD fundamentals.
- [Critical Discussions](https://www.kritikaldiscussions.com/) — A platform dedicated to exploring ==the intersection of critical pedagogy and competitive debate strategy==.
- [Vermont Debate Central](https://www.uvm.edu/cas/english/lawrence-debate-union) — UVM Lawrence Debate Union homepage. ==College files live on the NDT/CEDA openCaselist==.
- [The Debate Guru](https://thedebateguru.weebly.com/) — Community-driven site providing ==introductory case ideas and fundamental concept breakdowns for novices==.
- [LD Debate Prep](https://lddebateprep.org/) — A comprehensive repository of ==training materials specifically tailored for high-level Lincoln Douglas competition==.
- [DebateRounds](https://debaterounds.com/) — Comprehensive ==archive of high-level debate round recordings== across various circuits, providing essential technical film study.
- [Argument Institute](https://argumentinstitute.org/) — Platform dedicated to the ==structural integrity of argumentation== and high-level strategy and research methods.
- [Bill Batterman Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/@BillBatterman) — Exhaustive lecture archive from ==one of the circuit's most respected minds==, covering Policy and LD fundamentals.
- [Debate Drills YT](https://www.youtube.com/@DebateDrills) — Premier archive for ==technical drills and mechanics masterclasses==. Essential for circuit readiness.
- [Michigan Debate Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC2gnLZUFVVhjTy4UL9MhvRg/featured) — Strategy hub focusing on ==LD mechanics, technical drilling, and round evaluation==.
- [HSR Debate](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCUae8CTGOPAsx-SXL61BnpQ) — Video archive specializing in ==Policy debate theory and technical breakdown== of circuit-level rounds.
- [NSDA Training](https://www.youtube.com/@nsdaspeechanddebate) — ==Official training and final round videos== from the National Speech & Debate Association official archive.
- [One-World Debate](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCYPPO78Q16D9p4_bX0rR2QQ) — Technical analysis and instructional lectures on ==high-level argumentation and cross-ex mechanics==.
- [Proteus Academy](https://www.youtube.com/@proteusdebateacademy) — Strategy lab focused on ==high-level circuit trends== and conceptual edges in national competition.
- [Extemp Central](https://extempcentral.com/) — Portal for ==extemporaneous speaking topic questions==, strategy guides, and event news.
- [DebateDrills Resource List](https://www.debatedrills.com/external-resources) — ==A large curated external list of speech and debate resources==, maintained by a coaching organization.
