## List of Institute

- [NS Debate Camp](https://www.nsdebatecamp.com/glossary/tournament-of-champions) — Specialized training workshop with ==a renowned glossary for the Tournament of Champions== (TOC).
- [Lumos Debate](https://www.lumosdebate.com/) — ==Global debate camp and coaching initiative== focused on public speaking and competitive success across all formats.
- [Victory Briefs](https://victorybriefs.com/) — ==National hub for LD and PF training==, featuring specialized technical labs and leading publications.
- [Michigan Debate](https://michigandebate.com/) — ==The most rigorous Policy intensive workshop globally==, known for its extreme research standards.
- [Harvard Workshops](https://hdcsw.org/) — ==World-class summer workshops== taught by Ivy League champions and national circuit coaches.
- [Dartmouth DDI](https://www.ddidebate.org/) — The Dartmouth Debate Institute, ==one of the premier Policy workshops in the country==.
- [National Symposium for Debate (NSD)](https://www.nsdebatecamp.com/) — ==Residential and online camps in LD, PF, and Policy==, running since 2006.
- [DebateDrills Camps](https://www.debatedrills.com/) — ==Online and in-person LD and PF programs== with a published curriculum.
- [Summit Debate Workshops](https://summitdebate.com/) — High-level ==Public Forum, Congressional Debate, and Individual Events summer institute==.
- [Northwestern NHSI](https://nhsi.northwestern.edu/) — The National High School Institute — the 'cherubs' program, ==one of the oldest debate institutes in the country==.
- [National Debate Forum](https://nationaldebateforum.com/) — ==Elite Public Forum and Lincoln-Douglas summer debate camp==.
- [University of Michigan Classic](https://michigandebate.com/classic/) — Michigan's ==shorter-format programs== for debaters who cannot commit to seven weeks.
- [UT Dallas Debate Camp](https://debatecamp.utdallas.edu/) — ==Policy and LD summer programs== at the University of Texas at Dallas.
- [Wake Forest Debate](https://debate.wfu.edu/) — College program that also runs ==high school outreach and workshops==.
- [Georgetown Debate Seminar](https://debate.georgetown.edu/) — ==Long-standing policy institute== at Georgetown University.
- [Kentucky Debate Institute](https://ci.uky.edu/debate) — Summer camp ==hosted by the organizers of the Tournament of Champions==.
- [Capitol Debate Camps](https://capitoldebate.com/) — National summer camp provider running ==Public Forum, LD, and Speech workshops==.
- [UC Berkeley Debate Institute](https://berkeleydebate.com/) — West Coast institute specializing in ==Policy debate, LD framework, and Parliamentary debate==.
- [Emory National Debate Institute](https://barkleyforum.emory.edu/institutes/high-school/index.html) — Barkley Forum's high school institutes, ==running since 1966==.
- [Harvard Debate Council](https://harvarddebate.org/) — ==Advanced instruction in Policy, Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, and Speech==.
- [Ethos Debate](https://www.ethosdebate.com/) — Coaching and workshops focused on ==communication excellence and structural logic== in debate.
- [Champion Briefs](https://championbriefs.com/) — Topic analyses and evidence sets for PF and LD, written by former national champions.
- [Debate Resources LD](https://www.debateresources.com/lincoln-douglas) — ==LD specific materials== including briefs and technical primers for national circuit debaters.
- [CDA Financial Aid](https://www.cdadebate.com/financial-aid) — Essential resource for high school debaters seeking ==financial support and accessibility grants== for competition.
