## 1. CARDS: Crowdsourced Annotated Research for Debating Solutions

==The CARDS module provides a comprehensive environment for evidence management==, utilizing ==Auto-Highlight Agents that automatically highlight and underline text based on user-defined parameters== with customizable options, ==Full-Text Search for instant access across thousands of tagged evidence cards==, and ==AI-Powered Annotation for one-click summaries, warrant extensions, and logic-flaw detection.== Users can utilize ==Three Reading Modes—plain text, highlighted tags, and underline-only—for efficient review==, a Citation Auto-Formatter with one-click flow integration for speech documents, and a Mobile-Responsive design featuring full-screen overlays. Additionally, ==Auto-Research Outlines use specialized agents to automatically outline topics and continuously monitor for new quotes to keep research current.==

## 2. FIAT: Forum for Issue Analysis on Topics

==Recommendation Agents assist with research, summarization, flaw detection, and comparative quote analysis.== ==Judge Decision agents recommend multiple decision options, speech to flow, and quote to response options.== The ==Multi-column flow spreadsheet provides format-specific speech columns for PF, LD, Policy, and NDT== with inline editing. ==Shareable round URLs give every round a permanent link== for instant sharing with judges or teammates. Round management covers ==tournament setup, team pairing, judge assignments, and round notes in one place.== Speech docs offer a full markdown editor per speech with email sharing to judges and coaches. ==Smart timers provide format-aware prep and speech timing== with audio/visual alerts and auto-advance. Collaboration tools allow ==inviting judges and spectators by email with view-only and edit roles supported.== The ==Archive system lets users save, browse, and restore any past round with full flow history.== Finally, Mobile-optimized design features responsive swipe navigation between speech columns.

## 3. LEARN: Lectures from Educators, Archive of Rounds & Notes

The LEARN repository features ==College NDT Rounds, comprising approximately 1,400 entries dating back to 1995==, averaging over 50 new rounds annually, ==including all recent TOC and NDT elimination rounds.== It also hosts Debate Round Archives with ==approximately 350 Public Forum rounds (2015–present), 125 Policy rounds (2003–present), and 90 LD rounds (2019–present).== Users can access ==Instructional Videos totaling approximately 900 across 20 categories== such as topic lectures, camp coaching, kritik theory, counterplans, impact calculation, novice introductions, and speaking and delivery. ==Curated Top Picks offer approximately 100 hand-selected high-value rounds and lectures for focused study.== The platform includes a Searchable Grid with filters by title, channel, year, or view count, alongside inline YouTube playback with thumbnails. Additionally, it provides ==a Debate Dictionary featuring 200 terms with plain-English definitions== for theory, kritik, and procedural jargon; ==National Champion Records spanning 26 years (2000–2025) across NDT, Policy, LD, and PF==; and Team Rankings utilizing a TOC bid list and the DebateDrills Elo dual ranking system.

## 4. STREAM: Search with Top Result Extraction & Answer Model

==STREAM (Search with Top Result Extraction & Answer Model) is a comprehensive web search and research tool that enables inquiry-based analysis across 10 categories==, including academic, video, news, and file sources, ==by aggregating results from over 70 websites.== It provides ==advanced article previews that extract and summarize content from URLs, PDFs, and YouTube videos while automatically generating APA citations.== Users can ==select from multiple large language models== such as OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq, and Ollama, and can ==upload and query files including PDFs, DOCX, Google Docs, and multimedia content.== The platform also maintains a searchable history with optional privacy mode and ==enhances research by generating relevant follow-up questions to refine and deepen analysis.==

## 5. REASON: Research Editor for Annotated Summaries in Outline Notation

==REASON, the Research Editor for Annotated Summaries in Outline Notation, is built upon the TipTap editor framework== to provide a high-performance, Complex Rich Text Editor that ==functions as a comprehensive alternative to standard word processors.== The platform enables ==efficient knowledge management through a Nested Document Tree==, offering users drag-and-drop organization, tabbed navigation, and integration with custom storage sources. Document handling is streamlined via a Context Menu for rapid actions and ==robust Full-Text Search capabilities that allow users to locate information instantly by title or content.== Research workflows are enhanced by ==integrated AI Rewriting tools, which provide in-editor refinements to improve text quality.== For collaborative environments, the system includes ==granular Team Management and access controls.== Users maintain versatility through ==multiple View Modes, such as formatted, raw HTML, and Markdown.== The editor further provides high-precision Find & Replace functionality with match highlighting, alongside ==seamless Google Docs Integration for importing, exporting, and sharing.== To ensure data integrity, REASON utilizes Persistent Storage via SQLite, while power users benefit from extensive Keyboard Navigation for rapid command execution. Finally, ==specialized tools for Research Quotes enable users to capture, isolate, and categorize critical evidence and insights for synthesis and citation.==
