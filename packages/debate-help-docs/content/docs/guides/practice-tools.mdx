# Practice tools

The practice tools let you debate when there's no tournament: against another debater, against an AI opponent, in a simulated round
with a timer and an AI judge, or as a single speech under a word count. They're the *Practice* section of the
**[Coach Workspace](https://debate-ai.com/coach?section=practice)** and each has its own page. Unlike the
[training tools](/docs/guides/training-tools), most of them don't need a flowed round to start from.

## Two ways to run a round

**[Practice vs AI](https://debate-ai.com/versus-ai)** (`/practice/versus-ai`, [feature spec](/docs/features/practice-vs-ai)) is a full,
timed round against an AI opponent in real turn order. Pick a format and side, a topic, and one of thirteen
opponent personas across five difficulty tiers, then debate through timed opening, cross-examination, and
closing phases. At the end an AI judge scores the round and explains its decision. It's also on the dock as
**Practice vs AI**, and a round in progress can be resumed on the same account.

**[Practice Round Simulator](https://debate-ai.com/practice-round)** (`/practice`, [feature spec](/docs/features/practice-round-simulator))
recreates a tournament round's *conditions* — format, side, a timer, an AI judge paradigm, and an AI opponent
persona — and keeps your past attempts so you can compare them. Use it when you want to rehearse a specific
matchup or judge rather than play the AI's game.

## Practice against real people

**[Practice Partners](https://debate-ai.com/practice-partners)** (`/practice/partners`, [feature spec](/docs/features/practice-partners))
is the board for finding a human opponent or judge. Set up a practice profile — switch on **Open to
challenges**, **Volunteer to judge**, or both, and pick the formats, styles, speed, and level you're
comfortable with — and you're listed for other members to find, best matches first. Hit **Challenge** on
anyone open to challenges to propose a format, resolution, time, and optionally a volunteer judge; they get
a notification and can accept or decline. Accepted rounds get a webcam room code that both debaters and the
judge paste into the round workspace's Cameras panel. If you volunteer to judge, accepted rounds that still
need a judge are listed for you to pick up. It's the first panel in the Coach Workspace's Practice section.

**[Prediction Markets](https://debate-ai.com/practice/predictions)** (`/practice/predictions`, [feature spec](/docs/features/prediction-markets))
lets you bet play-money points (everyone starts with 1,000) on who wins a debate, who wins a tournament, or
whether a team's Glicko rating goes up. Prices move with every bet, and markets tied to rounds and events
hosted on the site, or to the rankings, settle themselves.

## Setting the judge and the opponent

Both round tools take their judge paradigm and opponent persona at setup time, from the same six built-in
AI judge paradigms and four built-in opponent styles, or from one you describe yourself.

| Tool | Route | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Judge Decision | `/practice/judge-decision` | Generate an AI decision for a round under its saved paradigm, grounded in the same flow summary the Argument Tree Outline shows. |

## Word-count speeches

**[Word-Count Speeches](https://debate-ai.com/word-count)** (`/word-count`, [feature spec](/docs/features/word-count-rounds))
replaces the clock with a maximum word count: one text area per speech in the chosen format, with a live
word-count badge recomputed on every keystroke. It's the right tool for written practice, for formats where
you'd rather cap length than time, and for drafting speeches you'll later deliver against the timer. Rounds
are saved to your account when signed in.

## Suggested loop

1. **Scout** the judge and opponent you're preparing for in the
   [Scouting section](/docs/guides/training-tools#scouting-section), and turn them into a paradigm and a
   persona.
2. **Rehearse** in the Practice Round Simulator under that paradigm and persona.
3. **Pressure-test** by playing the same side in Practice vs AI at a higher difficulty tier.
4. **Flow the practice round** in the round workspace and run the [training tools](/docs/guides/training-tools)
   on it — drills and coaching prompts work on practice rounds exactly as on tournament rounds.

## Tips

- **Sharing a section:** `/coaching?section=practice` opens the Coach Workspace on the practice tools.
- **Signed out?** Every practice tool except Practice Partners works without an account; signing in is what
  makes rounds, attempts, and word-count speeches follow you across devices. Practice Partners lists real
  people, so it needs a (non-guest) account to see or join the board.
