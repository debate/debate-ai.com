# Bible Citation Examples

This page demonstrates the Bible citation components for use in MDX content.

## Inline Bible Citation

Use `<BibleCitation>` for inline verse references that show the verse text on hover:

<BibleCitation reference="1 John 1:5" version="KJV" text="This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.">
  1 John 1:5 (KJV)
</BibleCitation>

The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it (<BibleCitation reference="John 1:5" version="ESV" text="The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." />).

## Bible Quote Block

Use `<BibleQuote>` for blockquote-style verse displays:

<BibleQuote
  reference="1 John 1:5"
  version="KJV"
  text="This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all."
/>

<BibleQuote
  reference="John 3:16"
  version="NIV"
  text="For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."
  caption="The gospel in miniature"
/>

## With Custom Qualification

<BibleCitation
  reference="Psalm 23:1"
  version="KJV"
  text="The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want."
  qualification="David, King of Israel"
>
  Psalm 23:1 (KJV)
</BibleCitation>
