1. **Mindset rejection does not end capitalism:** Rejecting a capitalist mindset does not end capitalism, and authors who live and work within it prove participation is not total endorsement; the neg instead breeds pessimism, critical ideas are already socially present, and Žižek’s “pseudo-activity” explains how symbolic rejection can replace material change.

4. **Institutions check atrocity:** O’Kane, Kershaw, and Pinker show institutions and pluralism constrain atrocities, while reframing away from the intended reader is like a malpractice doctor answering a patient with philosophy instead of treatment; Hitler’s absolutism and misapplied Marx illustrate the danger of systematizing social life into a total explanation.
5. **Technical policy is inescapable:** Decades of “rethinking” have not shown that consciousness change replaces policy; Linklater says technical policy is inescapable, Mertus says war and crisis preclude mindset movements, Won rejects a single root cause, people act on specific problems, the permutation remains possible, and “only a Sith deals in absolutes.”
