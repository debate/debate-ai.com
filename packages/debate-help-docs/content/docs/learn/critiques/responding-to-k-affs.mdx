# Responding to K Affs

### T-Framework
This is elaborated on [T-Framework](/t-framework) a lot more, but what T-fw really should boil down to are three standards/voters: SSD, TVA, limits/clash. All of these are great routes to go for to take out aff offense. T-fw debaters like to go for fairness, but an underutilized strategy and possibly a better one to go for is to beat the aff on their own grounds - education. Often, this is a much more compelling story than fairness because fairness is generic and can get boring and repetitive for experienced judges. Demonstrate how the resolution and debate provides a unique forum of education which is unique and key that outweighs the aff's education.

### Capitalism Ks
This is another generic strategy that negatives use against K affs: [Ks](Capitalism)(/capitalism-ks). Usually, the link would be something like the aff distracts us or destroys our ability to fight capitalism, which is the root of all evils and cap's impact outweighs. The alt would then solve the aff or be preferrable to the aff because it solves for capitalism which is a bigger umbrella issue.

### Non-Generics
The following strategies are not very common forms of answering Non-T affs, but can be interesting strategies.

1. **Truth Testing** - provide a counter role of the ballot via Truth Testing and demonstrate why the resolution is false via tricks and paradoxes. Be careful about this though - most judges don't like truth testing and is happy to disregard all of your tricks off the flow if the aff overviews on why tricks is harmful for their performance or identity of people they're trying to protect.
1. **Heavy presumption pushes** - most non-T affs don't do anything, and show why presumption flows neg because they can't have offense if they don't do anything.
1. **Turns** - just like how you would link/impact turn a kritik, you can do the same on a k aff. If the aff is mollow/psychoanalysis, you can turn their offense by saying psychoanalysis is harmful. For marxism, make turns as to why cap is better than marxism.
1. **Counterplans** - these can be quick, analytical, cheaty PICs that steal the aff offense but generate some sort of internal net benefit you can sit on for 6 minutes in the 2nr if dropped. For example, a common PIC vs Asian identity affs is fellow yellow, meaning that the neg endorses the reading of the aff unless against a fellow yellow debater because that causes the neg to negate their own identity which is harmful.
1. **Ballot PIKs** - advocate for why the aff's want or desire for the ballot is bad and harms their own movement.
