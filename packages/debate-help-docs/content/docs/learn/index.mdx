# Circuit Debater Library

This is a cleaned migration of the Circuit Debater MediaWiki export into Fumadocs-compatible MDX.

## Organization

- **Fundamentals:** Core concepts, speech strategy, round practice, and resources
- **Philosophy:** Ethical frameworks and philosophical literature
- **Theory and Topicality:** Procedural arguments and shells
- **Counterplans:** CP strategy, competition, and permutations
- **Disadvantages:** DA strategy and selected DA entries
- **Kritiks:** Kritik strategy and substantive positions
- **Past Topics:** One chronological, merged archive of historical topic pages
- **Reference:** Material that did not fit a primary category

## Editorial treatment

- Redirects, aliases, placeholders, site-admin pages, and very short stubs were omitted.
- Exact and near-duplicate entries were consolidated, keeping the more substantial version.
- Tournament-topic pages were intentionally merged into a single chronological document.
- Source wiki markup was converted to Markdown/MDX where feasible. Some complex legacy markup, citations, and links may need a final manual editorial pass.
