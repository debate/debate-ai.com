`@debate-ai/rankings` · [source on GitHub](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/tree/master/packages/debate-rankings)

What the web UI imports for rankings: the official loader (`loadRankingDataset(id, seasonYear?)` in
`extinction.ts`), which reads the live Elo ratings and TOC bids for HS PF, LD, Policy and
College Policy from 2015-16 on and merges the current season's aff/neg win rates from the typed CSV
loader over `debate-rankings-sync-data`' output; ratings moved onto the site's scale (`rating-offset.ts`), plus the
site-only team-label lookup (`findTeamRanking("Harker LL")` and friends) that matches a round
video's team to its rankings row, the tournament-entry lookup (`matchTournamentEntry`,
`rankingDatasetsForEvent`) that `/tournament` pairings and results use to show an entry's
rating in its own format's rankings, and past-season college policy tables (2009-10 to 2025-26)
scraped from debatedata.weebly.com (`college-history.ts`). Site additions live here so the
submodule never diverges from upstream.

Team labels (`leaderboard/team-label/TeamLabel.tsx`) carry the shared team stats hover card:
`TeamCodeLabel` opens it unless `hoverCard={false}` (set inside the GOAT tooltip), and
`TeamLabel` opens it when given `stats`. The card's body (`TeamStatsCardBody.tsx`) is a
`React.lazy` chunk, so it and the NDT / high school all-time tables load only on first hover;
the pure parts (code, badges, high school match) are in `teamHoverStats.ts`. `/tournament`
gets it through `<TournamentsApp TeamHoverCard>` (`debate-webview`'s `EntryHoverCard`). Known
gaps: touch screens get no hover, so phones don't see the card; labels without a rankings row
(e.g. the GOAT ticker, past high school seasons in listings) show only all-time records.

The rankings UI lives here too, under its own entry point `@debate-ai/rankings/leaderboard`
(the root stays React-free for the Worker): the `/practice/rankings` leaderboard, team and school
profile pages, the matchup simulator, team labels and school logos, follows and "my ranked
teams". Profile pages get their Videos section from the host through `ProfileVideosProvider`
(the grid itself is `ProfileVideos` in `debate-videos`, which depends on this package). Also owns
what used to be `debate-data-sync`'s rankings half: the ranking/results sync scripts
(`src/sync/`, including `OpponentTeamProfile`), the qualification and tournament-results
stores (`src/state/`), the topic formatters (`src/topics/`) and the bundled topics, champions,
tournaments, NDT history and school-name data (`data/`, exported as `./data/*`).

NPDL (high school parliamentary) rankings come from `src/sync/sync-npdl-rankings.ts`: it reads a
parliamentarydebate.org season page, finds the Google Sheets embedded under its `team-rankings` and
`school-rankings` headings, downloads each as CSV (the sheet's `pubhtml` table when CSV is blocked)
and keeps ranks exactly as published, ties included. `/api/rankings/npdl?season=2024-25` runs it
live in the Worker (6 h edge cache) and falls back to the bundled `data/npdl-rankings.json`, which
`bun run sync-npdl -- --season 2024-25` (or `--all-seasons`) refreshes from a machine that can reach
the site. 2024-25 should give 879 team rows and 26 school rows. `ParliRankingsSection` renders it in
the leaderboard's Parli tab (`/rank/PARLI`, after College Policy). Known gaps: season slugs are a hand-kept list (add next season's page to
`NPDL_SEASON_SLUGS`), and a page with two team sheets keeps only the first.

The leaderboard lives at `/rank/<format>` (`VPF`, `VLD`, `VCX`, `NDT`, `PARLI`), with only `?season=all-time`
and `?by=schools` in the query. `src/leaderboard/rankingsUrl.ts` builds and parses that address; the panel
writes it with `history.replaceState` so switching tabs does not remount the page, and the Worker
(`apps/debate-web/lib/redirects/rankings.ts`) 308s the old `/coaching/rankings?format=…`,
`/practice/rankings` and `/videos/rankings` addresses to it with the format moved into the path.

Also holds the school-claim rules and owner UI (`src/schools/`) that used to sit in `debate-webview`: claim
codes, member and logo validation, "My school" settings and the owner panel. The school profile page that
embeds videos stays in `debate-webview`.
