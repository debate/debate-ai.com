`@debate-ai/rankings` · [source on GitHub](https://github.com/debate/debate-ai.com/tree/master/packages/debate-rankings)

What the web UI imports for rankings: the official loader (`loadRankingDataset(id, seasonYear?)` in
`extinction.ts`), which reads the live Elo ratings and TOC bids for HS PF, LD, Policy and
College Policy from 2015-16 on and merges the current season's aff/neg win rates from the typed CSV
loader over `debate-rankings-sync-data`' output; ratings moved onto the site's scale (`rating-offset.ts`), plus the
site-only team-label lookup (`findTeamRanking("Harker LL")` and friends) that matches a round
video's team to its rankings row, the tournament-entry lookup (`matchTournamentEntry`,
`rankingDatasetsForEvent`) that `/tournaments` pairings and results use to show an entry's
rating in its own format's rankings, and past-season college policy tables (2009-10 to 2025-26)
scraped from debatedata.weebly.com (`college-history.ts`). Site additions live here so the
submodule never diverges from upstream.

The rankings UI lives here too, under its own entry point `@debate-ai/rankings/leaderboard`
(the root stays React-free for the Worker): the `/practice/rankings` leaderboard, team and school
profile pages, the matchup simulator, team labels and school logos, follows and "my ranked
teams". Profile pages get their Videos section from the host through `ProfileVideosProvider`
(the grid itself is `ProfileVideos` in `debate-videos`, which depends on this package). Also owns
what used to be `debate-data-sync`'s rankings half: the ranking/results sync scripts
(`src/sync/`, including `OpponentTeamProfile`), the qualification and tournament-results
stores (`src/state/`), the topic formatters (`src/topics/`) and the bundled topics, champions,
tournaments, NDT history and school-name data (`data/`, exported as `./data/*`).

Also holds the school-claim rules and owner UI (`src/schools/`) that used to sit in `debate-webview`: claim
codes, member and logo validation, "My school" settings and the owner panel. The school profile page that
embeds videos stays in `debate-webview`.
