# REASON docs sidebar — files on the document routes, opened in Debate Editor

The Files / Topic Starters / Open Tabs panels that sit above the tool nav
tree, and the URL round trip that turns a click on one of them into "that
file, loaded in Debate Editor, in the main column."

- **Routes:** `/cards` (and below it) and `/reason-editor` — the routes the
  documents are the subject of, per
  `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/sidebar-routes.ts`
- **Panels:** `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/docs/`
  (`ReasonDocsSidebarPanels`, `FileTree`, `TopicStarterTree`,
  `OpenTabsPanel`), over `ReasonDocsProvider` in the root layout
- **URL rules:** `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/route-selection.ts`,
  over the filename paths in `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/doc-path.ts`
- **Panel stack and its height split:**
  `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/panel-layout.ts`
- **Stored file format:** `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/debate-editor/` (`format.ts`,
  `content-format.ts`, `stored-cmir.ts`)
- **Public lookup by name:** `/api/topic-starters/by-path`
- **Where they show:** `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/sidebar-routes.ts`
- **Editor:** `/reason-editor` (`debate-editor`'s Debate Editor embed)

## Where the panels mount

`ReasonDocsSidebarPanels` is one component mounted in two places:

1. `AppSidebarShell`'s `<aside>`, on the routes `showsReasonDocsPanels`
   admits — `/cards` (the dock's "Shared" destination, and any page below
   it) and `/reason-editor`, whose desktop file navigation *is* this
   sidebar, the editor route having no `<aside>` of its own.
2. `/reason-editor` itself, as a collapsible strip below `md`, since that
   sidebar is `hidden md:flex`.

They are deliberately absent everywhere else. The panels used to ride in
every sidebar, `/videos` included — which took a third mount, an app-owned
`docsSlot` on `LecturesPage`, since `/videos` renders its own sidebar rather
than the shell. That put a document tree above the video library's own nav
on a page that is not about documents; the slot is gone and the videos
sidebar is the dock plus the video nav
(`packages/debate-videos/test/lectures-sidebar.test.tsx`). The trade is that
a file opened from `/cards` is no longer one click away after hopping to the
video library — the dock hop back is.

In the sidebar the panels sit under the dock and above the nav tree: the
tree is long enough (a section auto-expands to show where you are) that
anything below it starts under the fold.

## What else the `/cards` sidebar carries

Nothing. Clicking "Shared" in the dock otherwise lands on a column holding four
navigations at once — the document panels, the whole Apps / Coaching / Research
/ Practice tree, the glossary and rankings links, and the site footer — on a
page that is about the first of them.

`showsCardsOnlySidebar` (same module) marks the `/cards` subtree, and there
`AppSidebarShell` renders the dock and the docs panels and stops — no nav tree,
no reference links, no footer. Dropping the tree is also what frees the
column's height for the panels to divide (next section); narrowing it to its
Research section, as an earlier pass did, still left a submenu of cards links
under the file tree and still made the column something to scroll. The Research
entries are not lost: they are in the full tree that every other tool route's
sidebar carries, one dock hop away. The dock itself stays — it is the control
the reader clicked "Shared" in, and the way back to `/videos`.

`/reason-editor` keeps the full tree. It is reached *from* those other sections
rather than being one of them, and unlike `/cards` its sidebar is not only the
documents — the tree under the panels is how a reader leaves the editor for the
tool they were writing for.

The section is expanded by default on both routes — they are the only two it
mounts on, and on `/cards` these panels are the whole sidebar, so a collapsed
"Documents" row would leave that column empty but for the dock. A reader's own
collapse still wins and sticks, per-device, in `localStorage`. Nothing is
fetched until the section is actually open, so collapsing it costs no document
request.

## Dividing the column's height

With nothing under them on `/cards`, the panels take the column's leftover
height rather than a fixed slice of it — `AppSidebarShell` passes `fill`, and
that route's `<aside>` stops scrolling as a whole (a scrolling parent has no
height to hand a flexible child). Each panel then scrolls inside its own
share.

The shares are the reader's to set. The enabled panels are a vertical
`react-resizable-panels` group with a draggable separator on every seam, so a
reader with forty files can give the tree most of the column and leave Open
Tabs a couple of rows — sizes that used to be fixed in CSS, with the lower
panel capped at 40%. A panel cannot be dragged below 72px, which is a heading
plus a row: less than that is a sliver that says nothing and is too thin to
aim at to drag back.

A drag is remembered per-device in `localStorage`
(`reason-editor-sidebar-layout`), alongside the panel choice and the collapse
state. Only a drag is stored — the group reports its computed layout on mount
and on a constraint recompute through the same callback, and writing those
back would overwrite a deliberate split. A layout belongs to the panel set it
was dragged on, so switching a panel on reopens the group at the sizes saved
for *that* set instead of squeezing the new panel into what the others left;
a set with no saved sizes opens on the defaults, which weight Files 3 against
2 for each of the others. The stack order, the weights, the drag floor and the
parsing of a stored split are `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/panel-layout.ts`, kept out of
the component so they can be unit-tested without rendering a sidebar
(`packages/debate-reason-docs/test/lib/reason-docs/panel-layout.test.ts`); reading `localStorage`
stays in the component, alongside the panel-choice and collapse-state reads it
already does.

Off `/cards` the box keeps its definite height (`h-[380px]`, capped against
the viewport), because something else sits below it in a scrolling column:
`/reason-editor`'s sidebar has the nav tree under the panels, and below `md`
they ride as a strip above the editor. The separators work there too — they
just divide a fixed box instead of the column.

## Tabs: documents and topic starters together

`openTabs` holds owned documents and public topic starters in one strip
order. A topic starter's tab id is its row id negated
(`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/tab-ids.ts`) — the two tables both number rows from 1, so
negating keeps the ids apart while the close/reopen transitions in
`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/tab-actions.ts` stay plain number arithmetic. The provider's
`activeTab` is the tab on screen; `activeId` (the owned document) and
`topicDocument` (the starter) are derived from it, so every caller that only
cares about owned documents keeps reading `activeId`. Starters opened this
session are kept by id in `topicTabItems` — not looked up in `topicItems`, as
a shared link can open one the catalogue never listed — and stay there after
their tab closes so "Undo close" can bring them back. `selectTopicDocument`
adds a tab, or switches to the existing one.

Undo history is per tab because the Debate Editor singleton
(`debate-editor/overlay/react/singleton.ts`) keeps each `contentKey`'s
`EditorState` when another key claims the view, and restores it when that key
comes back — provided the host's content is still what that state last held
(or reported). Anything else mounts fresh. Up to 5 states are kept
(`SAVED_STATES_LIMIT`), least recently mounted dropped first.

### Suspended tabs

Only five tabs stay live (`MAX_LIVE_TABS` in
`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/tab-suspension.ts`, the one on
screen included). The provider keeps a least-recently-used order of tabs
(`tabUsage`, touched whenever `activeTab` changes, pruned of closed tabs) and
exposes every open tab past the five most recent as `suspendedTabs`, in strip
order; the active tab is never one of them. When a tab becomes suspended the
provider frees what it held:

- Debate Editor's saved `EditorState` for its `contentKey` (the whole doc plus
  undo history) and the HTML kept to recognise its echoes, through
  `discardSavedState` (`debate-editor/overlay/react/singleton.ts`), loaded on
  demand by `lib/debate-editor/editor-memory.ts` so the provider doesn't import
  the editor shell. The `contentKey` comes from `editorContentKey` in
  `tab-ids.ts`, the same function the editor screen mounts with.
- Its entry in `openHtmlRef`. A `.cmir` edit still waiting for its debounced
  encode is encoded synchronously first and written to the row (and the save
  queue), so the stored content the tab comes back from has every edit. If the
  engine isn't loaded yet the HTML copy is kept and freeing is retried on the
  next change.

The row itself stays in `documents` (file search and the tree read it), and the
tab stays in `openTabs`: `EditorTabStrip` dims it with a moon and lists it in a
"Suspended" dropdown at the strip's end, with "Close suspended tabs". Selecting
one is an ordinary `selectTab`; the editor mounts it fresh from
`documentHtml(row)`, and the next least recently used tab is suspended in turn.

## Opening a file

Picking a file — an owned document, a public topic starter, or an open tab —
does two things:

- sets it active in `ReasonDocsProvider`, so a client-side hop switches the
  editor instantly; and
- routes to `/reason-editor?doc=<file name>` (or `?topic=<file name>`), so
  the selection is addressable.

Provider state alone covers the hop but not a reload, a pasted link, or a
hard navigation — `/videos` is its own layout branch and can boot the editor
with an empty provider. Carrying the file in the query is what makes "click a
file, get *that* file in Debate Editor" hold from every sidebar rather than
only from the ones that hop client-side.

The name is the title slugified (`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/doc-slug.ts`): lowercased,
accents folded, apostrophes dropped, everything else non-alphanumeric
collapsed to single hyphens, capped at 80 characters on a word boundary, and
`untitled` when a title leaves nothing behind. Titles are not unique and ids
are, so two files that slugify the same are told apart by a `~<id>` suffix —
`impact-turns~d12` for document 12, `impact-turns~t7` for topic starter 7.
No title can forge one: `~` is stripped out of titles by the same slugifier.

Already on the editor route, a pick routes nothing at all. `/reason-editor`
and `/reason-editor/<slug>` are separate Next routes, so routing between
them would remount Debate Editor — and its undo history — on every tab switch;
the provider already has the file open, and `ReasonDocsRouteSync` renames
the address bar in place with `history.replaceState`. That same rewrite is
what upgrades an older `?doc=<id>` / `?topic=<id>` link (still read, and
still resolved) to the named path once the catalogue says what that file is
called, and what keeps the address on a file that is renamed while open. Any
other query parameter is kept — `?share=` and `?shareWith=` are read by the
same page.

`ReasonDocsRouteSync` (mounted once on `/reason-editor`, inside a
`<Suspense>` since it reads `useSearchParams`, renders nothing) reads it
back. The rules themselves are pure functions in
`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/route-selection.ts`, shared with the sidebar so the two
ends of the round trip cannot drift, and unit-tested in
`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/__tests__/route-selection.test.ts`.

Resolution order, per URL:

1. the URL's `topic` name, if it names a live topic starter;
2. the URL's `doc` name, against the reader's own tree and then the public
   one — a shared link names a file, not a table, so the same URL has to work
   for the person who owns the file and the person who only has the public
   copy;
3. a server lookup for a name neither tree placed (below);
4. otherwise, and only when nothing is open, the first file — so the reader
   lands on something readable instead of an empty pane.

A name that matches nothing anywhere (a deleted file, someone else's private
document) falls through to that same fallback rather than erroring. The deep
link applies once per URL: after it opens its file the reader can pick
another from the sidebar without the stale query dragging them back, and the
sidebar rewrites the query on every pick, which re-arms it.

The deep-link rule and the first-file fallback are one function on purpose.
As two React effects they raced: the fallback's closure still saw no
selection in the commit where the deep link opened its file, and opened the
first file over the top of it.

## The URL is the file's name

`?doc=impacts/warming-1ac`, not `?doc=34`. `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/reason-docs/doc-path.ts`
builds a path from the row's own title and the folders above it, and reads
one back; the sidebar and the public lookup route both go through it, so the
two ends cannot drift. It owes callers three things:

- **Stable.** The file extension is dropped before slugging, so the `.docx` a
  reader uploaded and the `.cmir` it is stored as address the same file.
  Accents fold (`Réchauffement` → `rechauffement`) rather than collapsing to
  a row of dashes.
- **Unique.** Two files can share a name; two URLs cannot. Duplicates are
  disambiguated by row id, and the *lower* id keeps the bare path, so a link
  in someone's history doesn't start opening a different file because a newer
  file was uploaded or the sidebar re-sorted.
- **Forgiving.** A URL that names only the file (`?doc=warming-1ac`) resolves
  when exactly one file ends that way — two candidates fall through to the
  fallback rather than a coin flip. A folder resolves to the first file
  inside it (descending into subfolders), so a folder link lands on something
  readable. And a numeric ref still resolves as a row id, so links minted
  before paths keep working. A rename is forgiven once: `documents.previous_slug`
  holds the file's own slug from just before its last rename, and a URL that
  matches neither the current path nor any other file's resolves against that
  instead before falling through to the fallback.

## A public file's URL works for anyone

The browsable catalogue (`/api/topic-starters`) is capped at 100 rows for the
sidebar's sake, so it cannot be what a link resolves against: a reader
following a shared link may be signed out, with no documents of their own and
a catalogue that never held the file. When neither tree places the name,
`ReasonDocsRouteSync` asks `/api/topic-starters/by-path?path=<name>` once,
which searches every published row (using the same `findItemByRef`) and
returns the one file, content included, so the editor opens it in the same
trip. Published rows only, in both the match and the ancestry. A lookup that
comes back empty is remembered for that URL, so the link falls through to the
normal fallback instead of asking again on every render.

## The Documents row

Above the panels sits the row of actions on files: New file, New folder,
Upload, and — on the editor route, signed in, with a document open — **Share
with contacts** (`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/editor/ShareWithContacts`, drawn in its
`icon` variant so it matches the three beside it). Sharing was a labelled
button in the editor's own header strip; it is an action on the open file, so
it belongs in the row that file's other actions are in. It is scoped to the
editor route because sharing starts a live co-editing session through the
Debate Editor engine, and that page is what mounts the engine — on `/cards` the
control would open a dialog that could only fail.

## Uploading a file

The Documents header has an Upload button, and the file tree takes a drop
from the desktop — onto a folder to import there, anywhere else to import at
the root. Both go through `importFiles` on the provider.

Every upload is stored as a Debate Editor native file, whatever it arrived as
(`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/debate-editor/stored-cmir.ts`): a `.docx` through `docxToCmir`, a `.cmir`
kept byte-for-byte (parsed first, so a mislabelled file is refused now rather
than on open), and `.html` / `.md` / `.txt` parsed through the schema. The
card parser is not in this path at all — `.cmir` is the only shape that holds
what Debate Editor's OOXML reader gets out of a Verbatim document, and the HTML
the app would otherwise keep flattens the outline, comments, images and marks
away.

The row keeps the name it was uploaded under, extension included, so the
`.docx` you dropped is the `.docx` you see in the sidebar (with its type on a
badge); clicking it opens it in Debate Editor, decoded from the stored `.cmir`.
The first file of an upload is opened and routed to, which makes uploading
and clicking one file the same gesture. One file that cannot be converted
doesn't lose the rest of a drop: it comes back as a message under the tree,
naming the file and the remedy.

Editing an imported file keeps it a `.cmir`. The editor reports HTML, so the
live copy of an open file is held as HTML in a ref and re-encoded on its own
600ms debounce in front of the save queue's — gzipping a card document on
every keystroke would be felt. An edit still waiting to be encoded when the
page goes away is encoded synchronously by the same `pagehide` /
tab-hidden handler that flushes the save queue, so the last half-second of
typing isn't lost. A re-encode that fails leaves the stored file alone rather
than overwriting a readable `.cmir` with a failure.

`documents.format` records which shape a row holds, exactly as
`topic_starter_items.format` does (`drizzle/0038_document_cmir.sql`);
documents written in the editor stay HTML and are untouched by any of this.

## Read-only topic starters

A topic starter opens in the same Debate Editor embed as an owned document, but
read-only: no toolbar, no AI tools, no `onChange`, and a `readOnly` title —
no status line above the editor; its tab names it. It gets its own `contentKey`
namespace (`topic-<id>`) so the engine treats it as a distinct claim from an
owned document that happens to share the id — the same reason `?doc=7` and
`?topic=7` are different URLs.

## Topic starters are stored as `.cmir`

The admin importer on `/admin` takes a `.docx` (or a `.zip` of them) and
stores each one as a Debate Editor native file — `.cmir`, gzipped JSON,
base64-encoded into the row's `content` column, with `format` set to
`"cmir"`.

The conversion is Debate Editor's own OOXML importer (`docxToCmir` in
`packages/debate-editor/src/native/convert.ts` — `fromDocxFull` piped into
`serializeNative`, the same pair the desktop bulk converter runs), wrapped
for the route by `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/topic-starters/import.ts`. What that
buys over the card HTML the importer used to store is everything HTML has no
element for: the Verbatim outline (pocket / hat / block / tag / analytic) as
typed nodes rather than `<h1>`…`<h4>`, comment threads, inline images, and
the named character styles that distinguish a cite from an emphasis from a
plain underline.

Reading it back is client-side: `storedContentToHtml`
(`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/debate-editor/stored-cmir.ts`, aliased for this table as
`topicStarterHtml`) parses the file with `parseNative` and serializes it
through the schema's own `toDOM` specs for the embed's `content` prop,
memoized per file on `/reason-editor`. Doing it in the browser keeps the
catalogue endpoint — which returns up to 100 rows — from converting files
nobody opens.

The rules are the app's rather than this table's — the reader's own uploads
are stored the same way — so they live in `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/debate-editor/` and
`packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/topic-starters/format.ts` is the Topic Starter names for them.

Rows imported before this carry HTML and still open: `format` says which a
row is, and content that arrives without its row falls back to sniffing the
bytes for the gzip magic. A `.cmir` that will not parse renders a notice
naming the failure rather than a blank document.

## One tree per topic

`topic_starter_items.topic_code` (added on first use by
`ensureTopicStarterColumns`) files a row under one resolution — the same
topic codes the addresses use (`vcx27`, `lda27`). A row whose code is null
inherits its nearest coded ancestor's, and a row with no coded ancestor
belongs to `DEFAULT_TOPIC_CODE`; that rule, and the filter both sides use,
are in `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/topic-starters/topic-scope.ts`
(`effectiveTopicCodes`, `filterItemsByTopic`).

LD and PF codes put the letter before the year (`lda27`, `pfb24`). Codes
from before that change (`ld27a`) still match `TOPIC_CODE_SOURCE`;
`normalizeTopicCode` in `topic-doc-paths.ts` turns them into the new shape
wherever a code is read (addresses, form fields, stored rows), and
`ensureTopicStarterColumns` rewrites stored rows once per isolate.

- **Import.** `TopicStarterUpload`'s Format / Year / Topic dropdowns send
  `topicCode`; `POST /api/admin/topic-starters` validates it against
  `topic-codes.ts` (an unknown code is a 400 `unknown-topic`), stamps it and
  the topic's year on every row it creates, and rebuilds the stored file
  index so the files show up at once rather than after the daily cron.
- **Sidebar.** `ReasonDocsSidebarPanels` filters `topicItems` to the code
  in the address (or the default) before handing them to
  `TopicStarterTree`, and passes that code into `createTopicFolder`,
  `publishToTopics` and `organizeTopicStarters`.
- **Changes.** `create-folder`, `submit` and `reorganize` carry an optional
  `topicCode`. A `move` stamps the row's effective code on it, so a file
  dragged out of an LD folder to the top level stays in the LD tree; a paste
  (`copyTopicStarterItem`) does the same for the copy's root. A reorganize
  with a code plans over that topic's rows only, so its `Aff`/`Neg` folders
  are that topic's own.
- **File index.** Entries carry `topicCode`; the index version is 2, so a
  copy stored before this (which would put every row in the default tree)
  is ignored and refetched.

## File stats on hover

Every file in both trees shows, on hover, its last modified date, author,
year, and size: pages, quotes, blocks and characters. The numbers are kept in
D1 so hovering never loads a file.

- **Columns.** `documents` and `topic_starter_items` both have `quote_count`,
  `block_count`, `page_count`, `char_count` (integers, null until counted) and
  `author` (text). `ensureTableColumns` adds them to an older D1 on first use
  (`ensureDocumentColumns` in `apps/debate-web/lib/file-stats/columns.ts`,
  `ensureTopicStarterColumns` for Topic Starters).
- **Counting.** `packages/debate-reason-docs/src/lib/file-stats/file-stats.ts`.
  A `.cmir` is gunzipped with `DecompressionStream` and its ProseMirror JSON
  walked: `card` nodes are quotes, `block` nodes are blocks, text node lengths
  sum to characters. Legacy HTML counts `div.pmd-card` / `h3.pmd-block`
  (falling back to `h4` / `h3`). Pages are an estimate: characters divided by
  3,000, at least one for any text.
- **When it runs.** On every write that sets content: `POST` and `PUT
  /api/doc/documents`, the admin `.docx`/`.zip` import, admin `items`, a
  submitted file, and a market purchase copy. A copied Topic Starter keeps
  the original's numbers and author.
- **Author.** A Topic Starter import reads the `.docx`'s `dc:creator`
  (`readDocxAuthor` in `@debate-ai/card-parser`, placeholders such as "User"
  skipped), else the importing admin's name. My Files rows take the uploaded
  `.docx`'s author, a copied Topic Starter's author, or the owner's name.
- **Backfill.** `apps/debate-web/lib/file-stats/backfill.ts` counts rows still
  at null in batches of 20 and fills `documents.author` from the owner's
  account name. The daily cron runs it for 15 seconds before rebuilding the
  Topic Starters index; the admin Imports tab's **Count file stats** button
  (`POST /api/admin/file-stats`) runs it in 20-second rounds until done.
- **Indexes.** Both file indexes carry the stats, author and a date
  (`updatedAt` for Topic Starters, `createdAt` for My Files); the index
  versions were bumped (Topic Starters 3, My Files 2) so old cached copies are
  refetched. A cached row missing the fields is filled from its index entry.
- **Year.** A Topic Starter's own `year`, else its topic code's season, else
  the year it last changed. A personal file shows the year it was created.

## Known gaps

- File stats on hover use the browser's own tooltip (`title`), so they appear
  after the usual hover delay and not on touch screens. The numbers in an open
  session update on the next load, not as you type: a save recounts them in
  D1, but the row in memory keeps the old ones. Topic Starter rows imported
  before the `author` column have no author (no `.docx` is kept to read it
  from). Page counts are estimates from characters, not a real layout.

- The 100-row catalogue (`GET /api/topic-starters`) is not filtered by
  topic; the client filters it together with the file index. A topic whose
  rows all fall outside the first 100 still shows them from the index, but
  their content loads on open rather than with the catalogue.

- Undo history is per tab but per page load: it lives in the editor's memory,
  so a reload — or a tab being suspended (past five open tabs, or a sixth
  distinct document in another editor host) — starts a tab's history fresh.
- A suspended tab whose editor key was still the mounted one when it was
  suspended (not possible from the strip, which never suspends the active or
  most recent tab) is saved again on the next switch, until the 5-state cap
  drops it.

- A dragged split is remembered per-device, so it does not follow a reader to
  another machine — and it is keyed by panel set, so three sets of panels are
  three separate splits to arrange.
- `showsReasonDocsPanels` is a hard-coded route list, not something derived
  from the nav data. A new documents route has to be added to it by hand.
- `SharedCardOpener`'s `?share=<id>` flow replaces the URL with a bare
  `/reason-editor`, dropping any `?doc=`/`?topic=` alongside it. The two
  never travel together in practice (a share link comes from `/contacts`),
  but nothing enforces that.
- Conversion runs in the import request, so a 100-file ZIP converts 100
  documents inside one Worker invocation — heavier than the regex pass it
  replaces. A batch large enough to hit the CPU limit fails as a dead
  request, leaving the files converted before it in the library. Splitting
  the archive is the workaround; moving the conversion into the browser (as
  the Parquet card importer does) is the fix.
