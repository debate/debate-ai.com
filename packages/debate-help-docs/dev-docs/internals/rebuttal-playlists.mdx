# Rebuttal Playlists

Ten curated playlists of famous rounds; two students (or a student and the AI)
each redo one side's final rebuttal of the same video, with AI coach help and
an AI judge. Mounted at the top of the Practice Drills tab of Practice Debates
(`components/practice/PracticeDebates.tsx` in `debate-webview`,
`?section=drills`). User-facing page:
[Rebuttal Playlists](/docs/features/rounds#rebuttal-playlists).

## The playlists are static

Each list is the ten most-watched available rounds (one per stack) in the
`videos` table that match its filter — e.g. `style = 1 AND round_level =
'Finals'` for policy finals, `arg_2nr IN ('K', 'Setcol', …)` for the kritik
set — picked when the feature shipped. They are hard-coded rather than queried
because progress is stored per `video_id`: a list that reshuffled with view
counts would silently change what "7 of 10" means. Refresh them by hand at the
start of a season. `POST` refuses a video that isn't on the playlist it names.

## Drill lifecycle

``applyDrillMove(state, actorId, action, nowSeconds, text?)`` is what `PATCH`
runs and what `availableDrillMoves` (and so every button) is built from.

- `createDrill` — `waiting` (no partner) or, against the AI, `writing` with
  `partner_kind = 'ai'`.
- `POST { partner: "partner" }` first calls `joinWaitingDrill`: the oldest
  `waiting` row on the same playlist and video whose open side is the joiner's,
  started by someone else, not blocked either way, younger than 7 days.
  The claim is a compare-and-set on `updated_at`, so two joiners can't both
  land; the loser tries the next candidate, then starts their own waiting drill.
- Rebuttals are independent, so there are no turns: either side may `submit`
  once, even while waiting. When both texts are in the row moves to `judging`.
- `coach` spends one of 3 requests per side (counted on the row) and the route
  asks the model; a coach call no model answered returns 503 and isn't spent.
- `use_ai` (starter, while waiting) sets the AI as partner; `cancel` (starter,
  while waiting) closes it.
- `advanceDrill` writes the AI partner's rebuttal and runs the judge. `POST`
  and `PATCH` run it before replying; `GET` runs up to 2 pending jobs, so a
  model outage is retried on the next read.

A partner's text is only sent to the viewer once the drill is `complete`.

## Progress and XP

Progress is computed on the page from the viewer's drills
(`allPlaylistProgress`): a video is done on a side once the viewer's rebuttal
for it is in, in any non-cancelled drill. The rank ladder counts rebuttals per
playlist video and side (200 = everything, both sides). Debater Level XP uses
the existing `rebuttal_redo` activity on each submit and `practice_win` once
per judged win (drill ids already paid are kept in `localStorage` under
`rebuttalDrillPaidWins`).

## Model calls

Through `challengeTextGenerator` in `lib/debate-challenges/ai.ts`: the caller's
own key first, then the shared keys. Limits instead of the plan's AI quota: 15
new AI drills a day, 12 open drills, 3 coach requests per side per drill.

## Known gaps

- The video's constructives aren't fed to the prompts — only the title, teams,
  round and the library's recorded 2NR — so the coach and judge reason about
  what the round most likely came down to and are told to say when guessing.
- Pairing is asynchronous (first-come-first-served on the same video), not a
  live "online now" lobby; waiting drills stop being offered after 7 days.
- XP is per device through the Debater Level's own sync; a win can pay again on
  a second device.
- There is no leaderboard of playlist progress yet.
