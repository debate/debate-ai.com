# Admin Buyers

The **Buyers** section of `/admin` (`/admin#buyers`, admins only) lists every
account that has paid for something, one row per account:

- **Plan subscribers** — Debate AI Pro VIP and Research Team, from
  `stripe_subscriptions` (written by `/api/stripe/webhook`). An account's
  active subscription wins over older canceled ones; otherwise the newest one
  shows. A subscription whose checkout never linked to an account shows by
  its Stripe email, marked "not linked to an account".
- **Wallet top-ups** — the sum of `wallet_transactions` rows of kind `topup`.
  A seller's `sale` credits are not purchases and are left out.
- **Files bought** — count and total of `file_purchases`.

Above the table: buyer count, active subscribers with monthly recurring
revenue (each active subscription's plan price from `PLANS`), total top-ups
and files sold. The table filters by name/email and by
All / Subscribers / Wallet & files. Active subscribers sort first, then by
most recent purchase.

## How it is built

- **Query:** `loadBuyers` in `apps/debate-web/lib/admin/buyers.ts`; three
  grouped reads merged in memory, then one chunked `user` lookup for names.
  A payment table that doesn't exist yet (each is created by its feature on
  first use) reads as empty instead of failing the report.
- **API:** `GET /api/admin/buyers` (`getAdminAccess()`; moderators get 403).
- **UI:** `packages/debate-webview/src/components/admin/users/BuyersPanel.tsx`,
  mounted as the `buyers` tab in `AdminDashboard.tsx`.
- **Tests:** `apps/debate-web/lib/admin/__tests__/buyers.test.ts` (real
  in-memory SQLite) and the Buyers case in
  `packages/debate-webview/test/components/admin/admin-dashboard.test.tsx`.

## Known gaps

- Amounts come from the app's own tables, not Stripe: refunds, disputes and
  prorations made in the Stripe dashboard don't show here.
- No paging — the whole list loads at once, fine at today's buyer counts.
- Research publisher subscriptions (`research_publisher_subscriptions`) are not
  included yet.
