# Overview

Debate-ai is an all-in-one platform for competitive speech and debate, combining research, practice, judging, and tournament tools in one site. The FIAT debate interface offers format-specific flow spreadsheets for PF, LD, Policy, and NDT with inline editing, split view, shareable round links, speech documents, format-aware timers, an in-round recorder, and AI-generated judge decisions grounded in saved paradigms and flow summaries, with rounds syncing to your account for history and export. Practice vs AI lets you run full timed rounds—opening statements, cross-examination, and closings—against 13 opponent personas across difficulty levels, using either the current topic or a custom resolution, ending in an AI-judged scorecard, supported by a Prep Workspace (briefings, scout-to-strategy, opponent and judge profiles, drills) and a Coach Workspace (AI coach mode, coaching programs, team calendar, coach materials).

The research and learning side centers on a library of about 3,600 debate videos—roughly 2,700 recorded rounds and 900 lectures across college, Policy, Public Forum, and Lincoln-Douglas formats—with a persistent picture-in-picture player, plus a crowdsourced research card system with full-text search over millions of quotes, filtering by school, team, tournament, event, or year, AI analysis per card (summaries, logic flaws, warrant extensions), three reading modes, and source verification. The REASON research editor, built on the ProseMirror-based Debate Editor, provides structured outlines (pockets, hats, blocks, tags, cards, analytics), lossless .docx round-trip including encrypted and damaged files, real-time collaboration via share codes and invite links, AI tools (card cutter, citations, translation, ask-about-selection), a research chat beside your docs, and a command bar for searching cards, commands, settings, and files. Tournaments integration lets you search live Tabroom events by name, state, circuit, or event and follow invitations, pairings, and results, host your own tournament, read judge paradigms, and view team and school rankings dating back to 2015-16 with Elo ratings, TOC bids, aff/neg win rates, and a matchup simulator.

To take the platform anywhere, there is a Chrome extension for reading articles, PDFs, and YouTube transcripts in an on-page panel with offline editor use, native desktop and Android apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android, and an MCP server giving Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor access to cards, videos, and saved rounds. The site is built with Next.js and shadcn/ui on Cloudflare Workers, with an interactive API (Scalar docs generated from debate-openapi.yml) and an npm debate-api-client package. Additional features include a File Market, Prediction Markets, News & Forums, Rules & Glossary, quests and leaderboards, all listed on the site's features page, with documentation at debate-ai.com/docs and community support via Discord and GitHub Discussions.
